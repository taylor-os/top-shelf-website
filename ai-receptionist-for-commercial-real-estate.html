The inquiry about your listing does not wait for you to leave the site tour

A principal or a tenant rep who calls about a listing, an available suite, or an investment opportunity is not going to leave a voicemail and wait. They are working several brokers and several buildings at once, and if the call rolls to voicemail while you are out showing space or in the middle of a negotiation, the conversation simply happens with whoever picked up. Commercial inquiries do not carry the panic of a home emergency, but they carry real money and a short window of attention, and a serious one that goes unanswered is a mandate or a tenant that quietly lands with another broker.

An answering service built for commercial real estate answers on the first ring, finds out whether the caller is a tenant looking for space, a landlord who wants representation, or an investor asking about a specific listing, captures the property and the details, and books the follow-up on your calendar. The inquiry is captured and qualified instead of lost to voicemail while you are standing in an empty warehouse with no time to talk.

Built around how commercial brokers actually get inquiries

Your inquiries do not arrive on a schedule and they do not all come from the same place. A sign call on a listing, a message off a marketing flyer, a referral from a past client, an out-of-market investor phoning from three time zones over, a tenant rep circling a submarket for a client, they all land while you are at a tour, in the car, or across the table closing something else. A voicemail box cannot tell a qualified principal from someone kicking tires, and a generic call center reading a script does not know a tenant representation call from a listing inquiry or a cooperating broker.

  • Answers every inquiry live, including the after-hours and weekend calls from investors and out-of-market principals working on their own clock.
  • Qualifies the way you would: is this a tenant, a landlord, a buyer, or a broker, what property or submarket, roughly what size and timeline, and who has the authority to decide.
  • Captures the property of interest and the details, books the call or tour on your calendar, and routes anything time-sensitive to you at once.
  • Treats a past client, a referral, or a broker you already work with differently from a first-time caller, so the relationships you have built never hit a voicemail.

Qualify the opportunity before it ever reaches your calendar

Commercial leads vary more than almost any other business. One call is a funded buyer with a signed letter and a deadline, the next is a curious owner who will not transact for years, and your time is far too expensive to spend the same amount on both. The answering service asks the qualifying questions up front, what the caller needs, the rough size and use, the timeline, and whether they are the decision maker, so a real opportunity reaches you ready to move while the early tire-kicker is captured, logged, and set aside for follow-up rather than dropped. You walk into every call already knowing who you are talking to.

You own the number, the inquiries, and the relationships

This runs on your existing number, or a new one registered in your name, not ours. Every caller, every property they asked about, and every contact detail is yours and exportable any time, because in commercial real estate your relationships are the business and they should never be locked inside software you rent. The answering service is one piece of the Top Shelf platform, and it comes together with the CRM on the Signature plan, so a lead you catch today is still being nurtured when the deal is finally ready months from now. Nothing you capture is left to go cold.

How it plays out

An out-of-state investor, captured while you are at a tour

An investor two states away is scanning listings on a Saturday and calls about a property you have listed, ready to talk about a 1031 exchange with a clock running on it. You are across town walking a client through a space and cannot pick up. Instead of a voicemail the investor never leaves, your answering service takes the call, confirms what they are looking for and their timeline, captures their number and the property, and books a call with you for Monday morning while flagging it as time-sensitive. You start the week with a qualified buyer already on your calendar instead of hearing the opportunity went to another broker.
