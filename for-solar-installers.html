Where a solar company actually loses the sale

A solar company does not usually lose a sale on price, it loses it in the long gaps a considered purchase creates. A homeowner fills out three or four quote forms in one sitting, often late at night, and the company that reaches them while they are still at the screen books the consult, so a lead you paid real money to generate goes to whoever called back first. After the consult, the proposal sits for weeks while the homeowner compares bids and talks it over, and if nobody stays in touch it quietly drifts to the company that did. The homeowner who asked for a quote a year ago, whose situation may have changed, sits forgotten while you pay to generate a brand new one.

Top Shelf closes those gaps. An AI receptionist answers every quote call and web lead the moment it lands, day or night, qualifies the homeowner, and books the consultation or hands your closer a ready lead. A CRM keeps every open proposal in front of you and sends timed check-ins over the weeks the decision takes, and keeps a light touch on the not-ready leads so your name is the one still there when they move. And your website and local SEO earn a cautious homeowner's trust and get you found for solar installers near them, so more of the pipeline is yours instead of rented.

One system, not four disconnected tools

The reason those leads slip is that answering, follow-up, and marketing usually live in separate tools, and a team out running consults all day cannot keep them in sync. Top Shelf is one connected system. The consult the receptionist books lands in the same CRM that runs the weeks of follow-up and asks a happy customer for a review once the job is done. Your marketing keeps your Google profile active and your reviews growing, so a homeowner researching for weeks keeps finding a credible, established company instead of a thin, quiet profile, and it brings you leads that are yours alone rather than an inquiry a marketplace also sold to your competitors. You keep your own number and your customer list, and both leave with you if you ever go.
