Automation for Garage Door Companies

Automation for garage door companies runs the routine follow-up around every job for you, sending appointment reminders so techs are not driving to no-shows, chasing replacement quotes on a schedule, and asking for a review the moment a repair is done, so nothing depends on someone at the shop remembering to send a text between jobs.

The follow-up is real work nobody has time to do

Every job has a tail of small tasks after the truck leaves: confirm tomorrow's appointments, remind the customer, check back on the quote they were thinking over, ask the happy ones for a review. None of it is hard, and all of it slips during a busy week when the phone will not stop. Each one that slips is a wasted drive to an empty house, a replacement quote that quietly dies, or a five-star review you earned and never got.

Reminders that cut the no-shows

Before every scheduled visit, the customer gets an automatic confirmation and a reminder by text, with a simple way to confirm or reschedule. That is the single biggest lever on no-shows, and it keeps a tech from driving across your service area to a house where nobody is home. You stop losing a slot and the fuel to an appointment that was never going to happen, without anyone at the shop making reminder calls.

Quote follow-ups that chase themselves

A new door is a decision, and most homeowners take a few weeks and a couple of quotes to make it. Once you send a replacement quote, a scheduled sequence of light check-ins goes out for you until the customer decides, so the one who went quiet gets a nudge instead of being forgotten. The quote was rarely a no, it was a not yet, and this makes sure someone follows up at the right time. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a customer or a note.

Review requests and tune-up reminders on autopilot

The moment a job is marked done, a review request goes out automatically while the customer is happiest, so your rating keeps climbing without anyone remembering to ask. Further out, past customers get a light tune-up reminder when their door is due, worn springs and dry rollers are cheaper to service than to fix after they fail, which brings back work you would otherwise never hear about. It is a steady, low-key touch, not a stream of spam.

How it plays out

A job that follows up on itself

Your tech finishes a spring repair on a Tuesday and marks the job done in the app. That afternoon the customer gets a review request with a one-tap link. A homeowner who got a new-door quote the week before gets an automatic check-in asking if they have any questions. A customer from last fall gets a reminder that their door is due for a tune-up before winter. All three go out on time, and nobody at the shop sat down to send a single one of them.

Questions Garage Door Companies Ask

Common Questions

01

What does it actually send?

The routine follow-up around a job: appointment confirmations and reminders, replacement-quote check-ins, review requests when a job is done, and tune-up reminders to past customers when their door is due. You approve the messages and the timing up front.

02

Does this replace me personally following up with customers?

No. It handles the routine, predictable messages so nobody has to remember them. You still make every real call and step in personally for anything that needs a conversation, like a big replacement decision.

03

Can it really chase my replacement quotes for me?

Yes. Once a quote goes out, a scheduled set of check-ins goes to that customer until they decide, so the ones who went quiet keep hearing from you instead of quietly dropping off your list.

04

Are automatic review requests allowed?

Yes. Asking every customer for an honest review is fine and encouraged. The request simply goes out at the right moment with a one-tap link; it never filters out unhappy customers or pays for reviews.

05

How long does setup take?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We build the reminder, follow-up, and review sequences around how you already work, so it is running within days. Start with a free audit and we will find the gaps where jobs and quotes are falling through today.
