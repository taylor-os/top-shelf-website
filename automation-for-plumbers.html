Automation for Plumbers

Automation for plumbers handles the texts and reminders around every job for you, the on-my-way message to a customer waiting on a leak, the appointment reminder that prevents a wasted trip, the follow-up and review request after the work is done, so dispatch runs smooth and nothing depends on you remembering between service calls.

A customer waiting on water wants to know when you will get there

When someone has a leak or no water, the wait for the plumber is the worst part, and a customer left wondering whether anyone is actually coming is the one who calls another shop to be safe or leaves a frustrated review afterward. Keeping every customer updated, confirmed for tomorrow, on the way now, running twenty minutes behind, is exactly the kind of thing that slips when you are elbow-deep in a job and the schedule is moving. The work can be perfect and the customer still unhappy simply because nobody told them what was happening.

Automation handles those messages for you. A confirmation the day before, an on-my-way text with a realistic arrival window when the tech heads out, and a note if the schedule slips, all sent automatically, so the customer feels looked after and you are not the one stopping to type it.

Reminders that keep your schedule full and on time

A plumber driving to a no-show is an hour and a tank of gas you never get back, and an empty slot a real job could have filled. Automatic confirmations and reminders by text before every scheduled visit keep no-shows down and give a customer an easy way to reschedule instead of just not being home. For an owner running a couple of trucks, that is a fuller, tighter schedule without anyone working the phones to confirm each stop.

The follow-up after the job runs itself

The job is not really done when you drive away. A quick check that everything is holding, an automatic review request while the customer is still relieved, and a maintenance reminder scheduled for the right number of months out all matter, and all are easy to forget once you are onto the next call. Automation fires each one on time, so a finished job turns into a review, a repeat customer, and the next visit without you keeping a mental list.

Cold quotes and lapsed customers come back on schedule

A quote that went quiet and a customer you have not seen in two years are not dead, they are just waiting for a reason to call. A scheduled check-in on an open estimate, and a seasonal nudge to a customer who is overdue, bring that work back around automatically instead of falling out of your memory. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a customer or a note, and every automated touch is logged where you can see it.

How it plays out

A customer who never has to wonder if you are coming

A homeowner books a water heater replacement for Tuesday morning. The day before, they get an automatic confirmation. Tuesday morning, when your tech leaves the shop, they get an on-my-way text with an arrival window. That afternoon, once the job is done, they get a thank-you with a one-tap review link, and the system quietly schedules a reminder to check the unit in a year. You never sat down to send any of it, and the customer felt taken care of from booking to follow-up.

Illustrative example, not a client.

Common Questions

01

What messages does it actually send?

The routine ones around a job: an appointment confirmation, an on-my-way text with an arrival window when the tech heads out, a note if the schedule slips, and a follow-up with a review request after the work is done. You approve the wording so it all sounds like you.

02

Does this replace me or my dispatcher talking to customers?

No. It handles the predictable, repetitive texts so nobody has to stop and type them, while you and your team still make every real call and step in personally for anything that needs a conversation.

03

Can it help with no-shows and wasted trips?

Yes. Automatic confirmations and reminders before each visit keep no-shows down and give the customer an easy way to reschedule, so your techs spend less time driving to empty houses.

04

Will it send maintenance and follow-up reminders on its own?

Yes. After a job it can schedule a check that everything is holding and a maintenance reminder for the right number of months out, and it can nudge cold quotes and lapsed customers on a schedule you set, all logged against the customer in your CRM.

How long does setup take?
