## Ranked fixes (max 12)

Replace it with a real-looking photo of a silver 2016 Chevrolet Equinox: three-quarter front, plain lot or plain background, no dealer signage, no readable plate. Keep the "18 photos" badge. If no photo can be used, draw the same flat car silhouette the real kit uses, never a camera icon.

Confirm that the Signature plan's AI really answers inbound texts and books the appointment. If it only answers calls (and the website chatbot), change the scene to a call it answered:
