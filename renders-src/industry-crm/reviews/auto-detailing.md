Reviewed as Dee (owner), Paloma (front desk), Trey (detailer) and Erin/Whitney (customers), against `briefs/auto-detailing.md` and the real Detail Suite.

Overall: the words are right everywhere (work order, WO, Detailer, Front desk, Ready by, Waiting on the customer's OK, Check in / No-show, deposit kept). No car-lot or home-services words leaked in. The money agrees across screens: Whitney $220 + $40 - $55 = $205, Keisha $250 / $62.50, Erin $260 / $65, Gabriel $62.50, owed $250, in today $105 incl. $15 tip, 5 open WOs = 1 + 2 + 1 + 1.    - Front 3/4 of a black full-size SUV.
   - Driver side.
   - Rear bumper corner close-up with a small, visible scuff.
   - 2nd-row black seats.
   - 3rd row with light tan dog hair on the seat.
   - Cargo carpet close-up matted with tan dog hair.

   Screen 4 reuses the last three as its thumbnails. If photo-real isn't possible, drop the whole-vehicle drawings and use only tight close-ups (carpet, seat, bumper corner), which hide the drawing better. Keep all the captions as they are.
