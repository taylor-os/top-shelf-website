## Fixes, ranked

1. **MUST: Screens 4 and 2, primary button on a client still in service.** At 10:50 Tara Quinn is mid-foils, and at 10:40 Olivia still has toner and a blowout to go. Yet the filled green button is "Done", which ends the visit. A stylist would call that a mis-tap waiting to happen, and it looks like the app doesn't understand a color appointment. Keep the "Done" label (the kit's state step) but draw it as an outlined secondary button, the same weight as "Hand to". On screen 2, make "Checkout" the filled emerald button instead. On screen 4, leave no filled button on the Tara card; Eli Vance's "Start" stays the only call to action.
2. **MUST: Screen 3, table header.** "TEAM MEMBER" becomes "TEAM" (already decided).
3. **MUST: Screen 3, the strip at the bottom.** Its title "NEXT TO ARRIVE" contradicts Natalie Shore's "Checked in" chip. Change the title to "UP NEXT" and keep the three entries as they are.
4. **Nice: Screen 2, now-line collision.** The 10:40 now-line runs through Harper's "Processing" label. Move that label to the top of the striped band (just under 10:00), so it sits above Ryan Feld's card and clear of the line.
5. **Nice: Screen 1, payout row.** The "Payout ready: Dani Fox" row wraps to three ragged lines. Make line 2 "Nov 15 to Nov 28 · net owed $1,486.40" and drop gross and card fees from this row; the Pay page shows the full four numbers.
6. **Nice: Screen 6, Bree's last text.** Give the client the price of what she added: "Done! Brow wax with Sofia at 11:45 ($22), right after your color. See you tomorrow."
7. **Nice: Screen 2, visit panel repetition.** "LAST VISIT: Sep 25 with Harper · Balayage & toner" repeats the first row of "EARLIER VISITS". Delete the "LAST VISIT" block, and rename the formula heading "FORMULA (LAST VISIT, SEP 25)".
8. **Nice: Screen 1, legend contrast.** "Confirmed" and "Booked" are hard to tell apart at a glance in the legend and on the bars. Draw Booked blocks as a white fill with a colored outline, the way screen 2 draws Sasha Bloom and Joy Hale, so the 7 AM owner sees who hasn't confirmed.
