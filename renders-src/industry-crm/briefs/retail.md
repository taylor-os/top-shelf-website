# Retail & Local: screen brief

Shop type: an independent gift and home-goods boutique (the same kind of shop as "Maple & Main Mercantile" on the industry page: candles, cards, pottery, linens, small gifts). It does no repairs; its "service" is gift wrap and personalization (painted names on ornaments, engraved cutting boards). What runs this shop is not appointments. It runs on **the hold shelf, special orders, the want list, and regulars whose taste the owner knows by heart.** Every screen is built on those.

Sample date on every screen: **Tue, Dec 8, 2026** (holiday season, so a busy week that still looks under control). Shop hours: 10 AM to 6 PM, Thursdays in December until 8 PM.

## 2. Roles and what they need first

**Owner (Claire), at 7 AM before she opens**
1. What's going home today: holds and special orders being picked up, and any hold about to leave the shelf with no reply from the customer.
2. What came in that people asked for, and who to tell "it's in."
3. Calls and website chats from last night: what the AI answered, and the one or two that need her.
4. Money that came in by text: deposits, pay links paid, house accounts still open.
5. Regulars worth a personal text this week (a gift date from last year, a VIP who's been away).
- Trust: it remembers that Ellie hates floral candles and that Mark shops for her in December. It feels like the notebook she keeps under the counter.
- Dismiss: it looks like a register or an inventory system she already has, it asks her to count stock, or it shows "jobs" and "technicians."

**Shop lead (Hailey), at the counter while the phone rings**
1. Find the person at the counter by name or phone in two taps.
2. What they like, their wish list, and the gift dates coming up.
3. Whether they have something on hold or a special order on the way.
4. Put something on hold, or start a special order and take a deposit by text, in under 30 seconds.
5. Messages to answer between customers.
- Trust: one hand, big targets, the answer is on the first screen.
- Dismiss: a 12-field form while a line forms at the register.

**Associate (Cole), at the wrap table and on the delivery run**
1. The wrap and personalization queue, sorted by when it's due.
2. The exact spelling of names to paint or engrave, in the customer's own words.
3. Tonight's deliveries: address, gate code, who's receiving.
4. A "ready" text he can send in one tap.
5. Mark it done.
- Trust: the spelling came from the customer in writing.
- Dismiss: retyping an order someone already took at the counter.

**Holiday associate (Mia)**: find a customer, find their cubby, mark it picked up. Nothing else.

**Customer (Priya, Lena, Ruth's family)**
1. Is my thing in? Where do I pick it up, and how long will you hold it?
2. What do I still owe, and can I pay now so I just grab it?
3. Are you open today, and until when?
4. Can you wrap it or hold it a few more days?
5. A real person to text back.
- Trust: the text sounds like Claire, it's about the one thing they asked for, and they don't need an app.
- Dismiss: a promo blast, a coupon, or "download our app."

## 3. Words

| Bone | Larkhaven word |
|---|---|
| Customer | Customer. Regular for repeat buyers, a ★ VIP tag for the top few. Household links spouses. |
| Visit | Pickup (a customer coming for a hold) and Delivery (local drop after close). No appointments. |
| Deal | Special order (ordered from a vendor for a named customer, with a deposit). Stages: Asked, Ordered, It's in, On the shelf, Picked up. |
| Work item | Hold (an item set aside in a shelf slot) and Wrap & engrave (personalization or gift wrap ticket with name spelling and a proof photo). |
| Money | Deposit, Balance due at pickup, Pay link, Paid, House account (monthly statement). |
| Conversation | Messages (calls, texts, website chat in one thread per customer). |
| Automation | "It's in" text, Hold reminder, Gift-date reminder, Review request. |
| Team | Owner, Shop lead, Associate, Holiday associate. |
| Customer view | "Your order is in" page, Your orders with us, pay link. |
| Website link | Website chat answers questions after close and adds people to the want list. |

The want list is not a separate record. It's customers tagged with an item they asked for, grouped by item.

**Owner sidebar, in order:** Today · Hold shelf · Special orders · Want list · Customers · Messages · Pickups & delivery · House accounts · Reviews · Settings (Team lives in Settings).

**Shop lead phone tabs:** Customers · Hold shelf · Messages · More (More: Want list, Special orders, Pickups & delivery, Reviews). No House accounts, no Team, no Settings.

**Associate phone tabs:** Wrap & engrave · Hold shelf · Deliveries · Messages.

**Holiday associate phone tabs:** Customers · Hold shelf. Can mark picked up; can't change money or send pay links.

**Words that must NOT appear** (they belong to other industries or to tools we don't sell): job, work order, dispatch, technician, tech, crew, truck, route, arrival window, service call, estimate, quote, invoice (in nav; "statement" is fine for house accounts), payroll, cash, cash drawer, register, POS, checkout, cart, shop online, shipping, SKU, inventory, stock count, in stock, out of stock, reorder point, units, lead, pipeline, deal, appointment, booking, client, patient, ticket number, #ORD-, "no-charge orders", "sent out, not billed", campaign, promo, coupon, loyalty points, rewards, blast.

## 6. Watch-outs

- **Not a register, not an inventory system, not a web store.** No "today's sales," no cart, no checkout, no stock counts, no "3 in stock." Money shown is only what moves through Top Shelf: deposits, pay links, house-account statements.
- **The AI receptionist never claims stock.** It says it'll have someone check and text back. That's honest, and it's what makes screen 6 believable.
- **"Tell the 4 who asked" is not a campaign.** It's one personal text per person who asked for that exact item, each with their name. No lists of everyone, no promos, coupons, loyalty points, or "shop our sale."
- **Wish lists are shared only when the customer said so.** Always show the "shared with Mark" label.
- **Keep it calm:** one hold leaving with no reply, one overnight call that needs Claire. Holiday-busy, not broken.
- **Tax:** Texas 8.25%, written as "incl. tax" ($42.00 becomes $45.47).
- **No real brands or shops:** describe products ("cedar & sage candle, 3-wick"), never a real candle or pottery brand, and no real Grapevine Main Street shop names. All numbers are 555.
- **Drop from the rejected draft:** the time-ordered "orders" timeline, staff chips on every row, Payroll, Cash, Quotes, Invoices nav, "Sent out, not billed," "No-charge orders," "#ORD-1048," "Out for delivery" as a headline status.
- **No real kit exists yet,** so nothing to match beyond the shared emerald visual kit. Customer-facing screen 5 uses the shop's own cream and terracotta look.
- **Optional:** the industry page tells the story of "Maple & Main Mercantile" and its owner Dana. If Taylor wants the screens to match that story, swap the shop name and owner. Nothing else changes.
