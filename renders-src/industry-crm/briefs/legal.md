# Legal: screen brief (slug `legal`)

Sample date across all 6 screens: **Monday, Nov 30, 2026** (the first business morning after a holiday weekend, so the overnight and weekend intake is the story). Every image keeps the bottom strip: "Sample screens · sample data, not a real business".

What the rejected emerald draft got wrong (do not repeat): a home-services "Today's jobs" list with the words swapped, tiles like "Meetings done 2 of 8", "No-charge consults 0", "Work done, not billed", nav items Payroll / Cash / Mail / Engagements, and a client page that reads like a customer account. ## 2. Roles and what they need first

**Owner / managing attorney (Grace)**, checks at 7 AM before court:
1. Who called while we were closed, and did every one of them get answered and booked.
2. Today: court settings, consults, client calls, in time order, with who is covering each.
3. Conflict checks waiting on her to clear before a consult happens.
4. Money: retainers and flat fees paid this month, pay links still out.
5. Prospects who consulted but have not retained, and this week's deadlines.
- Trust: she sees Saturday's 11 PM call already answered, booked and on Daniel's calendar without anyone touching it.
- Dismiss: anything that says "jobs", "techs" or "work done, not billed"; anything that shows case facts; anything that claims to calculate court deadlines for her.

**Intake coordinator (Tessa)**, works with the phone ringing:
1. New inquiries in order, with how long each has waited for a human.
2. Conflict check status on each (not started / pending / cleared).
3. Open consult slots by attorney.
4. One click to book the consult and send the consult-fee or retainer pay link by text.
5. Today's follow-ups with prospects who did not retain.
- Trust: the AI call summary is already filled in; she never retypes a name or number.
- Dismiss: if booking a consult takes her away from the inquiry she is looking at.

**Paralegal (Priya)** (appears as assignee across the set):
1. Deadlines this week on her matters.
2. Court dates and mediations, with client reminder texts set.
3. Tasks assigned to her, with due dates.
4. Client messages waiting for a reply.
5. Which clients have an installment due (so she can nudge kindly).
- Trust: every date has an automatic client reminder.
- Dismiss: if it pretends to be document management or a court-rules docketing system.

**Client (Jordan, Dana)**, on the phone:
1. When and where is my next date.
2. What I owe, and pay it from the text.
3. Who my attorney and paralegal are, and how to reach them.
4. A receipt that the payment went through.
5. A reminder before the date.
- Trust: a clear fee with a balance that adds up.
- Dismiss: legalese, or anything about the case showing up in a text preview.

**Prospective client (Renee)**, at 11 PM: a calm answer in the firm's name, a booked time, who will call and from what number, what the consult costs, and no legal advice or promises from a machine.

## 6. Watch-outs

- **No case facts, anywhere.** Practice area and matter type only. No charges, injuries, children's names, assets, or "what happened". The AI summary says "calling for a family member", never why.
- **AI never gives legal advice or predicts outcomes.** Its first line says so on screen 6. Keep that line.
- **Texts are lock-screen safe.** Reminders and pay links name the firm, the date and the amount only, never the matter.
- **Deadlines are entered by people.** No "auto-calculated", "court rules" or "docketing" language. The drawer footer line on screen 2 says this; keep it.
- **No trust accounting.** Show retainers and installments as ordinary invoices with Paid / Due. Never "trust balance", "IOLTA", "operating account", "transfer from trust".
- **No document management or e-signature.** No Documents tab, no upload, no "engagement signed". Stage "Retained" means the first payment came in.
- **No time billing.** No hours, timers or rates. Fees are flat fees, retainers and payment plans.
- **Tone:** calm and discreet. Amber for "pending", never red alerts. Numbers small and believable (7 inquiries, 2 conflict checks, 3 follow-ups), nothing overdue.
- **Consult fee:** shown for family law ($150) only; criminal defense and estate planning consults show no fee line. Keeps it believable without implying a rule.
- **Referrals:** the AI sending an out-of-practice caller a referral list (Gina Lockhart, Immigration) is fine; do not name the firms referred to.
- **No real kit exists for legal yet,** so there is nothing to match beyond the shared emerald kit from the homepage hero (`hero-dash-d.jpg`): keep its sidebar, header band, tile, chip, card and phone-icon styles exactly, and change only nav, layout, words and data as written here.
