The call you miss after a storm is the roof your competitor signs

Roofing runs on speed, and never more than in the days after a hail or wind event. A homeowner with a damaged roof does not call one roofer, they call three, and they hire whoever picks up and gets someone out to look. But your crews are already on roofs, your estimators are already booked, and the calls stack up faster than anyone can answer them. Every one that rolls to voicemail is a homeowner who has already dialed the next company on the list.

An AI receptionist closes that gap. It answers on the first ring, day or night, sounds calm and professional, finds out whether the caller has storm damage, an active leak, or an aging roof, then books a free inspection on your calendar and texts you the details before your estimator is off the current job. The lead is captured and scheduled instead of lost to whoever answered faster.

Built around how roofing actually books work

Your phones do not ring at a steady pace. They are quiet for weeks, then a storm rolls through and you get a season of calls in two days, most of them after hours, most of them from people who will hire the first roofer to show up. A voicemail box cannot triage that, and a generic call center does not know an insurance claim from a cash repair.

  • Answers 24/7, including the midnight-after-the-storm call and the Sunday inquiry off your yard sign.
  • Handles a surge, so a rush of calls all get answered at once instead of stacking up in voicemail.
  • Asks the questions you would ask: storm damage or wear, is it actively leaking, insurance claim or paying out of pocket, roof age, and the address.
  • Books the free inspection on your real calendar and sends you the lead by text on the spot.

The math is one signed roof

You do not need to recover many calls for this to pay for itself. A roof replacement is a big-ticket job, so one homeowner you would have lost to voicemail after a storm, or one inspection you would have missed on a weekend, is worth far more than the whole system costs for a long time. Everything it catches after that is margin. The point is to stop handing a storm's worth of leads to the roofer who happened to answer while you were on a roof.

You own the number, the leads, and the list

This runs on your existing phone number, or a new one registered in your name, not ours. Every caller, every lead, and every detail is yours and exportable any time, with no long contract and no holding your data hostage. The AI receptionist is one piece of the Top Shelf platform, and it plugs into the same CRM that follows up on every estimate, so a lead it captures at 11 p.m. after a storm still gets worked instead of going cold.

How it plays out

A hail storm Saturday, while your crews are already on roofs

Hail moves through on a Saturday afternoon and your phone starts ringing before the sky clears. Your two crews are already on roofs and your estimator is mid-inspection, so the calls would normally pile up in voicemail until Monday. Instead the AI receptionist answers every one, confirms who has active leaks and visible damage, and books free inspections across your open slots for the coming week. By the time your estimator checks the phone, there is a full week of inspections booked, each with a name, an address, and whether the homeowner plans to file a claim. You did not miss the surge, and none of those roofs went to the company down the road.

Questions Roofers Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it work with my current phone number?

Yes. It can answer on your existing number, or set up a new one registered in your name. Either way the number and every lead that comes through it belong to you and go with you if you ever leave.

02

Can it handle a flood of calls after a storm?

That is exactly what it is for. It answers every call at once, so a storm that would send a dozen homeowners to voicemail instead gets a dozen inspections booked. It does not get overwhelmed, put people on hold, or clock out at 5 p.m.

03

Can it tell an insurance claim from a cash repair?

Yes. It asks whether the homeowner has storm damage, whether the roof is actively leaking, and whether they plan to file a claim or pay out of pocket, so an adjuster-driven job and a quick repair are flagged differently before your estimator ever calls back.

04

Will it actually book the free inspection?

Yes. It books straight onto your calendar based on your availability, sends you the details by text right away, and can send the homeowner a confirmation. You set the rules, like how many inspections a day each estimator can take and how much drive time to leave between them.

05

Is this going to sound like a robot to my customers?

It answers naturally and professionally, and it is upfront rather than pretending to be a person. A homeowner with water coming through the ceiling cares about one thing, that someone picked up and is sending help, which beats a voicemail box every time. You can hear how it handles a live call before you decide.
