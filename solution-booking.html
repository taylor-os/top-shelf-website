How Online Booking Works for You

Online Booking

Customers pick an open time and book themselves, any hour of the day.

Why it matters: Half your day is spent playing phone tag over appointment times, and the customer who wanted to book at 9pm just gave up because nobody was there to answer. Every booking that has to wait for business hours is a booking you can lose to a competitor who lets people schedule right now.

40%Roughly 40% of online bookings are made after business hours, appointments you'd never capture if a phone call were the only way in. SchedulingKit
You get: You capture jobs around the clock and get hours of your week back from phone tag and back-and-forth.

The Dispatch Board

Every booked job lands on one board that shows who’s going where and when. Move a job to another tech or another time without a round of phone calls.

Why it matters: When the schedule lives in a notebook, a group text and somebody’s memory, jobs get double-booked and crews sit idle. One board everyone can see ends the guessing.

You get: a day you can see at a glance, from the first booking to the last job.

On-My-Way Texts

When your tech heads to the job, the customer gets a text saying you’re on the way.

Why it matters: A customer who has waited at home all morning with no word starts calling the office, or gives up and leaves. A simple heads-up keeps them home and keeps them happy.

You get: fewer “where are you?” calls and customers who are ready when you pull up.

Common Questions

What Owners Ask About Booking

01

Can customers really book when my office is closed?

Yes. Your booking page is open around the clock, so someone can pick an open slot at 11pm without waiting for you to answer the phone. A large share of online bookings happen after business hours, and those are the appointments you would otherwise never capture. The calendar fills while you sleep.

02

Will my customer know when we’re coming?

Yes. When your tech heads out, the customer gets an on-my-way text. They aren’t stuck waiting at home all morning with no word, and your office gets fewer “where are you?” calls.

03

How do booked jobs get to my crew?

Every booking lands on the dispatch board, which shows who’s going where and when. You can move a job to another tech or another time from the board instead of making a round of phone calls.

04

A customer called while I was on a job. Now what?

The AI receptionist in the same plan answers and books the job. If a call still gets missed, the caller gets a text back right away, so you don’t lose them because you were up a ladder at 2pm. The first business to respond usually wins the job.

05

Does the booking calendar connect to the rest of my system?

Yes. Booking is part of the Top Shelf CRM, which also runs your customer list, follow-up reminders, invoicing and payments. A new appointment goes straight into the same system instead of living in a separate app. Your calendar and your customer list stay yours.
