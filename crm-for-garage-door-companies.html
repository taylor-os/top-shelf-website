The quotes you already gave are the jobs you are losing

Most garage door companies do not have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. You go out, look at a sagging or dented door, and hand over a quote for a full replacement. The homeowner says they need to think about it, and they mean it, because a new door is a real decision they usually take a few weeks and two or three quotes to make. Then nobody follows up, and the job goes to whichever company checked back in.

A CRM keeps every quote on a follow-up schedule you set, with texts and emails that go out on time whether or not anyone at the shop remembers. The homeowner comparing three companies still hears from you and books with the one that stayed in touch instead of the one they talked to last.

Your past customers are the cheapest jobs you will ever get

Springs wear out, openers age, rollers and cables give up, and the door you serviced three years ago is due again. A past customer already trusts you and costs nothing to win back, but nobody can remember to reach out to every home you have worked on, so most of that repeat business sits unused and slowly forgets who fixed the door last time.

  • Every customer, job, note, and quote lives in one place instead of a truck notebook and three apps.
  • Tune-up reminders and check-ins go out on schedule, so past customers hear from you without anyone setting a reminder.
  • You can see which quotes are still open and which customers are due, so your time goes to the jobs closest to booking.

Answer first, without living on the phone

A new lead that hears back in the first few minutes is far more likely to book than one that waits an hour, and in a same-day trade the first company to respond usually wins the job. The CRM replies instantly with a text-back the moment a lead comes in and hands it to you with the details, so you are first without watching your inbox from the top of a ladder.

You own the customer list, not a lead app

Every customer and every note is yours and exportable any time. Switch software, change how you run the office, and your list comes with you instead of staying locked inside someone else's system. The CRM is one piece of the Top Shelf platform and plugs into the AI receptionist, so a call it answers lands in your list and gets followed up on automatically, and into online booking, so an appointment is logged against the right customer.

How it plays out

The replacement quote you almost forgot turns into a job

A homeowner asks for a quote on a new double door after a panel gets crushed backing out. You give them the number, they want to think it over and get one more quote, and you would normally lose track of them within a week. Instead the CRM keeps a light follow-up going for you: a quick check-in, an answer to a warranty question, a note that install slots are filling up. Three weeks later they book the door, and they book it with you, because yours is the company that stayed in touch. You did not chase them once by hand.
