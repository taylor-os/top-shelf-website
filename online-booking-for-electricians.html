Online Booking for Electricians

Online booking for electricians lets a homeowner pick a time for non-urgent work, an EV charger install, a panel inspection, adding a circuit, straight from your website or a text link, on your real availability, so you stop playing phone tag and the job lands on your schedule with the details you need to show up ready.

Phone tag is quietly costing you the easy jobs

A homeowner wants an EV charger installed or a panel inspected before they sell. It is not an emergency, so they are happy to schedule, but you are on a job, you call back that evening, they miss it, you trade voicemails, and two days later they booked the electrician who could lock in a time on the spot. The job was never the problem. The back and forth was. For planned work, the worst thing you can do is make someone wait for a callback.

A booking link lets them grab an open slot the moment they think of it, day or night, without reaching you first. You come off a job to find the install already on your schedule instead of a missed call you now have to chase.

It sorts the routine work from the emergencies

Self-scheduling is for the work that can wait: installs, inspections, adding a circuit or a ceiling fan, quotes for planned upgrades. The booking form asks what the job is and offers only those appointment types, so a homeowner books an EV charger install for next week while a true no-power emergency is still told to call you. You keep control: how much notice you need, how long each visit runs, buffer time to drive between jobs, and which hours are open at all.

A booking link everywhere a customer finds you

The link goes anywhere a customer already meets you: your website, your Google Business Profile, your email signature, and the text you send after a first call. Every one of those turns from a dead end that needs a callback into a place someone can book you in one tap. A homeowner comparing two electricians for a generator install on a Sunday night can put the visit on your calendar before they close the tab.

On your real calendar, and the job shows up

It syncs to the calendar you already use so it can never double-book you against a job you already have, and it sends the customer a confirmation and a reminder, which is what actually cuts down no-shows on scheduled work. Every booking is logged against the right customer in your CRM, and it works alongside the AI receptionist, which can book a caller who would rather talk than tap. The leads and the calendar stay yours.

How it plays out

A Sunday-night EV charger install books while you sleep

It is 8 p.m. Sunday and a homeowner just bought an electric car and wants a charger installed. It is not urgent, so instead of leaving a voicemail they will have to trade calls over, they land on your site, see your reviews, and book a charger install for Thursday morning from the calendar right there on the page. You wake up Monday to the job already scheduled, with the address, the panel details you asked for, and the customer confirmed. No phone tag, no lost job.

Questions Electricians Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it sync to my Google or Outlook calendar?

Yes. It reads your real calendar so it only ever offers times you are actually free, and new bookings drop straight onto it. It cannot double-book you against a job you already have scheduled.

02

Will homeowners try to book real emergencies through it?

You control which appointment types it offers, so it is set up for planned work like installs, inspections, and quotes. Someone with a true no-power or safety emergency is pointed to call you directly, where the AI receptionist can triage it.

03

Can it respect drive time and stop back-to-back bookings?

Yes. You set how much notice you need, how long each visit runs, and how much buffer to leave between jobs, so you are never booked with no time to get across town.

04

Will it cut down on no-shows?

It helps. Automatic confirmations and reminders by text and email are the single biggest lever on no-shows, and every booking includes them. Someone who booked themselves and got a reminder is far more likely to be there than a loosely agreed callback.

05

How fast can it be live?

Setup is included, no separate fee. We connect your calendar, set up your appointment types and rules, and put the link on your site and Google profile, so it is taking bookings in days. Start with a free audit and we will show you where you are losing planned jobs today.
