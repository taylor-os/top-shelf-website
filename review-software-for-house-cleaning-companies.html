Review Software for House Cleaning Companies

Review software for house cleaning companies asks every happy client for a review right after a clean, makes leaving one a single tap, and helps you reply to each, so the next homeowner deciding who to trust with a key to their home finds a wall of recent five-star reviews and calls you first.

Nobody hands a house key to the cleaner with three old reviews

Choosing a cleaning company is an unusually personal decision. You are letting strangers into your home, often when you are not there, sometimes with a key, an alarm code, or a garage code. So a homeowner comparing cleaners does not just glance at the star rating, they read the recent reviews to decide whether they trust these people in their house at all. A company with a handful of old reviews quietly loses those clients to the one with a fresh, steady wall of them, and you never hear the phone that did not ring.

The problem is almost never that your clients are unhappy. It is that a thrilled client walks into a spotless home, feels great for an hour, and never thinks to post about it, while asking in person feels awkward. Review software fixes the timing and the awkwardness, so the reputation your crews have already earned actually shows up where new clients are deciding.

Ask at the moment they walk into a clean home

The best time to ask is right after a clean, when the client walks in to fresh floors and the smell of a house that is finally spotless and is happiest with the work. The software sends the ask automatically at that moment, by text and email, with a link that drops them straight onto your Google profile in one tap. No cornering anyone in their doorway, no card that asks them to go find you later, and for recurring clients it paces the ask so a weekly customer is invited once when they are new and again now and then, never nagged after every single visit.

Respond to every review, good or bad

A thoughtful reply to each review tells the next homeowner reading it that you stand behind your crews, and responding is one of the signals that helps you show up higher in local search. The software flags every new review the moment it lands and helps you reply fast, so a rare critical one, the day a crew missed a room or ran late, gets a calm, professional response instead of silence, and every good one gets a genuine thank you.

More reviews, more clients, compounding

Recent reviews feed your Google Business Profile, which lifts you in the map pack a homeowner sees when they search for a cleaner near them, which brings more clients, which brings more reviews. It builds on itself. Your reputation is yours, tied to your own profile, not a directory or a lead-seller. Review requests are part of the Top Shelf platform and fire automatically off a clean marked complete in your CRM, so you collect reviews without lifting a finger.

How it plays out

One deep clean becomes next week's new client

Your crew finishes a first deep clean on a Thursday afternoon and leaves the house spotless. An hour later, the software texts your delighted client a thank-you with a one-tap link, and they post a warm review that mentions how careful and trustworthy the crew was, before dinner. The next week a neighbor down the street decides to hire a cleaner, searches, and reads that exact review sitting at the top of your profile. They call you instead of the company below you on the map, because yours is the one someone they almost know just trusted in their home. You never asked twice.

Questions House Cleaning Companies Ask

Common Questions

01

Which review sites does it work with?

It focuses on Google, which is where a homeowner choosing a cleaner looks first and what feeds your local search ranking. It can also point past clients to your other profiles. We follow each platform's own rules, including that a review should come from someone you actually cleaned for.

02

When does it ask for the review?

Right after a clean, when the client is happiest with the work, sent automatically by text and email with a one-tap link. For recurring clients it paces the ask so they are invited when they are new and occasionally after that, never after every visit.

03

Is automating review requests allowed?

Asking every client for an honest review is fine and encouraged. What is not allowed is filtering out unhappy clients or paying for reviews, and this does not do that. It simply asks everyone at the right moment and makes saying yes easy.

04

What happens if I get a bad review?

You find out immediately instead of weeks later, and the software helps you post a calm, professional reply. One measured response to a critical review, sitting under a wall of genuine positive ones, often reassures the next reader more than a spotless record would.

05

How long until it is running?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We connect your Google profile, set the timing and the message, and tie it to your completed cleans, so requests start going out in days. Start with a free audit and we will show you how your reputation stacks up against the cleaners you compete with.
