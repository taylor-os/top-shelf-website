How Reviews & Reputation Works for You

Review Generation & Reputation Management

A steady, automatic system for asking happy customers for reviews and managing your ratings across the web.

Why it matters: You do great work, but your competitor with 200 reviews shows up first and looks more trusted, while your handful of old ratings makes new customers hesitate and scroll past. Your star rating is the first thing a stranger judges you on, and even a fraction of a star swings real revenue.

5-9%A one-star rise in online rating drives a 5% to 9% increase in revenue for independent local businesses. Harvard Business School (Luca)
You get: You build a wall of recent five-star proof that wins the customer's trust before you ever pick up the phone.

Video Testimonial Collection

A simple, guided way to capture real customers telling their story on video, then put those clips to work for you.

Why it matters: Your best customers rave about you in person, but that gold vanishes the second they drive off, while a stranger deciding whether to trust you has nothing to go on but a name. People trust a real customer's face and voice far more than any claim you make about yourself, and that trust is what closes the sale.

79%79% of people have watched a video testimonial to learn about a business, and 77% say it helped convince them to buy. Wyzowl
You get: You hand every hesitant prospect living proof that you're the safe choice, doing the convincing for you before you ever talk.

Review Monitoring & Alerts

Every rating across Google, Facebook, and the rest pulled into one place, with an alert the moment a new one lands.

Why it matters: A one-star review can sit at the top of your Google listing for a week before you even know it's there, quietly turning away every new customer who reads it. What you can't see, you can't answer, and silence starts to read as guilt.

You get: You hear about every review the moment it posts, so nothing festers right where your next customer is looking.

Done-for-You Review Responses

A prompt, professional reply to every review, good or bad, written to sound like you.

Why it matters: A thoughtful reply to a hard review often matters more than the review itself, because the next customer is really watching how you handle it. An ignored review, or a defensive one, tells them everything they think they need to know.

You get: Every reviewer feels heard and every reader sees a business that cares, without you drafting a single reply.

Reviews on Your Site & Google

Your best reviews pulled straight onto your website and kept fresh on your Google profile.

Why it matters: The five-star proof you worked so hard for is scattered across a dozen platforms a customer will never think to check. Trust that lives somewhere nobody looks does none of the selling it could be doing.

You get: Your strongest proof sits right where people decide, doing the convincing before they ever reach out.

Common Questions

Your Reputation, Answered

01

How do you actually get customers to leave reviews?

We put a steady, automatic system behind the ask, so every happy customer gets invited to leave a review at the moment they are most pleased, right after the work is done. You are not the one remembering to ask, and you are not asking at an awkward time. That is how a handful of old ratings becomes a steady stream of recent five star proof. The good work you already do turns into the reviews that win the next customer.

02

What happens when I get a bad review?

You hear about it the moment it posts instead of stumbling on it a week later, and you get a prompt, professional reply written to sound like you. The next customer is really judging how you handle a hard review, so a calm and thoughtful response often matters more than the review itself. We also route happy customers toward a public rating and give unhappy ones a private way to reach you first, so problems get solved before they land in public. Nothing festers right where your next customer is looking.

03

Do you write fake reviews or hide the bad ones?

No. Every review comes from a real customer who actually used you, because fake proof falls apart the moment someone senses it. What we do is make sure the happy customers, who usually stay quiet, actually get asked, and we give an unhappy customer a private way to reach you first so you can make it right. You still get honest ratings, just more of the good ones you earned and were never collecting. Real proof is the only kind that sells.

04

Where do these reviews show up?

We pull your best reviews straight onto your website and keep your Google profile fresh, so your proof sits right where people decide instead of scattered across platforms nobody thinks to check. We also help you gather video testimonials, because people trust a real customer's face and voice more than anything you say about yourself. Your star rating is often the first thing a stranger judges you on, so we put it front and center. Trust that lives where customers look is trust that does the selling.

05

Do I own my reviews, and how do I start?

Your reviews and your review profiles are in your name from day one, and you keep all of it if you ever leave. We build the reputation on your accounts, not ours, so the proof you earn always belongs to you. Start with the free audit, where we show you where your reputation stands today and find the gap between that and the businesses winning the click. You get real numbers whether you work with us or not.
