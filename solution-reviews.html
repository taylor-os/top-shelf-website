How Reviews Work for You

Review Requests After Every Job

When the job is done, the customer gets a text asking for a review, while they’re still happy with the work. Nobody has to remember to ask.

Why it matters: You do great work, but your competitor with 200 reviews shows up first and looks more trusted, while your handful of old ratings makes new customers hesitate and scroll past. Your star rating is the first thing a stranger judges you on, and even a fraction of a star swings real revenue.

5-9%A one-star rise in online rating drives a 5% to 9% increase in revenue for independent local businesses. Harvard Business School (Luca)
You get: a steady run of recent reviews that earn a stranger’s trust before they ever call.

Review Replies

Every review gets a prompt, professional reply, good or bad, that sounds like you.

Why it matters: A thoughtful reply to a hard review often matters more than the review itself, because the next customer is really watching how you handle it. An ignored review, or a defensive one, tells them everything they think they need to know.

You get: every reviewer feels heard, and the next customer sees a business that pays attention.

Common Questions

Your Reputation, Answered

01

How do you actually get customers to leave reviews?

The CRM sends a review request after the job, when the customer is most pleased. You don’t have to remember to ask, and you’re not asking at an awkward time. That’s how a handful of old ratings turns into a steady run of recent ones.

02

What happens when I get a bad review?

It gets a prompt, professional reply that sounds like you. The next customer is really judging how you handle a hard review, so a calm answer often matters more than the review itself.

03

Do you write fake reviews or hide the bad ones?

No. Every review comes from a real customer who actually used you, because fake proof falls apart the moment someone senses it. The CRM just makes sure your happy customers, who usually stay quiet, actually get asked. You still get honest ratings, just more of the good ones you earned.

04

Is this a separate tool I have to log in to?

No. Review requests and review replies are part of the Signature plan, in the same CRM where you keep your customers, book jobs and send invoices. The request goes out from the job you already closed.

05

Do I own my reviews, and how do I start?

Your reviews and your review profiles stay in your name, and you keep all of it if you ever leave. To start, tap Try the CRM and fill out a short form. We call you, then send you a demo.
