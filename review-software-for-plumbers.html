Review Software for Plumbers

Review software for plumbers asks every happy customer for a review the moment the job is done and the water is running again, makes leaving one a single tap, and helps you reply to each, so the next homeowner searching in a panic finds a wall of recent five-star reviews and calls you first.

A panicked homeowner hires the plumber with the most recent reviews

When a pipe bursts, nobody asks around for a referral. They search for a plumber, glance at the map, and call the one with the most recent five-star reviews near them, because they are about to let a stranger into their home during a stressful moment and reviews are the fastest trust they can get. A thin profile with a handful of old reviews quietly loses those calls to the shop with a fresh wall of them, and you never even hear the phone that did not ring.

The problem is not that your customers are unhappy. It is that happy customers forget to post, and asking feels awkward when you are packing up the truck. Review software fixes the timing and the awkwardness so the reputation you have already earned actually shows up where people are looking.

Ask at the moment they are most relieved

The best time to ask is right after the water is running again and the customer is standing there grateful that the crisis is over. The software sends the ask automatically at that moment, by text and email, with a link that drops them straight onto your Google profile in one tap. No cornering people at the door, no business card that asks them to go find you later, and no reminder three weeks on when the relief has worn off and they have moved on.

Respond to every review, good or bad

A thoughtful reply to each review tells the next homeowner reading it that you stand behind your work, and responding is one of the signals that helps you show up higher in local search. The software flags every new review the moment it lands and helps you reply fast, so a rare critical one, the callback that went sideways, gets a calm, professional response instead of silence, and every good one gets a genuine thank you.

More reviews, more calls, compounding

Recent reviews feed your Google Business Profile, which lifts you in the map pack a homeowner sees when they search for a plumber near them, which brings more calls, which brings more reviews. It builds on itself. Your reputation is yours, tied to your own profile, not a directory or a lead-seller. Review requests are part of the Top Shelf platform and fire automatically off a job marked complete in your CRM, so you collect reviews without lifting a finger.

How it plays out

One drain cleaning becomes next week's emergency call

You clear a stubborn kitchen drain on a Thursday afternoon. An hour after you leave, the software texts your relieved customer a thank-you with a one-tap link, and they post a warm review before dinner. The next week a neighbor two streets over has a water heater start leaking, searches for a plumber, and reads that exact review sitting at the top of your profile. They call you instead of the name below you on the map. You never asked twice, and you were on another job when it happened.

Questions Plumbers Ask

Common Questions

01

Which review sites does it work with?

It focuses on Google, which is where a homeowner in a hurry looks first and what feeds your local search ranking. It can also point past customers to your other profiles. We follow each platform's own rules, including that a review should come from someone you actually did work for.

02

When does it ask for the review?

Right after the job is done and the customer is relieved, sent automatically by text and email with a one-tap link. You can adjust the timing and the wording so it sounds like you and not a form letter.

03

Is automating review requests allowed?

Asking every customer for an honest review is fine and encouraged. What is not allowed is filtering out unhappy customers or paying for reviews, and this does not do that. It simply asks everyone at the right moment and makes saying yes easy.

04

What happens if I get a bad review?

You find out immediately instead of weeks later, and the software helps you post a calm, professional reply. One measured response to a critical review, sitting under a wall of genuine positive ones, often reassures the next reader more than a spotless record would.

05

How long until it is running?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We connect your Google profile, set the timing and the message, and tie it to your completed jobs, so requests start going out in days. Start with a free audit and we will show you how your reputation stacks up against the plumbers you compete with.
