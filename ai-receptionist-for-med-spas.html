The consult you miss books with the spa that picked up

A woman who has been thinking about Botox or filler for months finally works up the nerve to call. If she gets voicemail, she does not leave one, she calls the next spa on the list, and the one after that, until someone picks up. Aesthetic work runs on trust and timing: the treatments are high-ticket and consultation-driven, and a first-time caller is nervous and comparison-shopping. Meanwhile your front desk is walking a client back to a room and your injector is mid-treatment with both hands busy, so the call rolls to voicemail and the lead, worth far more than one visit once she becomes a regular, goes to whoever answered.

An AI receptionist answers on the first ring, warm and calm with someone who is a little anxious, finds out what she is interested in, a tox appointment, a first-time filler consult, laser, a facial, answers the questions that decide whether she books, and puts the consultation on your calendar right then or texts her a booking link. The lead is captured and scheduled instead of lost to the spa that simply picked up.

Built around how med spas actually get inquiries

A lot of your interest never arrives as a tidy phone call during business hours. Someone sees a before-and-after on Instagram at eleven at night and wants to book while the nerve holds. Someone fills out the form on your site over the weekend. Someone calls on a lunch break and cannot leave a voicemail from an open-plan office. A voicemail box cannot answer a nervous first-timer, and a generic call center reading a script does not know a lip flip from a filler dissolve or which one needs your nurse.

  • Answers around the clock, including the late-night scroller who just found you on Instagram and wants to book before the feeling passes.
  • Replies by text and website chat, not only by phone, because many aesthetic clients would rather type than talk.
  • Knows your menu well enough to tell a quick tox touch-up from a first-time consult and route each one the right way.
  • Treats a loyal member or package client differently from a brand-new lead, so the people who already trust you never reach a voicemail.

The math is one consultation that books

You do not need to catch many inquiries for this to pay for itself. Aesthetic treatments are high-ticket and they repeat, so a single first-time consult you would otherwise have lost, one person who comes in for tox and stays for filler, a package, and a membership, is worth far more than the system costs across the months she keeps coming back. Everything it captures after that first save is on top. The point is to stop handing your most valuable inquiries to the spa that simply answered faster.

You own the number, the leads, and the client list

This runs on your existing business number, or a new one registered in your name, not ours. Every caller, every inquiry, and every booked consult is yours and exportable any time, so the client list you build is an asset you keep instead of something you rent back month to month. There is no long contract holding your data hostage. The AI receptionist is one piece of the Top Shelf platform, and it hands every lead it captures to the same CRM that follows up, so a consult that does not book on the first call still gets nurtured toward one.

How it plays out

An 11pm text, booked by morning

Questions Med Spas Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it work with my current phone number?

Yes. It can answer on your existing business number, or set up a new one registered in your name. Either way the number and every call and text that come through it belong to you and go with you if you ever leave.

02

Can it answer texts and website chat, not just phone calls?

Yes. Many aesthetic clients would rather type than talk, and a lot of your interest comes from people who found you on Instagram or TikTok after hours. It answers by phone, by text, and through the chat on your website, so a late-night inquiry gets a real reply while the interest is still warm instead of a voicemail box.

03

Will it actually book the consult on my calendar?

Yes. Routine consults and appointments land straight on your calendar based on your availability and the consult types you set, with a confirmation to the client and a note to you. For anything it cannot fully book, it captures the details and hands them to your team so no one is left chasing a phone tag trail.

04

Will it sound like a robot to a nervous first-time client?

It answers naturally and warmly, and it is upfront rather than pretending to be a person. Someone anxious about a first treatment mostly needs to feel that a real, professional spa has them, and a calm reply that answers the question beats a voicemail box every time. You can hear it handle a live call before you decide.

05

How fast can it be running?

Setup is included with no separate onboarding fee. We configure your treatment menu, your consult types, your calendar, and how it greets a caller, so it is answering in days, not weeks. Start with a free audit and we will show you what your current setup is missing.
