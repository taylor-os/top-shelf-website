For used-car lot owners

Run the Whole Lot From Your Phone

The Dealer Suite is a CRM for independent used-car lots. Sales, the desk, finance, the shop and your numbers live in one app, and every person on your team gets the screens their job needs.

What it is

One App, a Screen for Each Job

Your owner, sales manager, salespeople, finance manager, service advisor and technicians each sign in to their own view. Each role sees what it needs. Cost and gross stay hidden from the people who shouldn’t see them, and an audit log records who changed what.

It works on a phone on the lot and on a desktop in the office. Below is one ordinary day for each part of the lot, with screens from our demo lot. Every name, car and number in them is made up.

The owner’s day

Know Where Things Stand Before You Get In

Open the app over coffee and Home shows what needs you: deals waiting on your OK, leads nobody has called yet, and deals ready to fund. At your desk, Reports has the month in front of you.

  • A deal priced below your threshold or below cost waits for a manager’s OK.
  • Reports has Sales, Inventory, Finance and Service tabs, compared with the period before. Every table downloads as a CSV.
  • Inventory: scan the VIN and the free NHTSA lookup fills in the car. Stock numbers, up to 60 photos per car, days in stock, and the FTC Buyers Guide ready to print.
  • Your cars show on inventory pages for your website, and "Ask about this car" comes in as a lead.
  • Two-factor login and an append-only audit log.
The salesperson’s day

Work the Lot With a Phone in Your Hand

The Sales tab shows a salesperson the leads waiting, their own customers and cars, and today’s ups, right on the phone.

  • Each customer and car belongs to a salesperson, and only a manager can reassign them.
  • Every car has an interest board, so the whole floor can see who wants it.
  • Web leads can go out by round-robin. The showroom log keeps every up.
  • A direct line for each salesperson: click to call, voicemail, and texts. Every call and text lands on the customer’s timeline.
  • The desk works out tax, fees and a payment grid to the cent, with asking and selling price side by side.
  • Quotes go out by text, email or link. They’re quotes only. A customer can’t sign one.
  • The pipeline board shows each deal card with the car, trade, down payment, lender status, stips, appointment and salesperson.

Lender names in the demo screens are blurred.

The finance manager’s day

Track Every Deal Until the Money Is In

The Deals page lists every financed deal by stage: working, needs a new lender, ready to fund, contract out, funded and paid. Finance starts the day there.

  • Send a deal to several lenders and compare the answers side by side.
  • Stips are tracked on each deal, so you can see what’s in and what’s still needed.
  • An F&I product menu on the deal.
  • Paperwork is tracked as checks: who did each one, and when. The app doesn’t generate legal documents.
  • A funding tracker follows the contract from sent to back to funded, then to money received.
  • A Lenders page for the lenders you send deals to.

Lender names in the demo screens are blurred.

The service and shop day

The Shop on One Board

The service advisor writes repair orders for lot cars and for customers’ cars and moves them across the shop board. Technicians keep their phone on the fender and work from their own list.

  • Recon and customer-pay repair orders on a shop board: waiting, in progress, waiting on parts, quality check, done.
  • A customer approves the estimate from a private link.
  • Recon cost rolls into the car’s cost, so nobody types it twice.
  • Technicians see only their own jobs. They clock in and out on each one and add photos, and they never see prices or customer details.

What it doesn’t do

So nobody’s surprised later: it doesn’t send deals to lenders through a lender network, print contracts or e-sign them, service buy-here-pay-here loans, pull credit, or import your current data. A new lot’s inventory and customers go in by hand.
