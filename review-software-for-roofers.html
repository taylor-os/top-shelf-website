Review Software for Roofers

Review software for roofers asks every satisfied homeowner for a review at the right moment, makes leaving one a single tap, and helps you respond to each, so your reputation keeps winning big-ticket roofs instead of depending on the few customers who remember to post on their own.

A roof is a big check, so homeowners check you first

Before a homeowner hands a roofer thousands of dollars, they Google you and read what your past customers said. A roof is not a purchase people take lightly, and there are enough storm-chasing outfits and fly-by-night crews out there that homeowners are wary by default. A thin profile with a handful of old reviews quietly costs you jobs you never hear about, because the homeowner just called the roofer with the wall of recent five-star reviews instead.

The problem is not that your customers are unhappy. It is that happy customers forget to post, and asking feels awkward when you are wrapping up a job. Review software solves the timing and the awkwardness so the reputation you have actually earned on roofs shows up online where the next homeowner is looking.

Ask at the moment they are happiest

The best time to ask is right after the job is finished and the homeowner is standing in the driveway looking at a roof that no longer leaks. The software sends the ask automatically at that moment, by text and email, with a link that takes them straight to your Google profile in one tap. No cornering people, no business cards nobody acts on, and no asking three weeks later when the relief has worn off and the roof is just a roof again.

Respond to every review, good or bad

A thoughtful reply to each review tells the next homeowner that you stand behind your work, and responding is one of the signals that helps you show up in local search. The software flags every new review the moment it lands and helps you reply quickly, so a rare critical one, the storm job that ran long or the mix-up about a start date, gets a calm, professional response instead of silence, and every good one gets a thank you.

More reviews, more roofs, compounding

Recent reviews feed your Google Business Profile, which lifts you in the local results a homeowner sees when they search for a roofer, which brings more jobs, which brings more reviews. It builds on itself, and it matters most right after a storm when a flood of homeowners are all searching at once and comparing whoever shows up. Review requests are part of the Top Shelf platform and fire automatically off a job marked complete in your CRM.

How it plays out

One finished roof becomes the next job on the block

You finish a roof replacement on a Friday. That afternoon, the software texts your happy homeowner a thank-you with a one-tap link, and they post a warm review before dinner. A week later a neighbor two doors down, who watched your crew work all week and has been meaning to get their own aging roof looked at, Googles roofers in the area and reads that exact review at the top of your profile. They call you instead of a number off a storm-chaser's flyer. You never asked twice, and you were already on another job when it happened.

Questions Roofers Ask

Common Questions

01

Which review sites does it work with?

It focuses on Google, which is where most homeowners look first and what feeds your local search ranking. It can also point past customers to your other profiles. We follow each platform's own rules, including that a review should come from a real customer whose roof you actually worked on.

02

When does it ask for the review?

Right after the job is marked complete, when the homeowner is happiest, sent automatically by text and email with a one-tap link. You can adjust the timing and the wording so it sounds like you.

03

Is automating review requests allowed?

Asking every customer for an honest review is fine and encouraged. What is not allowed is filtering out unhappy customers or paying for reviews, and this does not do that. It simply asks everyone at the right moment and makes it easy to say yes.

04

What happens if I get a bad review?

You find out immediately instead of weeks later, and the software helps you post a calm, professional response. One thoughtful reply to a critical review, sitting under a wall of genuine positive ones, often reassures a wary homeowner more than a perfect record would.

05

How long until it is running?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We connect your Google profile, set the timing and the message, and tie it to your completed jobs, so requests start going out in days. Start with a free audit and we will show you how your reputation stacks up against the roofers you compete with.
