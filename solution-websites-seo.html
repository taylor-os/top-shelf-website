What It’s Costing You

Dated and Slow Is a Revenue Problem

Customers look you up before they call. 75% of consumers judge your credibility on website design alone, and users form an opinion in 50 milliseconds. 38% stop engaging if the layout is unattractive; 44% leave for a competitor when they can’t find your contact info; 88% never come back after a bad experience. Speed is money too, bounce rate jumps from ~7% at three seconds to 38% at five, and every one-second mobile delay can cut conversions up to 20%.

Then there’s the dead end. A visitor at 9pm who can’t send you a request moves on to the next result. Every form on a Top Shelf website sends the request straight into your CRM, so the lead is waiting for you in the morning.
