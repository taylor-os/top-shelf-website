Looking for a Vagaro Alternative for Your Salon or Spa?

Vagaro is well-regarded booking and business-management software for salons, spas, and fitness studios, and it is software you run yourself. Top Shelf is an all-in-one, done-for-you platform for those same local businesses. It includes online booking, but it also adds an AI receptionist that answers and books the calls a booking app never catches, plus your website, local SEO, review generation, and marketing, all managed for you on one bill. If you want strong software to operate, Vagaro is a fair pick. If you want the whole front office handled for you, that is where Top Shelf is different.

What Vagaro does well

Vagaro is a genuinely capable tool, and an honest comparison starts by saying so. On its own site it describes itself as software and an app for beauty, wellness, and fitness businesses, and it is built deep for that world. It publicly advertises online booking, a calendar, point of sale, payment processing, forms, reports, and a branded app, along with a consumer marketplace where clients can find and book a business directly. For a salon, spa, barber, nail studio, or fitness studio that wants one place to run the day, take appointments, and check out clients, that is a strong, purpose-built package.

The thing to understand is the model, not the feature list. Vagaro is self-serve software: you and your staff run the calendar, keep the listing current, and work the tools. That suits an owner who already has a steady flow of clients and simply wants a clean, reliable way to book and manage them. Getting found by new customers, answering the phone when it rings, gathering reviews, and running marketing are separate jobs, and a booking platform is not built to do those for you.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf is more than a booking app, and it is not something you have to run yourself. It is an all-in-one, done-for-you platform plus an agency that manages the whole thing for you. Online booking is part of it, so clients can still book themselves at any hour. The difference is everything wrapped around that booking. Top Shelf builds and manages your website and local SEO so new clients can find you, generates reviews so the ones who love you say so publicly, and runs your follow-up and marketing so quiet weeks fill back up.

The piece a booking tool is not built for is the phone. Plenty of salon, spa, and fitness clients still call instead of booking online, and those calls ring while you are mid-appointment or after you have closed for the night, so they slide to voicemail and never become appointments. Top Shelf adds an AI receptionist that answers on the first ring, day or night, and books the caller straight onto your calendar. It captures the exact demand a self-serve booking flow quietly misses. All of it arrives as one managed service on one bill, and your phone number, your client list, and your data stay yours.

Which one fits your business

If you already have a full book and you mainly want reliable software to schedule and check out the clients you have, Vagaro or a booking platform like it is a reasonable choice, and some owners are happy running their own tools. If your real problem is the opposite, being hard to find online, missing calls while you work, thin reviews, and no time to market, then an all-in-one that answers the phone, gets you found, and handles the follow-up will usually do more for your calendar than a booking app alone. Plenty of businesses even keep the booking tool they like and add Top Shelf for the website, phone, reviews, and marketing on top. The honest way to decide is to look at where clients are actually slipping away, which is exactly what a free audit shows you, and you can see the full package on the pricing page.
