Automation for Roofers

Automation for roofers keeps every lead, inspection, and job on track without anyone remembering to send a text, sending estimate follow-ups while a bid is still warm, reminding homeowners about a scheduled inspection, and requesting a review the moment a roof is finished, so nothing about the process depends on an estimator finding a free minute between roofs.

The follow-through slips when everyone is on a roof

The work that wins roofing jobs is not glamorous. It is the second text on a bid, the reminder the day before an inspection, the review ask the afternoon a roof is done. Each one is small, and each one gets skipped during a busy week because the person who should send it is up a ladder. After a storm, when the volume triples, the follow-through is the first thing to fall, and it is exactly the thing that turns inspections into signed roofs.

Automation handles the routine steps so they happen on every lead and every job, whether you have one inspection this week or forty after a storm. You still make every real call and every real decision. The predictable texts and reminders are simply sent for you, on time, every time.

Estimate follow-up that runs itself

A roofing bid rarely closes on the first visit, and it goes cold fast without a nudge. Once you leave an estimate, the homeowner gets a well-timed sequence, a thank-you, an answer to the questions people always ask, a check-in while the decision is fresh, all sent automatically until they book or clearly say no. The bids waiting on an insurance decision get a longer, gentler cadence, so a homeowner still deciding weeks later is still hearing from you instead of the roofer who happened to call last.

Inspection reminders that cut the drive to nowhere

A free inspection a homeowner forgot about is a wasted trip and a wasted slot your estimator could have given someone else. Automatic confirmations and reminders go out by text and email before every inspection, so the homeowner is actually home when your estimator pulls up, and a slot that opens because someone cancels gets freed instead of sitting empty. Fewer dead trips means more roofs looked at with the same crew.

The review ask, sent the moment a roof is done

The best time to ask for a review is the afternoon the job wraps, and it is the ask most likely to be forgotten when the crew is already loading up for the next roof. When a job is marked complete, the review request fires automatically, by text and email with a one-tap link, while the homeowner is still looking up at a roof that no longer leaks. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a contact or double-texts a homeowner.

How it plays out

A bid that never goes quiet

Your estimator inspects a roof on a Monday and leaves a bid for a homeowner who is waiting on their insurance company. Over the next few weeks the homeowner gets an automatic thank-you the same day, a text answering the claims question everyone asks, and a gentle check-in timed to when claims usually clear, none of which your estimator sat down to write. When the check comes, yours is the roofer still in front of them, so they text to schedule instead of calling around. The day the roof is finished, a review request goes out on its own, and a five-star review is posted before the crew reaches the next job.

Illustrative example, not a client.

Questions Roofers Ask

Common Questions

01

What does it actually automate?

The routine steps that win and finish jobs: estimate follow-ups after an inspection, reminders before a scheduled inspection, and a review request the moment a job is marked complete. Each runs on a schedule you approve, on every lead and every job.

02

Does this replace my estimators calling homeowners?

No. It handles the predictable texts and reminders so nobody has to remember them, and it frees your estimators to make the calls that actually need a person, like walking a homeowner through a claim or closing a bid that is on the fence.

03

How does it handle bids waiting on insurance?

Those get a longer, gentler follow-up cadence, because a claim can take weeks. A homeowner still waiting on an adjuster keeps hearing from you at the right intervals, so you are the roofer they call when the decision comes instead of whoever followed up most recently.

04

Can it stop double-texting a homeowner?

Yes. It runs against the same CRM database, so it knows where each homeowner is in the process and does not send an estimate follow-up to someone who already booked or a review ask to someone whose job is not done.

05

How long does setup take?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We build the follow-up, reminder, and review sequences around how you already work, so it is running within days. Start with a free audit and we will find the gap where jobs are slipping between inspection and signature today.
