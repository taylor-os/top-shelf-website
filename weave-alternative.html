Weave is a communication platform built for practices, and it is strong at phones, two-way patient texting, reminders, and payments, working right alongside your practice management software. Top Shelf is a done-for-you, managed all-in-one: it also builds and runs your website and local SEO, answers and books new-patient calls with an AI receptionist, generates reviews, and runs your marketing, all on one bill, with your number and your leads staying yours.

What Weave does well

Weave has earned its place with dental, optometry, and other local practices for a reason. On its own site Weave describes an all-in-one communication platform built around the front desk: a business phone system, two-way patient texting, appointment reminders and confirmations, digital forms, reviews, and billing with options like text-to-pay. It is designed to sit right next to the practice management software your team already uses, so the office can call, text, and collect payment without hopping between apps. For an established practice whose main job is engaging and retaining the patients it already has, that focus is a genuine strength.

Two things are worth knowing before you decide. First, Weave is software your own staff runs day to day, so the calling, texting, and follow-up still depend on someone at the desk driving it. Second, its center of gravity is communication with the patients you already have, not getting found by new ones. Building your website, ranking in local search, and running your marketing sit outside what a communication platform sets out to do. None of that is a knock on Weave. It is simply a different job than the one Top Shelf is built for.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf is not a tool you log into and operate. It is a done-for-you system that a team sets up and runs for you, covering the whole path a new patient travels from finding you to booked. That starts with a website and local SEO so people searching for your service actually land on you, and it includes an AI receptionist that answers on the first ring, day or night, asks your intake questions, and books the appointment straight onto your calendar. It keeps your front desk from sending new-patient and after-hours calls to voicemail while your team is with someone in the chair.

From there it closes the loop the way a growth system should. A CRM follows up on every lead automatically, review generation turns your happy customers into the reviews that win the next caller, and your marketing runs on top of it, all managed for you and billed as one line instead of a stack of logins. Your phone number, your leads, and your data stay yours, so nothing is locked to us. On the front desk, the AI handles scheduling and routing only. Anything clinical or specific to a patient is always handed to your own team, never answered by software.

Which one fits your practice

The honest split comes down to what you need most. If you are an established practice that mainly wants a polished communication hub wired into your practice management software, with patient texting, reminders, and payment collection handled in one place, a purpose-built platform like Weave is squarely aimed at that. If your bigger problem is being found by new patients and turning those calls into booked appointments, and you would rather someone built the website, ran the local SEO, answered the phone, gathered the reviews, and handled the marketing for you, an all-in-one, done-for-you setup usually fits better. Some offices even want both, a communication platform for their existing patients and a done-for-you engine for growth. The way to decide is to look at where new patients actually drop off today, which is exactly what a free audit shows you, whether you work with us or not.
