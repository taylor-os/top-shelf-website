Looking for a Weave Alternative?

Weave is a communication platform built for practices, and it is strong at phones, two-way patient texting, reminders, and payments, working right alongside your practice management software. Top Shelf is a right-sized CRM with an AI receptionist built in: it answers and books new-patient calls, texts back missed calls, follows up on every lead, and sends review requests, all on one plan set up for you, with your number and your leads staying yours.

What Weave does well

Weave has earned its place with dental, optometry, and other local practices for a reason. On its own site Weave describes an all-in-one communication platform built around the front desk: a business phone system, two-way patient texting, appointment reminders and confirmations, digital forms, reviews, and billing with options like text-to-pay. It is designed to sit right next to the practice management software your team already uses, so the office can call, text, and collect payment without hopping between apps. For an established practice whose main job is engaging and retaining the patients it already has, that focus is a genuine strength.

Two things are worth knowing before you decide. First, Weave is software your own staff runs day to day, so the calling, texting, and follow-up still depend on someone at the desk driving it. Second, its center of gravity is communication with the patients you already have, not getting found by new ones. None of that is a knock on Weave. It is simply a different job than the one Top Shelf is built for.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf is a right-sized CRM for a local practice, set up for you with no setup fee, and it covers the path a new patient travels from first call to booked. At its center is an AI receptionist that answers on the first ring, day or night, asks your intake questions, and books the appointment straight onto your calendar. It keeps your front desk from sending new-patient and after-hours calls to voicemail while your team is with someone in the chair.

From there it closes the loop the way a growth system should. The CRM keeps every lead in front of you with follow-up reminders, and review requests turn your happy customers into the reviews that win the next caller, all on one plan instead of a stack of logins. Your phone number, your leads, and your data stay yours, so nothing is locked to us. On the front desk, the AI handles scheduling and routing only. Anything clinical or specific to a patient is always handed to your own team, never answered by software.

Which one fits your practice

The honest split comes down to what you need most. If you are an established practice that mainly wants a polished communication hub wired into your practice management software, with patient texting, reminders, and payment collection handled in one place, a purpose-built platform like Weave is squarely aimed at that. If your bigger problem is turning new-patient calls into booked appointments, and you want the phone answered, every lead followed up, and reviews requested without adding staff, a CRM with an AI receptionist built in usually fits better. Some offices even want both, a communication platform for their existing patients and Top Shelf for catching and booking new ones. The way to decide is to look at where new patients actually drop off today. You can also see everything included on the pricing page.

Is Top Shelf a Weave alternative for a practice?

Does the AI receptionist handle medical questions?

No. The AI works the front desk only. It answers, asks your intake questions, books appointments, and routes calls. Anything clinical or specific to a patient is handed to your own team, never answered by the software. You set the rules for what can book and what gets sent to staff.
