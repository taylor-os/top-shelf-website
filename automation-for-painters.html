Automation for Painters

Automation for painters handles the texts and reminders around every job for you, the reminder before an estimate, the on-my-way text when the crew heads out, the update partway through a multi-day repaint, and the follow-up and review request when the job is done, so everything runs smooth and nothing depends on you remembering between jobs.

A homeowner living in a half-painted house wants to know when you are coming back

A repaint takes over a home for days. Furniture is moved, rooms are taped off, the family is living around drop cloths, and the worst part for the homeowner is not knowing what happens next: is the crew coming at eight or ten, are they back tomorrow to do the second coat, is the exterior job pushed because of the rain. A customer left wondering is the one who starts to worry they hired the wrong painter, even when the work is flawless. Keeping everyone updated is exactly the kind of thing that slips when you are running a brush and a crew at the same time.

Automation handles those messages for you. A confirmation before the estimate, an on-my-way text when the crew heads out, an update partway through a multi-day job, and a heads-up if weather pushes an exterior day, all sent automatically, so the homeowner feels looked after and you are not the one stopping to type it.

Reminders that keep your schedule full and on time

A painter driving to a no-show estimate is an evening and a tank of gas you never get back, and an empty slot a real walkthrough could have filled. Automatic confirmations and reminders by text before every estimate and every job start keep no-shows down and give a customer an easy way to reschedule instead of just not being home. For an owner running a couple of crews, that is a fuller, tighter schedule without anyone working the phones to confirm each stop.

Deposits, progress payments, and the follow-up run themselves

Painting jobs get paid in stages, a deposit to book, a progress payment as the work moves, the balance at the end, and chasing each one by hand is awkward and easy to put off. Automatic, polite reminders go out when a payment is due, so the money keeps pace with the work without an uncomfortable phone call. When the job wraps, the same system sends a thank-you, an automatic review request while the fresh paint still looks its best, and a note that you have their colors on file for touch-ups, all on time, so a finished job turns into a review and a future customer without you keeping a mental list.

Cold quotes and lapsed customers come back on schedule

A quote that went quiet and a customer you painted for two years ago are not dead, they are just waiting for a reason to call. A scheduled check-in on an open estimate, a seasonal nudge when exterior weather returns, and a reminder to a customer who is due for the next room bring that work back around automatically instead of falling out of your memory. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a customer or a note, and every automated touch is logged where you can see it.

How it plays out

A customer who never has to wonder what happens next

A homeowner books a three-day interior repaint. The day before it starts, they get an automatic confirmation. Each morning the crew heads out, they get an on-my-way text with an arrival window, and at the end of day two, a quick note that the crew will be back to finish the trim in the morning. When the deposit and the progress payment come due, polite reminders go out on their own. The afternoon the job wraps, they get a thank-you with a one-tap review link while the rooms still look their best, and the system quietly files the colors for a touch-up down the road. You never sat down to send any of it.

Illustrative example, not a client.

What messages does it actually send?

The routine ones around a job: an estimate confirmation, an on-my-way text when the crew heads out, an update partway through a multi-day job, a heads-up if weather pushes an exterior day, payment reminders, and a follow-up with a review request when the work is done. 02

Does this replace me or my crew leads talking to customers?

No. It handles the predictable, repetitive texts so nobody has to stop and type them, while you and your team still make every real call and step in personally for anything that needs a conversation.

03

Can it help with no-shows and wasted trips?

Yes. Automatic confirmations and reminders before each estimate and job start keep no-shows down and give the customer an easy way to reschedule, so you spend less time driving to empty houses.

Can it handle deposits and progress payments?

Yes. Painting gets paid in stages, and the system sends polite, automatic reminders when a deposit, a progress payment, or the balance is due, so the money keeps pace with the work without an awkward call. You set the amounts and the timing.
