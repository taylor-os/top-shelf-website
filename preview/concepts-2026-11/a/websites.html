Website questions

How much does a website cost, and what's included?

$499 one time. That covers a custom design built by us that works on any phone, a site built so Google can read it, a contact and booking form that feeds the CRM, and a logo if you need one.

Is the website part of the Signature plan?

No. It's a separate one-time purchase, and you don't need the plan to buy it.

Do I need a new website to use the CRM?

No. The plan adds an AI chatbot to the website you have now. The $499 site is there if you want a new one.

Is there a monthly fee for the website?

No. The website is $499, paid once.

Do you do ongoing SEO?

No. The site is built so Google can read it: clear titles, descriptions, headings and structure. That's part of the build, not an ongoing service.

How long does it take to build?

It depends on the site. We go over that on your call.

Who handles the domain and hosting?

We go over the domain and hosting for your site on your call.
