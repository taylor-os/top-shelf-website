Online Booking for Plumbers

Online booking for plumbers lets a customer pick a time for the work that is not a 2am emergency, a water heater quote, a drain cleaning, a faucet install, a yearly inspection, straight from your website or a text link on your real availability, so you stop playing phone tag and the visit lands on your calendar with the details you need to show up ready.

Not every plumbing call is a 2am emergency

A burst pipe needs a phone and a fast truck. But a lot of plumbing work is not urgent at all: a homeowner wants a quote on a new water heater, a drain snaked, a faucet swapped, or an annual inspection, and they would happily book it themselves if you let them. Making those customers call during business hours, wait on hold, and trade voicemails is how routine jobs quietly slip to the shop that made booking easier.

A booking link lets them grab an open slot the moment they think of it, at night or on a lunch break, without reaching you first. You wake up to the visit already on the calendar instead of a missed call you now have to chase between service stops.

It sorts the emergency from the routine before it books

Self-scheduling does not mean sending a flooding kitchen to a slot three days out. The booking form asks what is going on first, and a true emergency, active leak, sewage, no water, gets pointed straight to your emergency line to call right now, while genuinely routine work, a quote, a drain, an install, books itself onto your calendar. You set the rules: which jobs are bookable online, how long each type takes, how much notice you need, and which hours are open at all.

A booking link everywhere a customer finds you

The link goes anywhere someone already meets you: your website, your Google Business Profile, your email signature, and the text you send after a call. Every one of those turns from a dead end that needs a callback into a place someone can book you in one tap. A homeowner comparing plumbers on a Sunday night for a water heater they have been putting off can put the visit on your calendar before they close the tab, instead of leaving a voicemail they will forget by Monday.

On your real calendar, and the customer actually shows

It syncs to the calendar you already use so it can never double-book a tech against a job you already have, and it sends the customer a confirmation and a reminder, which is what actually cuts no-shows. A plumber driving across town to an empty house is a wasted hour you never get back. Every booking is logged against the right customer in your CRM, and it works alongside the AI receptionist, which can also book the caller who would rather talk than tap.

How it plays out

A Sunday-night water heater, booked before Monday

It is 9pm on a Sunday and a homeowner has finally had enough of a water heater that runs cold halfway through a shower. They land on your site, see real reviews, and instead of leaving a voicemail they will second-guess in the morning, they book a replacement quote for Wednesday at 10 from the calendar right there on the page. You wake up Monday to the visit already set, with their name, address, and the make of the old unit attached. No phone tag, no lost job.

Questions Plumbers Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it sync to my Google or Outlook calendar?

Yes. It reads your real calendar so it only ever offers times you are actually free, and new bookings drop straight onto it. It cannot double-book a tech against a job you already have scheduled.

02

Will emergencies try to book a slot three days out?

No. The form asks what is going on first, and a true emergency, active leak, sewage, no water, is pointed to your emergency line to call right now instead of booking a future slot. Only the work you mark as routine can self-schedule.

03

Can I control which jobs are bookable and how long they take?

Yes. You decide which job types are available online, how long each one runs, how much notice you need, and how much buffer to leave for drive time, so you are never booked with no way to get there.

04

Will it cut down on no-shows?

It helps. Automatic confirmations and reminders by text and email are the single biggest lever on no-shows, and every booking includes them. A customer who booked themselves and got a reminder is far more likely to be home than a loosely agreed callback.

05

How fast can it be live?

Setup is included, no separate fee. We connect your calendar, set up your job types and rules, and put the link on your site and profile, so it is taking bookings in days. Start with a free audit and we will show you where you are losing routine jobs today.
