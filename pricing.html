Pricing Questions

What Owners Ask Before They Start

01

Is there a setup fee?

No. There is no setup fee on the Signature plan. The website is one price, $499, paid once.

02

Is there a contract?

No. The Signature plan is month to month, and you can stop whenever you want.

03

What do I own?

The website we build for you is yours. So are your customer list, your phone number and your domain. The CRM is software you subscribe to, like any app you pay for monthly.

04

Are there extra costs?

Just the phone line. Each line is $50 a month and covers normal use. Usage above that is billed. There are no other monthly charges.

05

Which price do I pay?

It goes by how many people on your team use the CRM. 1 to 10 users is $99 a month. 11 to 25 users is $199 a month. For 26 or more, call us and we will work out the price with you.

06

Is the website part of the Signature plan?

No. The website is $499 one time, and you can buy it with the plan or on its own. If you keep the website you have now, the plan adds the AI chatbot to it.

07

What happens when I tap Try the CRM?

You fill out a short form. We call you, then send you a demo of the CRM.

08

Can the CRM be set up for my kind of business?

Yes. We can change the layout and screens to fit how you work, or build a CRM around your industry. It's the same Signature plan, $99 a month for up to 10 users. See the screens by industry.
