The quotes you already sent are the installs you are losing

Most landscapers do not have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. You walk a yard, hand over a bid for a patio, a full landscape install, or a new irrigation system, and the homeowner says they need to think about it. A big install is a decision people sit on for weeks and compare bids on. Then you get busy on the route and never circle back, so they collect two other prices and sign with whoever followed up, not necessarily the lowest bid. The quote was never dead. It just needed one more call or text a few days later, and that is the thing there is never time for during a mowing week.

A CRM keeps every open quote in front of you and follows up on a schedule you set, with texts and emails that go out on time whether or not you remember. The homeowner deciding between three landscapers hears from you again while the other two go quiet, and the install comes back to you.

Every yard you maintain is recurring revenue you can keep for years

Past customers and standing maintenance accounts are the cheapest, steadiest work a landscaper can get. You already know the property, they already trust your crew on it, and the mulch refresh, the aeration, the next season of mowing are yours if you stay in touch. But you cannot personally remember to re-sign a few hundred customers every spring or prompt every upsell at the right time of year.

  • Every customer, property, service history, gate code, and note lives in one place instead of a truck full of clipboards and your memory.
  • Seasonal reminders go out on schedule, spring cleanup and mulch, summer mowing, fall aeration and leaf cleanup, so the upsells come back around without you tracking dates.
  • You can see which customers paused over winter and reach the right ones to re-sign before the route fills up for the season.

The customers who dropped off over winter are still yours to win back

A lot of maintenance customers pause or cancel in the winter slow season and fully mean to restart in spring, then drift to whoever markets to them first when the grass starts growing. A steady, light touch, a late-winter check-in, a spring re-up note, a reminder of what their yard looked like when you kept it, keeps your name in front of them so the new season comes back to you. It costs almost nothing, and it is the difference between rebuilding your route from scratch every spring and keeping the customers you already earned.

You own the list, and it works with the rest of the system

Every customer and every note is yours and exportable any time, not locked inside software you rent. The CRM is one piece of the Top Shelf platform and connects to the AI receptionist, so a call it answers lands in your database and gets followed up on automatically, and to online booking, so a scheduled estimate is logged against the right customer with their full property history already attached. Nothing you have earned goes cold.

How it plays out

The patio quote that closes itself

You bid a backyard patio and planting job on a Monday and the homeowner tells you they want to get another price. Normally that is the last you hear of it. Instead the CRM sends a friendly check-in two days later and a short note a few days after that, both written to sound like you. The other two companies never followed up, so when the homeowner is ready on Friday, yours is the only name still in front of them, and they book with you without shopping any further. You never sat down to chase it.

Does it import my existing customers and properties?

Yes. Your current customers, leads, and job history come in and live in one place, with the property details and notes attached, and everything stays yours and exportable. The point is to make the route and customer list you already have actually work for you.

02

Will it really follow up on my install and design quotes automatically?

Yes, on the schedule you approve. An open estimate for a patio, a landscape install, or an irrigation job gets a check-in a few days later and another after that, all sent for you, so a homeowner comparing bids keeps hearing from you while the other companies go quiet. Can it send seasonal and upsell reminders?

Yes. You set the cadence, spring mulch and cleanup, fall aeration and overseeding, a fertilization round at the right time of year, and the reminders go out automatically so the recurring add-on work comes back around without you tracking every date.
