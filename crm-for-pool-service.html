CRM for Pool Service

A CRM for pool service keeps every route customer, repair quote, and past client in one place and works them for you, so your weekly recurring base stays put, the equipment quotes a homeowner is sitting on close, and the customer who dropped off last winter comes back this spring instead of calling someone else.

The repair quotes you already sent are money sitting still

Pool companies do not usually have a lead problem, they have a follow-up problem. You are at a pool, you spot a failing heater or a cracked filter, and you text over a quote for the fix. The homeowner says they need to think about it, then you move on to the next stop and never circle back. They sit on it, or collect another price, and a job you were standing on top of quietly evaporates. The quote was never dead. It needed one more nudge a few days later, and that is the thing there is never time for between stops on a full route.

A CRM keeps every open quote in front of you and follows up on a schedule you set, with texts and emails that go out on time whether or not you remember. The homeowner deciding on a new pump hears from you again while the reminder does the work, and the repair comes back to you instead of the company that simply followed up first.

Your recurring routes are the whole business

A weekly-service customer is not one job, they are revenue every month for as long as they own the pool, and the value of a pool company is basically the size and stickiness of its route book. That base slips away quietly: a card on file expires and a payment fails, a customer goes cold and nobody notices, a seasonal client never gets asked to come back. None of it lands as a dramatic loss, it just disappears one address at a time until the route is thinner than it was.

  • Every route customer, address, pool profile, service history, and note lives in one place instead of a clipboard, a spreadsheet, and your memory.
  • You can see at a glance who is due to renew, whose payment failed, and who has gone quiet, and reach the right customer before they drift.
  • Recurring billing and service reminders run on a schedule, so a renewal or a seasonal restart gets prompted automatically instead of forgotten.

The customer who dropped off is still yours to win back

A homeowner who cancelled over the winter, or who only runs the pool part of the year, has not left for good. They just stopped, and never got a reason to start again. A short, well-timed nudge before opening season, a note that it is time to think about getting the pool ready, a reminder to a customer you have not serviced in a while, brings that revenue back around. It costs almost nothing, and reactivating someone you already served is far cheaper than winning a stranger. A pool company that works its lapsed and seasonal list every spring is sitting on a goldmine most never bother to touch.

You own the list, and it works with the rest of the system

Every customer and every pool profile is yours and exportable any time, not locked inside software you rent. The CRM is one piece of the Top Shelf platform and connects to the answering service, so a new-customer call it captures lands in your database and gets followed up on automatically, and to online booking, so a scheduled opening or repair is logged against the right customer with their full pool history already attached. Nothing you have earned goes quiet.

How it plays out

The heater quote that closes itself

You are at a routine weekly stop in March and you notice the pool heater is on its last season, so you text the homeowner a quote to replace it before opening weather hits. They tell you they want to think about it, and normally that is the last you hear of it. Instead the CRM sends a friendly check-in two days later and a short note the next week, both written to sound like you. By the time the first warm weekend arrives and they actually want the pool ready, yours is the only quote still in front of them, and they book the replacement without shopping around. You never sat down to chase it.

Does it import my existing route and customers?

Yes. Your current route customers, past clients, pool profiles, and service history come in and live in one place, and everything stays yours and exportable. The point is to make the customer list you already built actually work for you instead of sitting on a clipboard.

02

Will it really chase repair quotes on its own?

Yes, on the schedule you approve. An open quote for a pump, a heater, or a green-to-clean gets a check-in a few days later and another after that, all sent for you, so a homeowner sitting on the decision keeps hearing from you. You can jump in and message anyone directly any time.

03

Can it help me win back seasonal and lapsed customers?

Yes. You set the timing, a nudge before opening season, a note to a customer who has gone quiet, a reminder to a part-year pool owner, and the CRM sends it automatically, so revenue you already earned once comes back around instead of drifting off to another company.

04

How is this different from the answering service?

The answering service is the front door, it catches new calls the moment they ring. The CRM works the customers you already have: your recurring routes, the quotes you already sent, and the people who lapsed. One captures new demand, the other makes sure nothing you already earned slips away.
