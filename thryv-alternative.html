Looking for a Thryv Alternative?

Thryv is an established all-in-one software platform that a business owner logs into and runs, with managed marketing available as an add-on on top. Top Shelf is the done-for-you alternative built for local service businesses. Instead of handing you a dashboard to operate, a team builds and runs the whole setup for you: a website built to rank in local search, a real AI receptionist that answers the phone and books the job, a CRM that follows up on every lead, review generation, and marketing, all on one bill. The honest way to choose is to decide whether you want to run the software yourself or have it run for you, and to make sure your phone number, leads, and data stay yours either way.

What Thryv does well

Thryv is a capable, well-established platform, and it earns its place in this comparison. It pulls a broad set of tools into one login: a CRM, online scheduling, invoicing and payments, listings across many directories, reputation and review tools, and email and SMS marketing, all in one connected system. On its own site Thryv also describes managed marketing services you can add on, plus live support for the people using it. If what you want is a single subscription that replaces a stack of separate apps, that breadth is a genuine strength.

The part worth understanding is the model. Thryv is a platform you and your staff log into and operate. You manage the CRM, you build the campaigns, you keep the calendar current, and the managed services sit as add-ons around that core. For an owner who wants to be hands-on and stay in the driver seat, that control is the whole appeal. The only real question is whether running the software is work you want to own, or work you would rather hand off.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf starts from the opposite end. Rather than handing you software to run, we build and manage the whole thing for you: the website, the local SEO, the CRM and its automated follow-up, review generation, and the marketing that feeds it. You get one point of contact and one bill instead of a dashboard to learn. The headline piece is a real AI receptionist that answers your phone day or night, asks the questions you would ask, and books the job straight onto your calendar, so a call that used to slip to voicemail turns into scheduled work.

This is a difference in who does the work, not a knock on doing it yourself. A platform you operate rewards the owner who has the time and the temperament to run it. A done-for-you service fits the owner who would rather spend that time on the actual job and let someone else keep the website, the phone, and the follow-up running. Both are legitimate choices. They simply answer different questions about how you want to spend your week.

What to check before you switch

Whatever you choose, get the ownership terms in writing. With Top Shelf, your phone number, your leads, and your customer data stay yours, and the work is built to be portable to you rather than tied up inside one company system. That matters most on the day you might want to leave, so it is a fair question to put to any provider you consider, us included: if we part ways, do I keep my number, my contacts, and my data? A clear yes protects you no matter whose name is on the invoice.

It is also worth being honest about what a website can and cannot promise. We build your site to rank in local search and to turn visitors into calls, but no honest provider can guarantee a specific spot on Google, and you should be wary of anyone who does. If you are weighing a move off an existing platform, our guide on switching from Hibu or Thryv walks through how a migration actually goes, and you can see exactly what each plan includes on our pricing page.
