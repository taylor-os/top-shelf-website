# Customer review: Karen, office manager, 3-chair family dental practice, McKinney

**Reviewer id:** dental-manager
**Industry page:** industry-medical-dental.html (current) / industries/medical-dental-crm.html + -website.html (concepts)
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-27, phone (390x844) first, then desktop (1440x900). Screenshots in the scratchpad folder `customer-reviews\dental-manager\`.

## Who I am and what I'm shopping for

I'm Karen. I've run the front office here for eleven years. Three chairs, two hygienists, one dentist (she owns it), an assistant, and me plus one other person at the desk. We already have practice management software for the schedule, charting, insurance and the patient ledger. I am not replacing that. What I want is help with the phones at lunch and after 5, appointment confirmations, recall, and text-to-pay so I stop mailing statements.

Before I take anything to the doctor, I need to answer her two questions:
1. **Is it HIPAA-safe?** That means a signed BAA, and I want to know where texts and call recordings live.
2. **Will it mess up our schedule?** That means: does it book into OUR software, or into a second calendar that my team has to keep in sync by hand?

I read the fine print. A vendor that says "we go over that on your demo call" a dozen times is a vendor that doesn't want it in writing.

## Current site (updated)

**1. First 10 seconds on the phone.** I get it: "Run the whole business from one app," Try the CRM, from $99 a month, no setup fee, no contract. That's clear. The dark gold look feels like a steakhouse menu, not something I'd put patient data in, but I'd keep scrolling because the price is right there. The first screen I see is "Good morning, Travis" from an HVAC company, so I know right away this wasn't made with me in mind.

**2. Built for a business like mine?** The homepage, no. Trucks, techs, on-my-way texts. The "See your business" pop-up worked. It filled my whole phone screen, I tapped Medical & Dental, and Brightwater Dental came up with the website and CRM side by side. That's a nice touch. The medical-dental page is the only page on any version that talks to me: lunch-hour new-patient calls, recall, no-shows, and texting. Problems:
- It names two fake practices on one page, "Cedar Ridge Family Dental" in the story and "Brightwater Dental" in the screens. Which one am I supposed to be?
- "the quotes that go cold" is in the dental story. We don't send quotes.
- "Common Questions We Hear" on my dental page lists **veterinary** questions. That tells me it's a template.

**3. Trust builders and killers.**
- **Builds:** a section titled "HIPAA-Compliant Setup, BAA Included" that says "we sign a Business Associate Agreement for your practice." That is the sentence I came for. It's the only reason I didn't close the tab. Prices are the same everywhere ($99 for up to 10 users, $50 a line, $499 website), and the pricing page footnote says the customer data is ours and goes with us.
- **Kills:** the HIPAA wording contradicts itself. One paragraph says "Yes, we handle HIPAA... your practice stays compliant." The FAQ says "You are responsible for confirming your own HIPAA obligations." I know the second one is the honest one, so the first one reads like a salesman. The confirmation fix says "your desk can text a confirmation from the app the day before." So it's manual? We already do that by phone. Recall is "set a follow-up reminder for when they're due," which for 1,800 patients means my team types in 1,800 reminders. The CRM FAQ answers data export, card fees, payout timing, setup time and support hours with "we go over that on your demo call," but the pricing page says plainly that my data comes with me. Pick one. All three testimonials are a garage door company, a contractor and a car lot. Nobody in healthcare.
- The HIPAA section sits about ten phone screens down. The doctor would never get that far.

**4. Unanswered questions.** Does it connect to our practice software, and which way? When the AI books at lunch, where does that appointment go? Is the call recording kept, and where? See the full list below.

**5. Try the CRM?** **Maybe.** It's the only version where I could tell the doctor "they say they'll sign a BAA." I would still call the number first, not fill out the form.

**6. Favorite / least favorite.** Favorite: the HIPAA & BAA block. Least favorite: vet questions on my dental page.

## Concept A (light, clean, blue)

**1. First 10 seconds on the phone.** Best first screen of the four. White, calm, a big blue Try the CRM button, a call-or-text button under it, then four checkmarks: from $99 a month for up to 10 users, no setup fee, month to month, websites from $499. I know what it is and what it costs before I scroll. It looks like healthcare software. I'd keep scrolling.

**2. Built for a business like mine?** The homepage is still general: "before you leave the driveway," "a crew of 6." But "How it works" in three steps (tap, we call you, send your first invoice) is the first time anyone told me what happens next. The medical-dental page is tidy: a breadcrumb, a Sample CRM / Sample website toggle, and screens for the dentist, front desk schedule, front desk counter, hygienist, patient family page and a lunch call. I zoomed in on desktop and the screens really are a dental office: Op 1, Op 2, Op 3, an ASAP list for filling a 10:30 cancellation, "22 of 25 confirmed," recall "31 of 52 booked," family visits booked together, a $85 balance with a Pay button. Whoever built that has seen a front desk. The lunch-call screen is the one I'd show the doctor: the AI booked a new mom and her 6-year-old at 12:41, sent a confirmation, and my coworker answered a parking question.

But here's the problem. That schedule screen **is a schedule**. It shows the day by operatory with appointments in it. We already have a schedule in our practice software. Nothing on this page says whether this one connects to ours or replaces it. If it's a second calendar, that's exactly "messing up our schedule," and the doctor will say no.

**3. Trust builders and killers.**
- **Builds:** the "What you won't run into" list on the homepage (no contract, no setup fee, same price every month). The footer line that every business shown is a made-up sample. Honest. "Built so Google can read it... It isn't an ongoing SEO service." I appreciate a vendor saying what it isn't.
- **Kills:** **HIPAA is not on the page at all.** Not on the homepage, not on the dental page. When I zoomed in, the only mention is a tiny tag in the corner of the dentist's screen: "Patient texting: HIPAA setup · BAA on file." It's maybe 9 pixels tall. If you have it, say it in a heading. Also: the page says "No health details anywhere," and the schedule screen shows "Crown visit," "Restorative (60 min)," "Whitening consult." Those are health details. I'm the person who catches that, and once I catch one thing I stop trusting the rest.
- On my phone, the hygienist and patient screens sit two across and are too small to read.

**4. Unanswered questions.** Same core list, plus: is that operatory schedule in the screens your calendar or ours?

**5. Try the CRM?** **Not yet.** I'd call first and ask about the BAA and our practice software. If those two answers were on the page, this is the version where I'd tap it.

**6. Favorite / least favorite.** Favorite: the lunch-call screen and the three-step "How it works." Least favorite: HIPAA buried in a 9-pixel badge.

## My questions that no version answered

1. **Do you connect to our practice management software?** Which ones (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental)? One way or two way? Which system is the source of truth for the schedule?
2. **When the AI receptionist books at lunch, where does it book?** Into our software, or a separate calendar? What stops it from putting a new-patient exam in a 30-minute hygiene slot, or double-booking Op 2? Can it hold a request for us to confirm instead of hard-booking?
3. **Will you sign a BAA before we send you any patient data?** Do your phone, texting and AI vendors sign BAAs with you? (Concepts A, B and C don't say BAA anywhere except a tiny badge in a screenshot.)
4. **Call recordings.** The lunch-call screen has a "Listen" button. Are calls recorded? Where is that stored, for how long, and who can play it?
5. **Recall.** Does it pull who's due from our software automatically, or does my team set a reminder for each patient by hand?
6. **Confirmations.** Automatic or manual? The current dental page says the desk texts them from the app; the sample screens say "Sent automatically." Which is true?
7. **Text-to-pay.** What's the card rate? When does the money land? Does the payment post to the patient's ledger in our software, or do we re-enter it?
8. **Texting consent and opt-out.** How do patients opt in and how does STOP work?
9. **What does the AI say to "I have a toothache"?** Does it ever give clinical advice? How does it handle an emergency after hours?
10. **Setup.** How long, who does it, and does my front desk lose a day? Do you train my team?
11. **"Normal use" on the $50 line.** What is normal for a 3-chair practice? What does going over cost? Is there downtime when we port our number?
12. **Who do I call when it breaks on a Monday at 8 a.m.?** Support hours, and is it a person.
13. **Leaving.** The pricing page says my data goes with me. The CRM FAQ says "we go over how your data comes in and goes out on your demo call." In what format, and how fast?

## Ranking (for a dental office manager)

1. **Concept A.** Calmest, most clinical, clearest first screen, the only one that tells me "how it works" in steps. Its dental screens are the most convincing thing on any version. It loses points for hiding HIPAA, but that's fixable copy, not a redesign.
2. **Current site.** Ugliest for medical and full of template slips, but it's the only one that says "BAA" in a heading. Substance beats looks for me. I'd move its HIPAA content into A.
3. **Concept C.** Most honest-feeling and the best Q&A format, but the open answers expose all the "demo call" dodges, and the cream newspaper style doesn't say healthcare.

## The 8 changes I'd make to Concept A

1. **Put HIPAA in a heading on the medical-dental pages, and a line on the homepage Medical & Dental card.** On `industries/medical-dental-crm.html`, right under "What owners get," add a box: "HIPAA and your patients' data. We sign a BAA with your practice before any patient data comes in. Texts never include treatment details. Call recordings are [kept / not kept] for [X] and stored [where]." Port it from the current site, but drop "your practice stays compliant." Say what you do, and that the practice keeps its own HIPAA duties. *Why:* it's the doctor's first question. If I can't answer it from your page, I never get to question two. (Steal from current site.)
2. **Add a "Works with your practice software" section on the same page.** Name which systems it connects to, and which direction. If it doesn't connect, say so and explain what happens instead (for example: the AI takes the request, texts the patient that the office will confirm, and my desk books it in our software). *Why:* this is the "will it mess up our schedule" answer. Right now the operatory schedule screen makes it look like a second calendar, which is a dealbreaker.
3. **Write a version of the dental pitch for practices that keep their software.** One short section: "Already have Dentrix or Eaglesoft? Keep it. Use Top Shelf for the phones, confirmations, recall texts and text-to-pay." *Why:* almost every dental office already has practice software. Selling me a whole new schedule is selling me a fight with my staff.
4. **Fix "No health details anywhere."** Either change the sample schedule ("Crown visit," "Restorative," "Whitening consult") to neutral labels, or reword the line to "Texts and reminders never say why a patient is coming in." *Why:* a careful reader catches the contradiction in ten seconds, and then doubts every other promise on the page.
5. **Answer the fine print in writing instead of "we go over that on your demo call."** Card rate, payout timing, setup time, support hours, data export format, what "normal use" on the $50 line means. Use Concept C's open Q&A layout on the CRM page so it's all readable without tapping. *Why:* every deferral reads like the number is bad. A price you'll say on the phone, you can print. (Steal C's open Q&A and its top bar with the DFW number.)
6. **Make the dental screens readable on a phone.** The hygienist, patient page and lunch-call phones sit two across and are too small to read at 390 wide. Show them one per row like Concept B, and let me tap a screen to see it full size. *Why:* the screens are your best proof, and most of us look at this on a phone between patients. (Steal from B.)
7. **Add a "Your first 30 days" timeline for a practice.** Week 1 we talk and port the number, week 2 we load patients and set confirmations, week 3 the AI goes live at lunch only, week 4 full hours. Name who does each step. Change "How it works" step 3 from "Send your first invoice" to something that fits whoever is reading. *Why:* "Setup that never finished" is how my last vendor went. A dated plan tells me you've done this before.
8. **Show proof from a healthcare office, and stop leading with HVAC.** If you have a dental or medical customer, one sentence from them on the dental page. If you don't, say "new to dental, here's what we've built" and lean on the screens. On the homepage, let the "See your business" pick carry through so I see Brightwater instead of Everline once I've chosen Medical & Dental. *Why:* a garage door testimonial doesn't tell me you understand recall. (Steal the current site's "See your business" pop-up and its dental-specific "where the money goes" problems, trimmed to the three that matter: lunch calls, no-shows, recall.)
