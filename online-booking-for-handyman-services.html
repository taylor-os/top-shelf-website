Online Booking for Handyman Services

Online booking for handyman services lets a customer pick a time for the work they need done, a couple hours of help, a TV mount, furniture assembly, a short punch-list, straight from your website or a text link on your real availability, so you stop trading voicemails and the visit lands on your calendar with the list already attached.

A small job is not worth a week of phone tag

When the job is small, the effort to book it has to be small too. A homeowner wants a couple hours of help with a list, or just a TV hung before the weekend, and they are happy to schedule it, but if they have to call during the day, wait for a callback, and trade voicemails, the reward is not worth the hassle. So they put it off, or they book the handyman who let them lock in a time on the spot. The job was never the problem. The back and forth was.

A booking link lets them grab an open slot the moment they think of it, at night or on a break, without reaching you first. You come off a job to find the visit already on your calendar instead of a missed call you now have to chase between stops.

It fills the gaps between bigger jobs

Small jobs are how you turn a loose day into a full one. A one-hour mount or a quick repair fits neatly into the gap between two bigger jobs, and a booking tool that knows your real availability can offer those openings instead of leaving them empty. You set the rules: a minimum visit length so a ten-minute job does not cost you a whole trip, how long each type of work runs, how much buffer to leave for drive time, and which hours are open at all. You can even steer bookings toward days you are already working an area, so the small stuff clusters instead of scattering you across town.

A booking link everywhere a customer finds you

The link goes anywhere someone already meets you: your website, your Google Business Profile, your email signature, and the text you send after a job. Every one of those turns from a dead end that needs a callback into a place someone can book you in one tap. A homeowner scrolling for someone to assemble a crib before the baby comes, or hang a heavy mirror they do not trust themselves with, can put the visit on your calendar before they close the tab instead of leaving a voicemail they forget by morning.

On your real calendar, and the customer actually shows

It syncs to the calendar you already use so it can never double-book you against a job you already have, and it sends the customer a confirmation and a reminder, which is what actually cuts no-shows. Driving across town to an empty house is an hour you never get back, and on a small job that wasted trip can wipe out the whole ticket. Every booking is logged against the right customer in your CRM, and it works alongside the AI receptionist, which can also book the caller who would rather talk than tap.

How it plays out

A Saturday list, booked Sunday night

It is 9pm on a Sunday and a homeowner is finally fed up with a growing list, a curtain rod to hang, a cabinet door that will not stay shut, a smoke detector chirping somewhere. Instead of leaving a voicemail they will second-guess in the morning, they land on your site, see real reviews, and book two hours of handyman time for Saturday at 9 from the calendar right there on the page. You wake up to the visit already set, with their name, address, and the list attached. No phone tag, no lost job.

Questions Handyman Businesses Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it sync to my Google or Outlook calendar?

Yes. It reads your real calendar so it only ever offers times you are actually free, and new bookings drop straight onto it. It cannot double-book you against a job you already have scheduled.

02

Can customers book by the hour or by the job?

Both, however you want to sell it. You can offer set tasks like a TV mount or furniture assembly, or blocks of time like a two-hour or half-day visit, with a minimum length so a tiny job never costs you a whole trip for nothing.

03

Can I control the jobs, the length, and drive time?

Yes. You decide which job types are bookable online, how long each one runs, how much notice you need, and how much buffer to leave for driving, so you are never booked with no way to get across town in time.

04

Will it cut down on no-shows?

It helps. Automatic confirmations and reminders by text and email are the single biggest lever on no-shows, and every booking includes them. A customer who booked themselves and got a reminder is far more likely to be home than a loosely agreed callback.

05

How fast can it be live?
