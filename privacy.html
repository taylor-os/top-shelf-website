Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

How Top Shelf Business Solutions LLC collects, uses, and protects your information — including our text-messaging program.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Top Shelf Business Solutions LLC (“Top Shelf,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit topshelfsolutions.io or contact us. Questions? Email contact@topshelfsolutions.io.

Information we collect

Information you provide when you request an audit or contact us — such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, and any details you share. We also collect basic usage data (e.g., pages visited) to improve the site.

How we use your information

To respond to your requests, prepare and deliver your audit and reports, provide our services, and — only where you have opted in — send you communications such as text messages and email. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS / text messaging terms

By providing your mobile number and opting in (for example, by checking the SMS consent box on our form or replying to a message), you agree to receive text messages from Top Shelf Business Solutions LLC.

  • Message types: audit and report follow-ups, appointment and consultation reminders, account and service updates, and occasional offers.
  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or email contact@topshelfsolutions.io.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Your mobile opt-in is never shared. No mobile information or SMS opt-in consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate (for example, our messaging and CRM platforms), and only as needed to provide our services. As noted above, mobile opt-in and SMS consent data are never shared for third-party marketing.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts anytime by replying STOP, and unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message. To request access to or deletion of your information, email contact@topshelfsolutions.io.

Data security & changes

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
