The call you miss is the emergency that hires the next electrician

When a homeowner smells something burning, sees an outlet spark, or loses power to half the house, they do not leave a voicemail. They call the next electrician on the list, and the one after that, until someone picks up. You are in a panel, up a ladder, or on a commercial job with your phone in the truck, so the call rolls to voicemail and the job is gone. That is not a small miss. A panel replacement or an emergency service call is real money walking straight to whoever answered.

An AI receptionist closes that gap. It answers on the first ring, stays calm, finds out whether this is a safety emergency or planned work, gets the address and the problem, and either books the visit on your schedule or flags a true emergency for you right now, then texts you the details before you are off the ladder.

Built around how electricians actually work

Your day is not a desk with a phone on it. You are in crawl spaces and attics, on rooftops and inside panels, and the calls that matter most, the after-hours no-power call and the Saturday burning-smell call, come in exactly when you cannot stop to answer. A voicemail box does not triage anything, and a generic call center does not know a breaker that tripped once and reset from one that will not, or a quick service call from a full panel upgrade.

  • Answers 24/7, including the 11 p.m. half-the-house-has-no-power call and the weekend safety call.
  • Triages the way you would: any burning smell, sparking, or heat, is there any power at all, has the breaker been reset.
  • Books the visit on your real schedule and texts you the address and the problem on the spot.
  • Handles a true safety emergency differently from a routine inspection, so an urgent call is never treated like a tire-kicker.

Safety calls need triage, not a voicemail box

There is a real difference between a breaker that tripped once and reset fine and a panel that keeps tripping, buzzing, or smells hot. One can wait for a scheduled slot, the other should not. The AI receptionist asks the questions that sort them, whether there is a burning smell, whether any power is still on, whether it is safe to stay in the home, so a genuine hazard gets your attention right away and a routine issue gets booked for your next opening instead of pulling you off a job for nothing.

You own the number, the leads, and the customer list

This runs on your existing phone number, or a new one registered in your name, not ours. Every caller, every job, and every contact detail is yours and stays yours, exportable any time. There is no long contract and no holding your data hostage to keep you paying. The AI receptionist is one piece of the Top Shelf platform, and it plugs into the same CRM that follows up on every quote and past customer, so nothing it captures goes cold.

A Saturday-night panel call, while you are on another job

Questions Electricians Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it work with my current phone number?

Yes. It can answer on your existing number, or set up a new one registered in your name. Either way the number and every lead that comes through it belong to you and go with you if you ever leave.

02

Can it tell a real emergency from a routine call?

It triages with the questions you would ask: any burning smell, sparking, or heat, is there any power at all, has the breaker been reset. A genuine hazard gets flagged to you right away, and routine work gets booked for your next opening instead of interrupting the job you are already on.

03

Can it actually book the visit?

Yes. It books onto your real schedule based on your availability, sends you the address and problem by text right away, and can confirm the time with the caller. You set the rules, like how much notice you need and which blocks are open for work.

04

Is this going to sound like a robot to my customers?

It answers naturally and professionally, and it is upfront rather than pretending to be a person. When someone has no power, what they care about is that a real process is handling their call instead of a voicemail box. You can hear how it takes a live call before you decide.

05

How fast can it be running?
