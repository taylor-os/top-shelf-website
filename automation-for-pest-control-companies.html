Automation for Pest Control Companies

Automation for pest control companies handles the texts and reminders around every job for you, the notice that rebooks a quarterly plan, the prep and on-my-way message before a visit, the review request and plan offer after the work is done, so your routes stay full and nothing depends on you remembering between stops.

Recurring plan visits that rebook themselves

The recurring plan is the whole business, and it only works if the next visit actually gets scheduled. Every quarterly service and every monthly stop has to be rebooked for the plan to keep billing and the customer to stay yours, and doing that by hand, reminding hundreds of members their next service is due, is exactly the task that slips when the office is buried and the techs are on full routes. A plan visit that never gets rebooked is a member quietly sliding toward churn.

Automation sends each member their rebooking notice on schedule, at the right point in their cadence, with a link to confirm or pick a time, so the recurring visits fill back in on their own and the plan proves its worth without anyone in the office chasing a list.

Prep and on-my-way texts, so the visit actually works

A pest treatment often needs the customer to do something first, pull things out from under the sink, secure pets and kids away from where you will spray, or run through the prep a bed bug job requires, and a customer who was not told is a wasted trip. Automation sends the confirmation, a prep reminder with exactly what to do before you arrive, and an on-my-way text with a realistic window when the tech heads out, all sent for you. The customer is ready when you get there, the house is set up for the treatment to work, and you are not the one stopping to type it between stops.

The follow-up after the job runs itself

The job is not really done when you drive away. A quick check that the problem has stopped, an automatic review request while the customer is still relieved, and, for a one-time job, an offer to move onto the recurring protection plan all matter, and all are easy to forget once you are onto the next stop. Automation fires each one on time, so a finished job turns into a review, a plan member, and a customer for years instead of a single fee you never hear from again.

Warranties, lapsed members, and cold quotes come back on schedule

A termite warranty due to renew, a plan member who drifted off, and a bed bug or termite quote that went quiet are not dead, they are just waiting for a reason to act. A scheduled renewal reminder before a warranty expires, a seasonal nudge to a lapsed member, and a check-in on an open quote bring that work back around automatically instead of falling out of your memory. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a customer or a note, and every automated touch is logged where you can see it.

How it plays out

A plan member who never has to remember their next service

A homeowner is on your quarterly protection plan. As their next service comes due, the automation sends a rebooking notice with a link to confirm the time, then a prep reminder the day before telling them to move the patio furniture and keep the dog inside, and an on-my-way text when your tech leaves the stop before them. The visit happens without a single phone call from your office, the treatment works because the yard was ready, and the plan renews on the strength of actually getting used. You never sat down to schedule any of it.

Questions Pest Control Companies Ask

Common Questions

01

What messages does it actually send?

The routine ones around a job: the rebooking notice for a recurring plan, an appointment confirmation, a prep reminder with what to do before you arrive, an on-my-way text when the tech heads out, and a follow-up with a review request after the work is done. You approve the wording so it all sounds like you.

02

Can it rebook my recurring plan visits automatically?

Yes. It tracks each plan's cadence and sends the member a rebooking notice when their next service is due, with a link to confirm or pick a time, so the recurring visits fill back in without anyone in the office working a list.

03

Can it send the prep instructions customers need before a treatment?

Yes. A prep reminder goes out before the visit with exactly what the customer needs to do, clear areas, secure pets and kids, or run through bed bug prep, so the tech is not driving to a house that is not ready and the treatment actually works.

04

Will it handle warranties, lapsed members, and cold quotes?

Yes. It can send a renewal reminder before a termite warranty expires, a seasonal nudge to a member who drifted off, and a check-in on an open quote, all on a schedule you set and all logged against the customer in your CRM.

05

How long does setup take?
