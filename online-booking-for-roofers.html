Online Booking for Roofers

Online booking for roofers lets a homeowner schedule a free inspection straight from your website, a storm-response ad, or a text link, on your real availability, so you stop playing phone tag and the inspection lands on your calendar with the address and the damage details your estimator needs to show up ready.

Phone tag is quietly costing you inspections

A homeowner notices a missing shingle or a water stain and wants someone to look at it. Your estimator is on a roof, so they call back two hours later, the homeowner is at work, they trade voicemails, a day passes, and the roof gets looked at by the company that could lock in a time on the spot. The inspection was never the problem. The back and forth was. When someone is worried enough about their roof to call, the worst thing you can do is make them wait for a callback.

A booking link lets them grab an open inspection slot the moment they feel it, day or night, without reaching anyone first. Your estimator wakes up to the inspection already set instead of a missed call they now have to chase between jobs.

It qualifies the roof before it books

Self-scheduling does not mean sending an estimator across town for nothing. The booking form asks what matters first: storm damage or normal wear, whether the roof is actively leaking, insurance claim or paying directly, the age of the roof, and the address. A clear storm-damage claim books an inspection, and a vague question gets routed for a quick call, so your estimator's day is spent on roofs worth driving to. You set the rules: how many inspections a day, how long each takes, and how much drive time to leave between them.

A booking link everywhere a homeowner finds you

The link goes anywhere a lead already meets you: your website, your Google Business Profile, your storm-season ads, your email signature, and the text you send after a missed call. Every one of those turns from a dead end that needs a callback into a place a homeowner can book an inspection in one tap. After a storm, when everyone is calling every roofer at once, letting people book themselves is the difference between catching the surge and drowning in it.

On your real calendar, and the inspection shows up

It syncs to the calendar your estimators already use so it can never double-book a slot, and it sends the homeowner a confirmation and a reminder, which is what actually cuts no-shows on free inspections. Every booking is logged against the right homeowner in your CRM, and it works alongside the AI receptionist, which can book a caller who would rather talk than tap. The leads and the calendar stay yours.

How it plays out

A storm-night homeowner books an inspection while you sleep

It is 10 p.m. after a wind storm and a homeowner sees shingles in the yard. They are deciding which roofer to call in the morning. They land on your site, see your real reviews, and instead of leaving a voicemail they will forget by breakfast, they book a free inspection for Thursday at 9 from the calendar right there on the page. Your estimator wakes up to the inspection already set, with the address, the roof age, and a note that the homeowner plans to file a claim. No phone tag, and the roof did not go to the first company that answered.

Questions Roofers Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it sync to my estimators' calendars?

Yes. It reads their real calendars so it only offers times someone is actually free, and new inspections drop straight on. It cannot double-book an estimator against a job or another inspection already scheduled.

02

Can homeowners book, or does it send someone out for junk leads?

You control that. The form asks about damage, leaks, roof age, and whether it is an insurance claim, so a real prospect books an inspection and a vague inquiry gets routed to a quick call first. Your estimator's time goes to roofs worth the drive.

03

Can it respect drive time and stop back-to-back inspections across town?

Yes. You set how many inspections a day each estimator takes, how long each runs, and how much buffer to leave between them, so nobody is booked with no time to cross the city.

04

Will it cut down on no-shows for free inspections?

It helps. Automatic confirmations and reminders by text and email are the single biggest lever on no-shows, and every booking includes them. A homeowner who booked themselves and got a reminder is far more likely to be home than a loosely agreed callback.

05

How fast can it be live?

Setup is included, no separate fee. We connect your calendars, set up your inspection types and rules, and put the link on your site, ads, and Google profile, so it is taking bookings in days. Start with a free audit and we will find the gap where inspections are slipping through today.
