When it rains, the whole route shifts, and everyone wants to know when you are coming

Weather runs a landscaper's calendar more than anything else. A rained-out morning bumps every stop on the route, and within an hour the phone is full of customers asking when you will be there, or worse, a commercial account assuming the crew skipped them. Texting each one back is exactly the kind of thing that slips when you are busy reshuffling a whole day of stops on the fly. The work can be perfect and the customer still unhappy simply because nobody told them the schedule moved.

Automation handles those messages for you. A heads-up before the crew arrives, a rain-delay text sent to the whole affected route at once, and a note when a stop moves, all sent automatically, so nobody is left wondering and no account thinks you no-showed while you are out running the rest of the day.

Reminders that keep your route full and on time

A crew that pulls up to a locked gate, a dog loose in the yard, or a car parked over the beds loses the stop and throws off the rest of the route. A confirmation the day before and a heads-up the morning of keep the property ready and the crew moving, and give a customer an easy way to say if today does not work. For an owner running a couple of crews, that is a fuller, tighter route without anyone working the phones to confirm every stop.

The follow-up after the job runs itself

The job is not really done when the trailer pulls away. A quick check that the customer is happy, an automatic review request while the yard is looking its best, and a seasonal reminder, aeration in the fall, mulch in the spring, a fertilization round scheduled for the right month, all matter, and all are easy to forget once you are onto the next stop. Automation fires each one on time, so a finished job turns into a review, an upsell, and the next visit without you keeping a mental list.

Cold quotes and lapsed customers come back on schedule

An install quote that went quiet and a maintenance customer who dropped off over the winter are not dead, they are just waiting for a reason to call. A scheduled check-in on an open bid, and a spring re-up nudge to a customer who has not been on the route in a while, bring that work back around automatically instead of falling out of your memory. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a customer or a note, and every automated touch is logged where you can see it.

How it plays out

A rained-out Tuesday that does not become a day on the phone

Rain rolls in on a Tuesday morning and the whole day's route has to move to Thursday. Instead of your office fielding a stack of calls one by one, the system texts every affected customer at once that the crew will be there Thursday, with a simple line for anyone who needs a different day. The commercial account that would have assumed you skipped them knows exactly when you are coming. By that afternoon the route is reset, nobody is annoyed, and you never picked up the phone to explain the weather.

Questions Landscapers Ask

Common Questions

01

What messages does it actually send?

The routine ones around your route and your jobs: a confirmation and a heads-up before the crew arrives, a rain-delay text to the whole affected route when the weather moves the day, and a follow-up with a review request after the work is done. You approve the wording so it all sounds like you.

02

Does this replace me or my office talking to customers?

No. It handles the predictable, repetitive texts so nobody has to stop and type them, while you and your team still make every real call and step in personally for anything that needs a conversation, like a commercial account with a special request.

03

Can it reschedule my whole route when it rains?

Yes, and this is where it earns its keep for a landscaper. When the weather moves your day, it can notify every affected stop at once with the new day, so a rained-out morning does not turn into an afternoon on the phone or a customer who thinks you forgot them.

04

Will it send seasonal and follow-up reminders on its own?

Yes. After a job it can schedule a review request and a seasonal reminder, aeration, mulch, or a fertilization round, for the right month, and it can nudge cold quotes and customers who lapsed over the winter, all logged against the customer in your CRM.

05

How long does setup take?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We build the reminder, rain-delay, and follow-up sequences around how you already run your route, so it is working within days.
