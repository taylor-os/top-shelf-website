The quote call you miss is the recurring client the next maid service signs

A homeowner who wants a cleaner is not going to leave a voicemail and wait. They found three maid services on Google and they are calling down the list until someone picks up and gives them a real answer. But your day is spent inside homes, running a crew, hands on a vacuum or a mop, so the phone rings through to voicemail and the caller is already dialing the next company before yours even finishes ringing. And in cleaning, the call you just lost is rarely a single job. A new client who wanted weekly or biweekly service is weeks, months, sometimes years of cleans, and all of it just went to the company that answered.

An AI receptionist answers on the first ring, whether you are mid-clean or the call comes in on a weekend evening while someone is finally sitting down to book a cleaner. It gathers what you need to quote, the size of the home, how often they want it, and what kind of clean, and either books the visit or hands you a ready-to-price lead. The recurring client is captured instead of lost to the maid service down the road.

Built around how cleaning calls actually come in

Your phone does not ring while you are sitting at a desk. It rings while you are inside a home you are cleaning, driving between stops, or off the clock in the evening when a busy family finally has a minute to line up a cleaner. A voicemail box cannot gather a quote, and a generic call center reading a script does not know a standard clean from a deep clean or a move-out.

  • Answers during the day while your crews are inside homes, and in the evenings and weekends when people actually sit down to book a cleaner.
  • Gathers what a real quote needs: home size, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, one-time or recurring, and whether it is a standard clean, a deep clean, a move-in or move-out, or post-construction.
  • Books the recurring slot or the first clean straight onto your calendar and texts you the details on the spot.
  • Handles a current client calling to reschedule, skip a week, or add the inside of the fridge differently from a brand-new prospect, so the people who already trust you never hit a voicemail.

The math is one recurring client

You do not need to catch many calls for this to pay for itself. A single weekly or biweekly client you would have lost to voicemail is not one clean, it is a standing spot on your schedule that pays week after week for as long as they stay with you, which is often years. One of those kept is usually worth more than the system costs for a long stretch, and everything it captures after that is on top. The point is to stop handing your best recurring clients to whichever company simply answered first.

You own the number, the calls, and the client list

This runs on the number you already advertise, or a new one registered in your name, not ours. Every caller, every address, and every note about the home is yours and exportable any time, so the client list you are building is an asset you keep instead of something you rent back month to month. There is no long contract holding your data hostage. The AI receptionist is one piece of the Top Shelf platform, and it hands every call it captures to the same CRM that follows up, so a quote or a first-clean offer never slips.

How it plays out

A move-out clean with a Friday deadline, booked while you work

A renter finds out on Monday that their lease ends Friday and they need a move-out clean to get the deposit back. They search for a cleaner and call the first few they find. Two go to voicemail. Yours answers, confirms the size of the place and the move-out deadline, quotes the deep clean it will take, and books your crew for Thursday morning while your team is inside another home and never touches a phone. You come off the job to the clean already on your calendar with the address, the square footage, and the deadline attached, instead of hearing about it after they hired someone else.

Yes, that is the point. It asks what you would ask, the size of the home, how many bedrooms and bathrooms, how often they want service, and whether it is a standard clean, a deep clean, or a move-out, so a recurring client and a one-time job each come to you ready to schedule and price.
