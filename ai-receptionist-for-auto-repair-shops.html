AI Receptionist for Auto Repair Shops

An AI receptionist for auto repair shops answers every call the moment it rings, even when every tech and the owner are under a car or out on a test drive. It sorts out whether the caller needs a quote, a drop-off, or a tow, books it on your schedule, and keeps your own number yours.

The call you miss is the customer who dials the next shop

The phone rings while every tech, including you, is under a car, on a lift, or out on a test drive chasing a noise. Nobody is free to grab the counter, so the call rolls to voicemail, and a driver who needs their car looked at today does not leave a message. They just dial the next shop on the list, and the one after that, until a person picks up. That is how a shop with a full bay quietly loses the work it never even heard ring.

An AI receptionist answers on the first ring, finds out what the car is doing, whether it is drivable or needs a tow, and either books the drop-off on your schedule or takes a message with everything you need. The job is captured instead of handed to the shop down the road that happened to be standing by the phone.

Built around how a busy bay actually gets calls

Most of the calls that hit an auto shop are not roadside emergencies. They are people checking on a car that is already in the bay, asking what a repair will run, or trying to book a drop-off, and they all land in the middle of the workday when the counter is empty and the phone is ringing over an impact wrench. A voicemail box is not an answering service, and a generic call center reading a script does not know a brake job from an oil change or which car can wait until next week.

  • Answers every call during the busy bay, so a driver checking on their car or asking whether you can look at a warning light today reaches a real answer instead of a voicemail.
  • Triages the way your service writer would: what the car is doing, whether it is drivable or needs a tow, whether it is a warning light, a noise, a leak, or routine maintenance, and how soon the customer needs it back.
  • Gathers the year, make, model, and the complaint, books the drop-off on your calendar, and texts you the details so the car shows up with a work order already started.
  • Handles a regular differently from a first-time caller, so the customers who keep your bays full never hit a voicemail.

One first-time customer is worth years of work

You do not need to catch many calls for this to earn its keep. A first-time driver who trusts you with an oil change comes back for brakes, for the state inspection, for the timing belt, and sends their family and coworkers your way, so a single call you would have lost to voicemail is the start of years of steady work, not one ticket. Everything the receptionist captures after that is on top. The point is to stop handing your first-time callers to whoever happened to answer faster while your hands were full.

You own the number, the calls, and the customer list

This runs on your existing shop number, or a new one registered in your name, not ours. Every caller, every vehicle, and every note is yours and exportable any time, so your customer list stays an asset you own instead of something you rent back month to month. There is no long contract holding your data hostage. The AI receptionist is one piece of the Top Shelf platform, and it comes together with the CRM on the Signature plan, so every call it captures lands in the same system that follows up and a quote or a service reminder never slips.

How it plays out

A check-engine light, booked while you were under a car

A driver on the way to work sees the check-engine light come on and pulls over to find a shop. They call yours first. Every tech is heads down in the bay, so on any other day it goes to voicemail and they keep driving to the next shop. Instead the receptionist answers, asks whether the car is still running normally, confirms it is drivable, and books a diagnostic drop-off for the next morning while flagging it to your phone. You come out from under a car to a work order already started, with the year, make, model, and the complaint attached, instead of hearing about a customer who went somewhere else.

Does it work with my current shop number?

Yes. It can answer on your existing number, or set up a new one registered in your name. Either way the number and every call that comes through it belong to you and go with you if you ever leave.

Yes. It asks whether the car is drivable, whether it is safe where it sits, and whether the customer needs a tow, then books the drop-off or flags it to you right away so you can decide how to get the vehicle in.

Yes. It books against your real availability and the bay time a job needs, and you get a text with the year, make, model, and complaint so the car arrives with a work order already started.
