What It’s Costing You

The Missed Call Is the Profit Killer

Small businesses answer only about 37.8% of incoming calls. For every ten that ring, six or seven go unanswered — and 85% of the people who hit voicemail never call back. 62% immediately dial a competitor. Every missed call is a permanent loss of that customer’s lifetime value: ~$850 for a dental patient, $275–$1,200 for a plumbing or electrical service call, $400–$450 for an auto ticket, thousands for a law firm.

The best leads hide after hours. 25–40% of home-service calls come outside 8am–5pm; 48% of plumbing emergencies land between 6pm and 8am — and those emergency tickets are worth $450–$600 versus ~$275 for a standard daytime job. A locked door at 6pm makes you invisible to more than half your potential customers. Meanwhile a web form that comes in at 9pm and gets a reply the next afternoon is a lead that already went cold — 82% of people would rather use a chatbot than wait, and only 18% will wait even 15 minutes.

Example call After hours · a homeowner’s water heater is leaking
AI receptionist

Thanks for calling [Your Business]. This is the front desk. What can I help you with tonight?

Caller · a homeowner

Hey, uh, my water heater is leaking all over the garage floor. Is somebody actually there? It’s pretty late.

AI receptionist

You’ve got me, and I can get this handled for you. First, is everyone in the house okay, and is it just water, no gas smell or anything electrical?

Caller · a homeowner

No, everyone’s fine. It’s just water, but it’s a lot of it.

AI receptionist

Good, that’s the part that matters. If you can safely reach the valve on top of the tank, turning it clockwise stops more water from coming out while we get someone to you. Can I grab your name and the address?

Caller · a homeowner

Sure. It’s Jordan, and I’m at 14 Miller Court.

AI receptionist

Thanks, Jordan. I have you at 14 Miller Court, and I’ll text the confirmation to the number you’re calling from. I can get a technician out first thing tomorrow between 8 and 10 in the morning, or mark it urgent for a callback tonight if you’d rather not wait. Which works better?

Caller · a homeowner

Tomorrow morning is fine, honestly. I just didn’t want to leave a voicemail nobody checks.

AI receptionist

You won’t have to. You’re booked for tomorrow, 8 to 10 AM, for a leaking water heater at 14 Miller Court. I’m sending the confirmation text now and letting the owner know it came in tonight. Anything else while I have you?

Caller · a homeowner

No, that’s it. Thanks a lot.

AI receptionist

Happy to help, Jordan. Get some rest, and we’ll see you in the morning.
