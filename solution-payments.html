How Payments & Financing Works for You

Payments & Invoicing

A branded, easy way to bill customers and let them pay you online in a couple of taps.

Why it matters: You finished the job weeks ago and you're still texting 'just checking in' about that invoice, feeling like a nag while the money sits in someone else's account. Meanwhile your own bills are stacking up. Every day an invoice sits unpaid is a day your cash is working for the customer instead of for you.

35%E-invoices get paid about 35% faster than paper invoices, and invoices with an online-pay option are paid up to 4x faster. unimaze.com
You get: You get paid faster, stop chasing, and always know exactly what's owed and what's landed in the bank.

Financing / Buy-Now-Pay-Later

A simple 'pay monthly' option your customers can choose right when they're deciding to say yes.

Why it matters: A customer needs the full repair but their eyes go wide at the number, so they pick the cheap patch job, or walk away entirely. You leave the bigger, better work on the table because the price scared them off. When a customer can't stomach the lump sum, you lose the sale you should have won.

61%One HVAC & plumbing business increased its average HVAC ticket by 61% (and plumbing 34%) after offering consumer financing. buyfin.com
You get: More customers say yes to the full job, your average ticket goes up, and the money hits your account fast.

Text-to-Pay & Pay-by-Link

A secure payment link you can text on the spot, so the customer pays from their phone before you even leave.

Why it matters: The customer says 'just send me an invoice,' and you both know what that means. It drops to the bottom of an inbox and you're chasing it again next week. The longer paying takes, the easier it is for them to put off.

You get: Payment happens in the moment, while you're still standing there, instead of turning into a week of polite reminders.

Recurring & Membership Billing

Cards kept securely on file and charged automatically for plans, memberships, and retainers.

Why it matters: Every month you re-send the same invoice to the same regulars and wait on the same checks. It's steady money that still takes manual work to collect, and any month it slips your mind is money you just don't see.

You get: Predictable revenue that lands on schedule by itself, with no invoice to send and nobody to chase.

Lower Processing Rates

A plain-English look at what you actually pay to accept cards, and a setup that stops you overpaying.

Why it matters: Card fees are buried in a statement built to be impossible to read, and most owners never notice their effective rate crept up years ago. It's a few points skimmed off every single sale, quietly, forever.

You get: More of every sale stays in your pocket, and you finally know exactly what each swipe is costing you.

Common Questions

What Owners Ask About Getting Paid

01

How does this actually get me paid faster?

Customers get a branded invoice they can pay online in a couple of taps, instead of a paper bill that sits at the bottom of a pile. E-invoices with an online-pay option get paid noticeably faster than paper invoices and checks. You stop chasing, and you always know what is owed and what has landed in the bank.

02

What is the financing option, and why would I offer it?

Financing is a simple pay-monthly option your customer can choose right when they are deciding whether to say yes. When the full price makes someone hesitate, monthly payments let them approve the whole job instead of the cheap patch or walking away. Businesses that offer it tend to see a bigger average ticket, and the money still hits your account fast.

03

Can I take payment on the spot, before I leave the job?

Yes. You can text a secure payment link right there, and the customer pays from their phone before you pack up. Payment happens in the moment while you are still standing there, instead of turning into a week of polite reminders.

04

How do memberships and repeat billing work?

Cards are kept securely on file and charged automatically for plans, memberships, and retainers. You stop re-sending the same invoice to the same regulars every month and waiting on the same checks. It is predictable revenue that lands on schedule, with nobody to chase.

Will you help me stop overpaying on card fees?

Yes. We give you a plain-English look at what you actually pay to accept cards, since those fees are buried in a statement built to be hard to read. Then we set you up so you stop overpaying and keep more of every sale. The free audit is where we show you what each swipe is really costing you.
