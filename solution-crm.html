What It’s Costing You

The Fortune Is in the Follow-Up

Only 27% of leads ever get contacted at all, and up to 73% are never reached. 80% of new leads never become customers, and the number-one reason is poor follow-up: late responses, no nurturing, dropped conversations. Nearly half of salespeople never follow up after the first touch, yet 80% of sales need five or more. You paid, in ads, time, or referrals, to make the phone ring, then let most of those people walk because nobody chased them.

And your best future customers are the ones who already paid you once. Selling to an existing customer closes 60-70% of the time versus 5-20% cold, and reactivating them is 5-7x cheaper than buying a new lead with ads. Most businesses have a goldmine of past customers and dead quotes sitting in a spreadsheet, or worse, in one employee’s phone that walks out the door when they quit. A CRM puts all of it in one place the business owns.

What the CRM Does

Every Lead Kept. Every Follow-Up Remembered.

Here is what each part gets you.

Every Customer in One Place

Names, numbers, notes and past jobs live in one contact list the business owns. When someone calls back a year later, you know who they are and what you did for them.

Your customer list stops living in one employee’s phone. If that person leaves, the list stays.

A Pipeline You Can See

Every new lead and open quote sits on one board, from the first call to the booked job. You can see what’s waiting on you and what’s waiting on the customer.

You stop holding it all in your head, and a hot quote doesn’t go cold because nobody remembered it.

Follow-Up Reminders

Put a reminder on any lead or quote. When it’s time to call back, the CRM tells you, or whoever owns that lead.

Most sales take five or more touches, and nearly half of salespeople stop after one. The reminder keeps you from being one of them.

Estimates and the Dispatch Board

Send an estimate from the customer’s record. When they say yes, it turns into a job, and the dispatch board shows who’s going where and when.

The quote, the job and the schedule sit together. Nobody copies details from one app into another.

A Customer Hub

Your customer gets one link where they can see their estimates and invoices, so they don’t have to call and ask.

Fewer “just checking on my quote” calls, and your business looks organized from the customer’s side too.

Your Follow-Up Questions

01

What is a CRM, and do I need one if I already have a system?

A CRM is one place that holds every lead and every past customer and reminds you when to follow up. Most owners are running on a spreadsheet, sticky notes, or one employee's phone that walks out the door when that person quits. A CRM puts all of it in one place the business owns, and nobody has to remember who to call. If you have ever lost a quote or forgotten to call someone back, you need one.

02

What happens when I miss a call?

The caller gets a text back right away, so they hear from you before they try the next business. The AI receptionist in the same plan can also answer the call and book the job. Buyers tend to go with whoever answers first, and this keeps that business yours.

03

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. The Signature plan is priced by the number of users: $99 a month for 1 to 10 users, $199 a month for 11 to 25 users, and call for pricing at 26 users and up. Everyone works from the same customer list, the same pipeline and the same dispatch board.

04

Will leads and customers still slip through the cracks?

That’s the whole point of it. Every new lead and open quote sits on the pipeline with a follow-up reminder, and a missed call gets a text back right away. You see every open job on one board instead of holding it all in your head.
