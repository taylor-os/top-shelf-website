01

Never miss a job

Most work starts with a phone call. The CRM makes sure that call goes somewhere, even when your hands are full or the shop is closed.

  • An AI receptionist that answers and books

    It picks up when you can't, takes the caller's details and puts the job on your calendar. You find a booked job in the morning instead of a voicemail.

  • Missed calls get a text back

    If a call still slips by, the caller gets a text from your business right away, before they try the next company on the list.

  • Calls and texts on your own number

    Call and text customers from the app on the number you already have, or we set you up with a new one. Each line is $50 a month and covers normal use.

  • A chatbot on your website

    We add an AI chatbot to the website you have now, so visitors can get answers after hours too.

02

Get paid from the customer's phone

Paper invoices and "I'll mail a check" slow everything down. Here the invoice goes out by text and the customer pays from the same link.

  • Invoices by text

    Send the invoice the minute the work is done. It goes to the customer's phone as a text with a link.

  • Customers pay by card from their phone

    They tap the link, enter a card and they're done. Nobody has to call the office or find a stamp.

  • Card payments in the same app

    Payments land next to the job and the customer record, so you can see what's paid and what's still owed without opening another program.

03

Estimates people can read

A quote on the back of a business card gets lost. A clear estimate in the customer's texts doesn't.

  • Send estimates by text

    Build the estimate from the customer's record and text it before you leave. They read it on their phone, line by line.

  • A customer hub for every customer

    Each customer gets one link where they can see their estimates and invoices, so they don't have to call and ask.

  • See which estimates are still open

    Open estimates stay in front of you with a follow-up reminder, so a big quote doesn't sit for a week while everyone forgets about it.

04

Everything in one place

Customer details, open quotes and who to call back usually live in five places. The CRM puts them in one.

  • One customer list the business keeps

    Names, numbers, notes and past jobs sit in one list. When someone calls back a year later, you know who they are and what you did for them. If an employee leaves, the list stays with the business.

  • A pipeline you can see

    Every new lead and open quote sits on one board, from the first call to the booked job. You can see what's waiting on you and what's waiting on the customer.

  • Follow-up reminders

    Put a reminder on any lead or quote. When it's time to call back, the CRM tells you or whoever owns that lead.

  • Your whole team on the same page

    Up to 10 people use it for $99 a month. Everyone works from the same list, the same pipeline and the same schedule.

05

One schedule board

When the office and the crew look at the same schedule, nobody has to call around to find out who's where.

  • A dispatch board for the day

    See which job goes to which person, and when, on one board. Move a job and everyone sees the change.

  • On-my-way texts in one tap

    Your tech taps once and the customer gets a text that someone is on the way. That's one less "when are you coming?" call to the office.

06

Customers book themselves

Plenty of people would rather book at 10pm than call at 10am. Let them.

  • Online booking

    Customers pick a time from your real calendar, day or night, and the booking shows up in the CRM.

  • Website forms that feed the CRM

    Every request sent from a Top Shelf website goes straight into your CRM, so it's waiting for you instead of sitting in an inbox.

07

More reviews

Happy customers forget to leave a review unless someone asks while the job is still fresh.

  • One tap after every job

    Tap once and your review link goes to the customer by text. It takes a few seconds, so it actually gets done.

  • See who has been asked

    You can see who got a request and who left a review, and write your replies in the app.

08

Built around your trade

A dentist's day and a roofer's day look nothing alike, so the CRM shouldn't look the same for both.

  • Layouts that fit how you work

    We can change the layout and screens to match your business, or build a custom CRM for your industry. Either way it's the same $99 a month for up to 10 users.
