Where an optometry practice actually loses patients

Optometry is really two businesses on one floor, the eye exam in the back and a retail optical up front, and that is exactly where patients slip away. The person who answers the phone is usually on the optical floor fitting frames or checking a patient out, so when someone calls to book an exam, the call rings to voicemail and they book the next optometrist. What walks away is not one appointment. It is the exam, the glasses or contacts that ride on top of it, the vision benefits they were about to spend with you, and often the whole family.

The same money slips away from the patient list you already have. A patient whose annual exam is due, a contact-lens wearer about to run out, a shopper who priced a box against an online seller, each one drifts without a nudge. Top Shelf closes the gaps. An AI receptionist answers every call, handles the vision-plan and order-status questions, and books the exam, so nobody leaves the optical floor to pick up. A CRM sends annual recall and reorder reminders before a wearer runs out.

One system, not a phone and a spreadsheet

Recalls slip and calls go to voicemail because answering, follow-up, reviews, and the website live in separate places, and one person cannot run the optical and all of them at once. Top Shelf connects them. The exam the receptionist books lands in the same CRM that sends next year's recall and the contact-lens reorder reminder, and your website merchandises the optical and sends its exam requests into the same place. Because it is one system, the exam, the eyewear order, and the annual recall sit on the same patient record instead of in three separate places. Patient information is handled with the discretion a medical office is held to, and your number and patient list stay yours if you ever leave.

The pricing is plain. The Signature plan, $99 a month for up to 10 users, includes the AI receptionist and the CRM that answer the phone and work your recall and reorder list. A professional website is $499 one time, and you do not need a plan to buy one. There is no setup fee on any of it. What no honest company can promise is a specific ranking by a specific date, because nobody controls Google, and the AI receptionist sticks to scheduling, order status, and intake, never eye-care or vision advice.
