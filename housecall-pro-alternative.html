Looking for a Housecall Pro Alternative?

Housecall Pro is field-service management software that home-service pros run themselves to schedule jobs, dispatch technicians, invoice, and take payments. Top Shelf is a done-for-you alternative built for local businesses: your website and local SEO, an AI receptionist that answers the phone, a CRM that follows up on every lead, review generation, and marketing, all set up and managed for you on one monthly bill. The real difference is the model. Housecall Pro is software you operate; Top Shelf is a team that runs the front office for you.

What Housecall Pro does well

Housecall Pro earned its reputation on the operational side of a home-service business, and that strength is real. As described on its site at the time of writing, and worth checking there for current details, it handles scheduling and dispatch with a drag-and-drop calendar, quotes and invoices, card and bank payments, GPS fleet tracking, and a mobile app that technicians run from the truck. For an owner who wants to sit in the driver seat and run the day themselves, it is a capable, well-built platform, and it covers a lot of ground across dozens of home-service trades.

It is worth being clear about what kind of tool it is, because that is the honest basis for any comparison. Housecall Pro is self-serve software: you and your office staff sign in, set it up, and operate it, whether you are a one-person shop or a larger team. It has broadened over the years and now offers its own website, call-answering, and review features too. Those are still tools you switch on and manage yourself, which is exactly right for owners who want that control, and exactly the part some owners would rather hand off.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf is built around a different answer to who does the work. Instead of software you operate, it is a done-for-you platform and agency: a team builds your website, runs your local SEO, sets up the CRM, answers your phone, generates reviews, and handles the marketing, then manages all of it for you. You get one monthly bill instead of a stack of logins to keep up with. The AI receptionist answers every call and books the job straight onto your calendar, and the CRM behind it follows up on every lead automatically, so a missed call or a cold quote does not quietly slip past you.

Two things stay true no matter which plan you are on. You own your phone number, your leads, and your data, so what you build stays yours. And Top Shelf is honest about what it is not: it is not field-operations software, so if you want to run dispatch, work orders, and technician scheduling yourself, that is not what this replaces. What it replaces is the pile of front-office work, getting found, answering, following up, and marketing, that an owner rarely has the time to do well alone.

Which one fits your business

The honest way to choose is by what you actually want to own day to day. If your main need is running the job, scheduling crews, dispatching, invoicing, and collecting payment, and you want to operate that software yourself, Housecall Pro is purpose-built for exactly that. If your main need is the opposite, getting found online, having the phone answered, and having every lead followed up and every review requested for you without adding another system to run, then a done-for-you platform is the better fit. Some owners even use both, letting job-management software run the field while Top Shelf runs the front office. Top Shelf offers plans that start at $299 a month with no setup fee, and the honest first step is a free look at where your leads are actually going.
