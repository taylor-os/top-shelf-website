Looking for a Housecall Pro Alternative?

Housecall Pro is field-service management software that home-service pros run themselves to schedule jobs, dispatch technicians, invoice, and take payments. Top Shelf is a right-sized CRM built for local businesses: an AI receptionist that answers the phone and books the job, a CRM that follows up on every lead, missed-call text-back, and review requests, all set up for you on one plan. The real difference is focus. Housecall Pro is built around running the job; Top Shelf is built around catching the call and the lead.

What Housecall Pro does well

Housecall Pro earned its reputation on the operational side of a home-service business, and that strength is real. As described on its site at the time of writing, and worth checking there for current details, it handles scheduling and dispatch with a drag-and-drop calendar, quotes and invoices, card and bank payments, GPS fleet tracking, and a mobile app that technicians run from the truck. For an owner who wants to sit in the driver seat and run the day themselves, it is a capable, well-built platform, and it covers a lot of ground across dozens of home-service trades.

It is worth being clear about what kind of tool it is, because that is the honest basis for any comparison. Housecall Pro is self-serve software: you and your office staff sign in, set it up, and operate it, whether you are a one-person shop or a larger team. It has broadened over the years and now offers its own website, call-answering, and review features too. Those are still tools you switch on and manage yourself, which is exactly right for owners who want that control.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf is built around the front office. It is a right-sized CRM with an AI receptionist built in, and we set it up for you, including the questions the receptionist asks and your booking rules. You get one plan instead of a stack of logins to keep up with. The AI receptionist answers every call and books the job straight onto your calendar, and the CRM behind it follows up on every lead automatically, so a missed call or a cold quote does not quietly slip past you.

Two things stay true either way. You own your phone number, your leads, and your data, so what you build stays yours. And Top Shelf is honest about what it is not: it has a simple dispatch board, but it is not a full field-operations suite, so if you want to run deep work orders and technician scheduling yourself, that is not what this replaces. What it replaces is the pile of front-office work, answering, booking, following up, and asking for reviews, that an owner rarely has the time to do well alone.

Which one fits your business

The honest way to choose is by what you actually want to own day to day. If your main need is running the job, scheduling crews, dispatching, invoicing, and collecting payment, and you want to operate that software yourself, Housecall Pro is purpose-built for exactly that. If your main need is the opposite, having the phone answered, every lead followed up, and every review requested without building it all yourself, then a CRM with an AI receptionist built in is the better fit. Some owners even use both, letting job-management software run the field while Top Shelf runs the front office. The Top Shelf Signature plan starts at $99 a month for up to 10 users, with no setup fee.
