Automation for Real Estate Agents

Automation for real estate agents keeps every active deal on script once it goes under contract, sending milestone updates to buyers and sellers, collecting showing feedback for you, and reviving cold leads on a schedule, so nothing about a transaction depends on you remembering to send a text between showings.

A transaction runs on updates nobody sees you send

Once a home goes under contract, a client wants to know what is happening at every stage, inspection, appraisal, financing, clear to close, even when nothing has actually changed. With two or three deals moving at once, remembering to text each client at each stage is exactly the kind of small task that slips during a busy week, and a client who feels forgotten mid-transaction is the one most likely to complain, even when the deal itself is fully on track.

Every deal, kept on script automatically

Once a transaction hits a milestone, under contract, inspection scheduled, appraisal ordered, financing contingency cleared, clear to close, the client gets an update without you touching your phone. You still make every real decision and every real call. The routine "here is where we stand" text is simply handled for you, on time, on every deal, whether you have one active file or a dozen.

Showing feedback that collects itself

After every showing, a request automatically goes to the agent who brought the buyer, asking what they thought, whether the price felt right, and whether there is real interest. The answers land in one place instead of you personally calling or texting every agent who has come through, and a seller who is anxious after a quiet week gets real feedback faster because you are not the bottleneck collecting it.

A cold lead does not have to stay cold

Not every lead is ready this month. A buyer who went quiet, or a seller who said maybe next year, can resurface automatically on a schedule with a light check-in instead of falling out of your memory entirely. The lead was never dead, it was waiting for someone to reach back out at the right time, and this makes sure that happens without you keeping a mental list. It runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a contact or a note.

How it plays out

A seller who never has to wonder what is happening

A listing goes under contract on a Tuesday. Over the next three weeks the seller gets an automatic text the day the inspection is scheduled, another when the appraisal comes back, and another when financing clears, each one timed to the actual milestone in their file. You never sat down to write any of them, and the seller never had to call and ask where things stood.

Questions Real Estate Agents Ask

Common Questions

01

What milestones does it actually track?

The stages a typical transaction moves through: under contract, inspection, appraisal, financing contingency cleared, and clear to close. Updates go out automatically as each one happens in the file.

02

Does this replace me personally checking in with clients?

No. It handles the routine "here is where things stand" updates so you are not the one remembering to send them. You still make every real decision and step in personally for anything that actually needs a conversation.

03

Can it really collect showing feedback for me?

Yes. A request goes out to the showing agent automatically after each showing, and the responses are compiled in one place instead of you chasing down every agent individually.

04

What happens to leads that have gone quiet?

They get a scheduled check-in instead of being forgotten. You set how long a lead should go untouched before it resurfaces, so old leads keep getting a reason to come back instead of sitting in a list.

05

How long does setup take?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We build the milestone sequences and feedback requests around how you already work, so it is running within days. Start with a free audit and we will show you what is currently falling through during your transactions.
