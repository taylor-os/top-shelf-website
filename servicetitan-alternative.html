ServiceTitan is a powerful, enterprise-grade operations platform built for larger and growing trades businesses that have the team to run it. What ServiceTitan does well

ServiceTitan is a genuinely powerful platform, and it is fair to say so up front. It publicly calls itself the operating system for the trades and all-in-one software for commercial and residential contractors, pulling dispatching, CRM, job costing, reporting, and more into one system. For a larger or fast-growing contractor with a full office team, dispatchers, and managers who work in the software every day, that depth is a real advantage. It is built to scale, with multi-location and enterprise-grade features for companies running many crews across many branches.

That power comes with a shape worth knowing before you commit. A platform this comprehensive is something your business runs, which means people on your side to set it up, learn it, and keep it going day to day. For an established operation with the volume and the staff to put it to work, that is exactly the trade worth making. The question for a smaller business is simply whether you are at that size and stage yet.

Where Top Shelf is different

Top Shelf is a different model, not a smaller version of the same one. Instead of software your team operates, it is a done-for-you platform and agency that is managed for the owner. Top Shelf builds and runs your website and local SEO, answers your phone with an AI receptionist that books the job, runs a CRM that follows up on every lead automatically, and handles your reviews and marketing. It arrives as one flat monthly bill, with no setup fee, and your phone number, your leads, and your customer data always stay yours.

The heart of the difference is who does the work. With a full operations platform, your office runs the system. With Top Shelf, the work of answering calls, following up, and keeping the marketing going is handled for you, so a small business gets the results without hiring the team or learning the software. It is not an enterprise operations suite, and it does not pretend to be one. What it does is take the everyday jobs of a busy local business off the owner's plate.

Which one fits your business

If you run an established or larger operation with the crews, the call volume, and the office staff to run a full operations platform, ServiceTitan is built for exactly that, and it does that job well. The honest question is whether your business is at that size and stage yet. If you are a small local business whose real problem is a phone that goes unanswered, a website that does not bring in work, and leads that never get a second touch, a comprehensive platform you have to run yourself is usually more tool than you need. Top Shelf is right-sized for that business: the essentials handled for you, on a flat bill, with no heavy software to staff. You can see exactly what each plan includes and decide from there.
