Your happy clients rebook only if you reach them

Most med spas do not have a new-client problem so much as a rebooking problem. Botox wears off in a few months. Filler softens over the year. Laser and body contouring are sold as packages of several sessions. A client walks out thrilled and fully meaning to come back, then life gets busy, no reminder ever comes, and she resurfaces months later at whatever spa ran a promo that week, if she comes back at all. The treatment was always going to repeat. It just needed a well-timed nudge, and that is the thing a full front desk never gets around to.

A CRM keeps every client and their full treatment history and cycle in front of you and sends the reminder for you, on the schedule you set, whether or not anyone at the desk remembers. The client whose tox is about to fade hears from you right on time and rebooks, instead of forgetting you until some other spa runs the ad that reminds her she is overdue.

Every treatment runs on a cycle you can plan around

Aesthetic clients are the most predictable repeat business there is, if someone is tracking it. You know roughly when tox needs a touch-up, when a filler client is due for a refresh, when a package still has sessions on it, and when a membership is about to renew. But no one can hold the schedules of a few thousand clients in their head, so most of that built-in repeat revenue slips away unless something is watching the dates for you.

  • Every client, their treatments, products, notes, and consent history live in one place instead of scattered across paper charts, a booking app, and what the front desk happens to remember.
  • Reminders fire on the cycle each treatment runs on, a tox touch-up, a filler refresh, the next session in a package, so the rebooking happens without anyone tracking dates.
  • Memberships and packages are tracked, so you can see who still has sessions to use and who is due to renew, and reach them before the benefit lapses.
  • You can see who has not been in for a while and send the right client the right reason to come back at the right moment.

The client who drifted off is still yours to win back

A client who has not been in for eight months is not being disloyal. She got busy, no reminder came, and your name slipped her mind. A light, well-timed touch, a note that she is due, a quick check-in, a reason to come back this month, puts you back in front of her before some other spa promo does. It costs almost nothing, and it is the difference between a client who came in twice and one who stays with you for years of treatments. Reactivating the clients you already earned is the cheapest growth a med spa has.

Fewer no-shows, and a client list you actually keep

A no-show on a booked treatment is a room and an hour you cannot get back, so the CRM sends confirmations and reminders that keep your chairs full and give clients an easy way to reschedule instead of simply vanishing. Every client, note, and treatment record is yours and exportable any time, not locked inside software you rent. The CRM is one piece of the Top Shelf platform: it connects to the AI receptionist, so a consult it answers lands in your database and gets followed up on, and to online booking, so every visit is logged against the right client with their full history already attached.

How it plays out

The Botox client who rebooks on time

A client comes in for Botox in March, loves the result, and fully means to come back before it fades. Normally that is where it ends, until she notices in July that it has worn off and books wherever she happens to see an ad. Instead, the CRM knows her cycle and sends a friendly note in early June that she is coming due, written to sound like your spa, with a link to book. She rebooks for the second week of June without ever letting the result fully fade, and she is back on your calendar before she would have thought to look elsewhere. No one at the desk had to remember.

Questions Med Spas Ask

Common Questions

01

Does it import my existing clients and treatment history?

Yes. Your current clients, their treatment history, packages, and notes come in and live in one place, and everything stays yours and exportable. The point is to make the client list you already have actually work for you.

02

Will it really remind clients when their next treatment is due?

Yes, on the cycle each treatment runs on and the schedule you approve. A tox client hears from you as she comes due, a package client gets nudged to use the sessions she paid for, and a member is reminded before benefits reset, all sent for you. You can step in and message anyone yourself any time.

03

Can it track memberships and packages?

Yes. It tracks who still has sessions to use, who is due to renew, and who has quietly fallen behind on a membership they are paying for, so you can reach them before the value goes unused and a member cancels.

04

How is this different from the booking tool I already use?

A booking calendar records appointments. It does not watch treatment cycles, follow up on a client who drifted off, flag a package that is going unused, or reactivate someone you have not seen in a year. The CRM does all of that on a schedule, so the repeat revenue actually shows up instead of depending on memory.

05

How long until it is set up?

Setup is included with no separate onboarding fee. We bring in your clients, build your reminder and reactivation sequences around your real treatment cycles, and connect it to your calls and calendar, so it is working in days.
