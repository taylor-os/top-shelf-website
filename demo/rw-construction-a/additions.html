Additions

Sometimes the house you have is the right house. It just needs more of it.

More room without moving

An addition or a major remodel lets you stay in the neighborhood you chose and still get the space you need. We plan it around how you live and how the existing home is built, so the new part feels like it was always there.

When a project needs drawings, permits or engineering, our planning and architecture service takes care of it, including 3D renderings so you can see the space before it's built.

The same crew handles the finish work: kitchens, bathrooms, whole-home updates and the details that tie old and new together.

  • Bathrooms
  • Whole-home remodels
  • Guest bath to full bath conversions
