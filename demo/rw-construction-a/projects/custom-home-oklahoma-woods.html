A custom home in the Oklahoma woods

Not every build starts on a city lot. This one is a custom home on a wooded property in Oklahoma, and it's proof of something we tell every client: we'll build wherever you are.

Category
New Builds
Posted
November 2024
Status
Framing in progress

Where it started

A remote site in the woods, with natural, uneven ground and trees on every side.

The plan

Put a home in the woods without bulldozing the woods. We prepped the site with as little disruption to the surrounding land as we could manage.

How we're building it

The ground isn't flat, so the house sits on an elevated foundation. A concrete foundation carries elevated wood framing, which lets the structure work with the slope instead of fighting it.

Where it stands

When we shared this update, the foundation was in and framing was in progress. We'll post more as the house goes up.
