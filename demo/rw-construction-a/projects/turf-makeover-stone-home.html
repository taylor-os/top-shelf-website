A turf makeover beside a stone home

A stone home deserves a yard to match. This one got a full turf makeover.

Category
Landscaping

Where it started

Ground beside the home that needed grading, structure and a finished surface.

How we built it

We graded the yard so water drains the right way, then laid premium artificial turf. Custom flagstone paths are set right into the turf, and we cleaned up and refined the existing shrubs and tree lines.

The result

A rock garden with water features gives the yard a natural focal point. Because it's turf, it stays green all year.
