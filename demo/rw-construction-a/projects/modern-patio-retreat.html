A modern patio retreat

A simple path through the yard, turned into a modern patio retreat.

Category
Outdoor Living

Where it started

A plain path and not much reason to stop on it.

The plan

A clean, modern place to sit, with texture underfoot and some privacy.

How we built it

We poured concrete pads with a salt finish and set Texas Black gravel between them, which gives the patio its crisp grid. A nearby water feature adds sound and movement.

The result

Greenery around the edges adds privacy, so the patio feels tucked away from everything else.
