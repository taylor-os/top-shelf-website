New builds

Custom homes from the ground up. We'll build wherever you are.

Starting from bare ground

A new home starts long before the framing goes up. The site has to be prepared, the foundation has to suit the ground, and the plans have to be right.

Our current build is a custom home on a wooded property in Oklahoma. The ground there isn't flat, so the house sits on a concrete foundation with elevated wood framing, and the site was prepped with as little disruption to the trees as possible. If your lot is somewhere unusual, that's fine with us.

Need plans first? Our planning and architecture service covers concepts, blueprints, permits and 3D renderings.

What a new build includes

  • Site preparation
  • Foundations
  • Framing
  • Plans, permits and renderings
