Remodels

Kitchens, bathrooms, whole homes and the small upgrades that change how a house feels.

Make the house you have work better

A good remodel starts with how you actually use the space. Maybe the kitchen is too tight, the guest bath never got finished, or the front door doesn't match the rest of the house.

We handle kitchens, bathrooms and whole-home remodels, plus the details that make a big difference, like turning a guest bath into a full bath or giving an entry a new door and trim. Sometimes the job is something you didn't know was possible, like the wine cellar we built out of a water-heater closet.
