Who we are

RW Construction is led by Rance Walters and Crystal Kenealy: a builder who has spent his career working outdoors and in, and a designer who knows what a property is worth.

That pairing shapes every job. Rance keeps the work hands-on. Crystal makes sure the finished space looks right and makes sense for the property. It works for a backyard in Dallas, and it works for a house in the Oklahoma woods.
