About us

Rance Walters and Crystal Kenealy bring different backgrounds to the same job. One builds, and the other designs and knows the property market.

Built on two kinds of experience

RW Construction handles residential and commercial work: remodels, new builds, outdoor living, landscaping, commercial projects, and planning and architecture. The team has more than 20 years of experience and more than 1,000 completed projects behind it.

The company runs on the mix at the top. Rance brings a builder's instincts from years of hands-on work. Crystal brings a designer's eye and a real understanding of the property market. Between them, a project gets built well and makes sense as an investment.

We also go where the work is. One of our recent builds is a custom home in the Oklahoma woods, so if your land is a little off the beaten path, call us anyway.

Meet the team

Rance Walters

Leads the team

Rance is originally from Mississippi. His career in landscaping runs from small residential yards all the way to large-scale projects, including work for the Texas Rangers.

He's a hands-on builder, and he's as comfortable on an interior remodel as he is out in the yard. When he's off the clock, you'll find him with his two sons or on a golf course.

Crystal Kenealy

Design and real estate

Crystal grew up in Indiana. Before RW Construction, she was a Senior Project Manager for Fossil's jewelry division.

She pairs an eye for design with a solid understanding of the real estate market, and she helps clients make sure their projects line up with their property-investment goals. Away from work, she spends time with her daughter, enjoys being festive and loves dinner out with friends.
