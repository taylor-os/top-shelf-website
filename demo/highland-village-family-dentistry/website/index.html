What we treat

Ten treatments, grouped the way a visit actually goes.

Some of it is keeping teeth out of trouble. Some of it is fixing what has already gone wrong, and some of it is about how a smile looks. Every treatment below opens.

Everyday care

The visits that keep a mouth healthy, plus the ones nobody puts in the calendar on purpose.

Four services

  • The routine visit that catches problems while they are still small.

  • Pain, a break, or something that came loose and cannot wait.

  • First visits, sealants and check-ups, at a pace a child can cope with.

  • For patients who find the chair harder than the treatment.

Repair and replace

What to do about a tooth that is damaged, worn, or already gone.

Three services

  • The crown is milled and fitted in one visit, which spares you a temporary and a second trip.

  • A permanent replacement rooted in the jaw, for a tooth that cannot be saved.

  • Implants anchor the denture, so it stays put.

Look and alignment

Work aimed at how teeth look and how they line up, rather than only how they function.

Three services

  • Changing how teeth look rather than only how they work.

  • Moving teeth into line, for adults as well as teenagers.

  • Whitening done in the practice, under supervision, rather than off the pharmacy shelf.
