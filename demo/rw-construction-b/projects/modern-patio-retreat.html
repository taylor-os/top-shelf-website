Outdoor Living

Modern patio retreat

A plain path became a grid of poured concrete pads set in black gravel.

Type
Patio + hardscape

The challenge

A simple walkway through the yard that wasn't doing much for anyone.

What we built

We replaced it with salt-finish poured concrete pads, with Texas Black gravel between them. A water feature sits close by, and new greenery around the edges gives the space some privacy.

The result

A clean, modern place to sit outside that feels tucked away from the neighbors.

Spec

  • Salt-finish poured concrete
  • Texas Black gravel
  • Nearby water feature
  • Privacy greenery
