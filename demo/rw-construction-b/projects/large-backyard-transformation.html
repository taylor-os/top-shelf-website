Landscaping

From bare to beautiful: a big backyard

A large backyard that was mostly bare dirt, now turfed, planted and laid out with gravel paths.

Type
Full backyard landscape

The challenge

A big yard with almost nothing in it. Bare ground, no paths and no plan.

What we built

We cleared and leveled the whole yard and laid artificial turf. Crushed-gravel paths with black edging run through it. Then came trees, evergreens, shrubs and accent plants, plus irrigation and drainage to keep everything healthy.

The result

A finished backyard from fence to fence, planted and ready to use.

Spec

  • Cleared + leveled
  • Artificial turf
  • Crushed-gravel paths
  • Black edging
  • Trees, evergreens + shrubs
  • Irrigation + drainage
