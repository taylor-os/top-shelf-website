Custom home in the Oklahoma woods

A custom home going up on a wooded lot in Oklahoma. It's a long drive from Dallas, and it's where the client chose to build.

Type
New custom home
Where
Oklahoma
Posted
November 2024
Status
Framing in progress

The challenge

The lot was remote, thick with trees and far from flat. The house had to fit the land without clear-cutting the reason the client bought it.

What we built

We prepped the site while disturbing as little of the surrounding woods as we could. The concrete foundation went in first. On top of it we raised an elevated foundation that follows the natural slope, then started the elevated wood framing.

The result

The framing is still in progress, and these photos were taken mid-build. We'll travel for the right project, and this one shows it.
