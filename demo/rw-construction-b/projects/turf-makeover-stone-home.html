Landscaping

Turf makeover beside a stone home

Drainage fixed, turf down, flagstone set in, and a yard that finally matches the stone house it sits beside.

Type
Landscaping + drainage

The challenge

The yard around this stone home needed to drain properly and needed a finish that lived up to the house.

What we built

We graded the yard for drainage, then laid premium artificial turf and set custom flagstone paths into it. The shrubs and tree lines were cleaned up and reshaped, and a rock garden with water features went in.

The result

The yard drains properly now, and the turf and flagstone finally suit the stone house.

Spec

  • Graded for drainage
  • Premium artificial turf
  • Custom flagstone paths
  • Refined shrubs + tree lines
  • Rock garden
  • Water features
