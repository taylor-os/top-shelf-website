Remodel

Guest bath, gold and marble

A guest bath that went from a bare pedestal sink to a room with real storage and some real personality.

Type
Bathroom remodel
Room
Guest bath

The challenge

A standalone pedestal sink, no storage to speak of and nothing on the wall worth a second look.

What we built

We pulled the pedestal and built a custom three-drawer vanity with a marble top. A textured gold vessel sink and gold faucet sit on the marble, with gold handles to match. Behind it, a curved custom backsplash and an ornate gold mirror.

The result

Drawers where there were none, and a small room that now carries itself like a bigger one.

Spec

  • Custom 3-drawer vanity
  • Marble top
  • Textured gold vessel sink
  • Gold faucet + handles
  • Curved custom backsplash
  • Ornate gold mirror
