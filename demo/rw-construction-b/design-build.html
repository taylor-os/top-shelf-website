Planning & architecture

Good builds are decided on paper. Our five-step planning process takes a project from rough idea to permitted drawings and a 3D preview, before anyone breaks ground.

Free consultation See the work

Five steps, in order

The steps run in order.

  1. 01

    Concept development

    We start with what the space has to do and how you want it to feel, then sketch until the idea is clear enough to draw properly.

  2. 02

    Custom designs + blueprints

    The concept becomes custom architectural drawings: real plans, real dimensions, drawn for your site.

  3. 03

    Permit assistance

    We help with the permit paperwork and check the design against building code, so approvals aren't something you figure out alone.

  4. 04

    Collaboration

    Engineers, designers and contractors each get the same plan, and we keep them coordinated so the build matches the drawings.

  5. 05

    3D modeling + renderings

    Before construction starts, you see the space in 3D and in renderings. It's the cheapest moment to change your mind.

For homes and businesses

The same process runs for a backyard, an addition, a new custom home or a commercial tenant build-out. The size changes, the order doesn't.
