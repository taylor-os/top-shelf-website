Our story

A family business since 1985.

Scrubz Carpet Care is a local cleaning and restoration company. The family has owned and run it since 1985.

Owner David Keith Mayfield has more than 30 years in the trade. Customer service is our top priority, and the motto covers the rest: we're old skool, and we do it right.

Carpet is the core of what we do. We also clean upholstery, tile and grout, air ducts and dryer vents, and we run a 24-hour emergency line for floods and spills.
