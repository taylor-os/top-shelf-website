What we do

Six reasons to stop by.

Want to check on something before you make the trip? Call the shop and ask.

  1. 01

    Bikes

    Come look at bikes in person. Stand over one and squeeze the brakes. Ask us whatever you want.

  2. 02

    Used bikes

    We sell used bikes too. Call and ask what's on the floor right now.

  3. 03

    Rentals

    Need a bike for a while and not forever? Call and ask what's available to rent.

  4. 04

    Repairs

    A flat, a rattle, a squeal, a shifter with a mind of its own. Bring it in and we'll take a look.

  5. 05

    Bike wash

    Roll in dirty. We wash bikes here, so yours doesn't have to go home that way.

  6. 06

    Coffee

    Yes, there's coffee. It goes well with talking about bikes.
