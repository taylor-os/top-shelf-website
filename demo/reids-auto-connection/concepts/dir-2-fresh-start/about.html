Our story

A second chance, done right.

Reids Auto Connection didn't start as one more lot on the corner. It started because too many good, hard-working people were being turned away over a single hard chapter — a bankruptcy, a repossession, a credit score that had stopped telling the truth about who they are.

Founder Reid M. Anderson — a veteran who knows firsthand what it means to rebuild — opened these doors in 2009 with one rule that hasn't moved since: treat every person who walks in like they matter, because they do. All these years later, we're still veteran-owned, still BBB A+ accredited, and still doing the one thing we set out to do.

A note from the owner

Why I do this.

I've watched too many good people get told no by folks who never looked past a number on a screen.

When I got out of the service and started this dealership in 2009, I made myself a promise: nobody would ever leave my lot feeling smaller than when they walked in. Life humbles all of us — a job loss, a medical bill, a divorce, a bankruptcy you never saw coming. None of that makes you a bad bet. It just means you had a hard chapter.

So we built the Fresh Start Vehicle Program around one idea: meet people where they are, tell them the truth, and get them into a reliable car they can actually afford — with $0 down on most loans and no games about the price. When you make your payments, most of our loans report to the credit bureaus, so the car that got you moving also helps you rebuild.

I'm not interested in the biggest lot around. I'm interested in the person shaking my hand, driving off with their kids in the back seat, feeling like themselves again. That's the whole job. That's why I still show up every day.

— Reid M. Anderson, Owner Veteran · Founder of Reids Auto Connection, est. 2009
