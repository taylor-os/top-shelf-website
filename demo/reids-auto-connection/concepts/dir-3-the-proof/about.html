A note from the owner

Why I still show up.

"I've watched too many good people get told 'no' by folks who never looked past a number on a screen."

When I came home from the service and started this dealership in 2009, I made myself a promise: nobody would leave my lot feeling smaller than when they walked in. Life humbles all of us — a job loss, a medical bill, a divorce, a bankruptcy you never saw coming. None of that makes you a bad bet. It means you had a hard chapter.

So we built the Fresh Start Vehicle Program around one idea: meet people where they are, tell them the truth, and get them into a reliable car they can actually afford — with $0 down on most loans and no games about the price. When you make your payments, most of our loans report to the credit bureaus, so the car that got you moving also helps you rebuild.

I'm not interested in being the biggest lot around. I'm interested in the person shaking my hand, driving off with their kids in the back seat, feeling like themselves again. That's the whole job. That's why I still show up every day.

R Reid M. Anderson, Owner Veteran · Founder of Reids Auto Connection, est. 2009
