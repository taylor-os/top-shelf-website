Straight Answers

Fresh Start financing questions

Can I get approved during an active bankruptcy?+

Yes. We finance buyers in both Chapter 7 and Chapter 13, whether the case is discharged or still open. If your Chapter 13 plan requires it, we help coordinate trustee approval so the loan fits — you don't have to figure that part out alone.

How much do I need to put down?+

Most loans are $0 down. The only amounts you'll owe out of pocket are state tax, title & license, which every dealer is required to collect. We'll tell you your exact numbers before you sign — no surprises.

Will checking hurt my credit?+

No. Seeing what you qualify for is a soft look that won't affect your score, and you don't need to give a Social Security number to get a first answer. You only move to a full application once you're ready.

What should I bring to finish?+

To wrap up, bring a valid driver's license, proof of income (recent pay stubs or bank statements), proof of residence (a utility bill or lease), and a few references. If you're in a Chapter 13, bring your plan details too. Not sure? Call and we'll tell you exactly what applies to you.

Can I buy if I'm out of state?+

Give us a call. We serve buyers nationwide — and we're happy to talk through the details wherever you're coming from. The fastest way to know for sure is to reach a real person at (972) 436-1600.
