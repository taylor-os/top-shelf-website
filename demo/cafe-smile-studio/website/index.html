What the practice does

Eight treatments, from the appointment you book every six months to the work that takes several visits to plan.

Checkup and cleaning

The regular visit. An exam, a scale and polish, and an honest answer about anything that needs watching.

Dental emergency

A broken tooth, a lost crown, or pain that will not wait for a scheduled appointment.

Smile aesthetics

Cosmetic work planned around your face rather than a catalogue, so the result still looks like you.

Smile makeovers

Several treatments planned together as one result, rather than fixed one tooth at a time.

Veneers

Thin porcelain facings bonded to the front of a tooth to change its shape, shade or alignment.

Implants

A titanium post placed in the jaw to carry a replacement tooth that stays put.

Invisalign

Clear removable aligners that straighten teeth without brackets or wires.

Botox

Administered in a dental setting, where the muscles of the jaw and face are already part of the day's work.

About the practice

Cafe Smile Studio is a dental practice on Justin Road in Highland Village. The checkups and cleanings keep a mouth out of trouble. The cosmetic side is there when somebody wants a change they can see. And then there are the emergencies, which nobody schedules.
