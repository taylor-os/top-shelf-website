About this home

Stone, stucco and a tile roof give this single-story home a warm, Hill Country look from the street, and the front porch is deep enough for a pair of rocking chairs. Inside, arched doorways lead to a living room with a stone fireplace and windows onto the backyard.

The kitchen has cream cabinets, granite counters, a stone backsplash and an island with seating, plus a breakfast nook in the bay window. Off the entry, a sunny flex room with a built-in window seat works as an office, a reading room or a hobby space.

The primary bedroom sits at the back of the house, and its bath has a garden tub under an arched window and a separate walk-in shower. Out back, a cedar pergola covers a flagstone patio with room to eat outside, and raised garden beds run along the fence.
