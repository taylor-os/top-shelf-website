Meet Sarah

Three decades of caring for people. Now she helps them move.

Sarah was born and raised in Dallas and earned her nursing degree at Dallas Baptist University. She spent most of a 30-year career in pediatrics. When she retired, she got her real estate license and started helping her neighbors in Robson Ranch buy and sell.

More than 75 homes later, she still does the job the way she did nursing: she listens first and explains things plainly, and she stays with you until it's done.
