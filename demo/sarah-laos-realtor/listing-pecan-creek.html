About this home

A big pecan tree shades the back of this red brick home, which makes a Texas summer a lot easier. The family room has a painted brick fireplace, crown molding and a wall of windows facing the yard.

The kitchen was refreshed with sage green lower cabinets, white quartz counters and subway tile, and it opens to a breakfast area. The sunroom on the back of the house gets light from three sides and is a good spot for morning coffee.

Two bedrooms and two full baths keep things simple. The primary bedroom has plantation shutters, and its bath has a white double vanity and a tiled tub and shower.
