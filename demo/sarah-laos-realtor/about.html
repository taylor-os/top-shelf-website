Her story

From pediatrics to real estate.

Sarah Laos was born and raised in Dallas, and stayed there until she moved to Robson Ranch in Denton many years ago.

She earned a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Dallas Baptist University and spent most of her 30-year nursing career working in different areas of pediatrics. When she retired from nursing, she decided to start a second career in real estate, with a focus on the community she already called home.

She has now been a licensed Realtor for more than ten years, working across the Dallas-Fort Worth area and especially in Robson Ranch. She has sold more than 75 homes.

Sarah recently joined Ready Real Estate, based in Dallas. Her daughter and son-in-law live in the Dallas area too.

Ask her why she does this work and the answer is simple: she loves helping her clients with all their real estate needs.
