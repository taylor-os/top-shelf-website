Robson Ranch, Denton, Texas.

An active-adult golf community on the edge of Denton, and the place Sarah has called home for years.

Why it's different

Golf-course views and real neighbors.

People come to Robson Ranch for the golf and the easier pace, and there's plenty going on when you want company.

Homes here vary a lot by floor plan, lot and view. Two houses on the same street can sell for very different prices. That's where living here helps. Sarah has walked these streets for years and can tell you which views, layouts and lots buyers pay more for.

Questions

Robson Ranch FAQ.

Is Robson Ranch an age-qualified community?

Yes. Robson Ranch is an active-adult community, and the community sets its own rules about who can buy and live there. Sarah will walk you through the current requirements before you make an offer.

Are there HOA dues?

Yes. Dues and what they cover are set by the association and can change, so Sarah will get you the current figures for any home you're considering.

Can Sarah help me sell a home outside Robson Ranch?

Yes. Sarah works across Dallas-Fort Worth. If you're selling elsewhere in the area and buying in Robson Ranch, she can coordinate both sides.

How do I find out what my Robson Ranch home is worth?

Ask Sarah for a free market analysis. She'll look at recent sales of similar floor plans, lots and views and give you a realistic price range.
