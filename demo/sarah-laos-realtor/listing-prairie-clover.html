About this home

Built in 2021, this modern farmhouse has white brick, black windows and a cedar-post porch facing a front yard planted with native grasses that don't need much water. The living room has a vaulted ceiling, a shiplap fireplace wall and white oak floors that run through the main rooms.

In the kitchen, a matte black island with a white oak top sits between white cabinets, quartz counters, open shelving and a farmhouse sink under the window. A home office behind black glass French doors stays quiet without feeling closed off.

Each of the three bedrooms has its own full bath. The primary bath has a walk-in zellige shower and a white oak double vanity, and the covered back porch has a cedar ceiling and room for a long table.
