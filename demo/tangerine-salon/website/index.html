About

A place to exhale

Tangerine Salon is an Aveda Certified salon in The Shops at Highland Village, one of five Tangerine locations across the Metroplex. Color is most of what happens here: highlights, balayage, all over color, and Bellami hand-tied extensions. Every appointment starts with a consultation, because the answer for your hair is not the answer for the person in the next chair.
