The lab and the X-ray are down the hall.

Bloodwork and imaging are handled in house instead of being sent out to an outside lab.

In house lab work

CBC, chemistry, heartworm testing, and FIV/FeLV testing all run here in the building rather than going out to a third party lab.

Digital radiology

Digital X-ray in the clinic, so imaging happens during the visit.

Early detection testing

Screening that catches changes in older pets before they turn into symptoms you can see.

Veterinary chiropractor

We partner with a veterinary chiropractor, available Mondays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM by appointment.

Surgery and orthopedics

Spay and neuter, dental procedures, and orthopedic surgery for dogs and cats.

Nutritional counseling

Diet guidance, including help choosing food as your pet's needs change.
