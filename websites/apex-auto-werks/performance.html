The approach

Every build starts and ends on the dyno.

We baseline your car so we know exactly what it makes before we touch it. Then we build to a plan and confirm the result on the same dyno, so the numbers are real and the car is safe.

  • Baseline and final dyno pulls, documented
  • Air-fuel and knock monitored the whole way
  • Tuned for the fuel you actually run
How a build works

From first call to final pull.

Consult

We talk through how you drive, what you want, and a realistic budget. No fantasy builds, no wasted money.

Baseline

Your car goes on the dyno as-is so we have honest numbers to build from and to measure against.

Build

Parts go on in the right order, installed properly, with everything torqued and checked to spec.

Retune & verify

We tune, confirm on the dyno, then hand you the sheet. You leave knowing exactly what your car does.
