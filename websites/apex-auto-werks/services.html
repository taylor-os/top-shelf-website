Home / Services

Service done to the standard your car was built to.

Every make we touch gets factory-level tools, factory-grade parts, and a written plan you approve before we start. Here's what we handle.

Scheduled Maintenance

The routine service that quietly saves you thousands: oil and filters, fluids, brakes, inspections, all on the factory schedule.

  • Factory-interval oil and fluid service
  • Brake, belt and inspection intervals
  • Records kept to protect your warranty

Diagnostics & Repair

A check-engine light is a starting point, not an answer. We read it with factory tools and test until we know the real cause.

  • Factory-level scan and coding tools
  • Real diagnosis, not parts-cannon guessing
  • Photos and findings sent to your phone

Brakes & Suspension

The parts that decide how your car stops and corners. Pads, rotors, bushings, shocks and alignment, all set to spec.

  • OE and performance brake options
  • Struts, control arms and bushings
  • Precision alignment on every job

Engine & Transmission

The jobs other shops send away: timing chains, oil leaks, clutches, cooling systems and transmission service, done in-house.

  • Timing chains, guides and tensioners
  • Cooling, gaskets and oil-leak repair
  • Clutch and transmission service

Electrical & Electronics

Modern European cars are computers on wheels. We chase down electrical faults and code new parts the way the factory does.

  • Battery, charging and starting faults
  • Module coding and programming
  • Sensor and wiring diagnostics

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Thinking about a used European car? Bring it here first. We put it on the lift and give you the full story before you buy.

  • Full lift and scan inspection
  • Written report with photos
  • An honest buy or walk-away call
How a visit works

Four steps. No surprises.

Digital inspection

Clear estimate

You get a written estimate, prioritized into what's urgent, what's soon, and what can wait.

Your approval

Nothing gets done until you say yes. No mystery charges added at the counter.

Done right

The work is completed to spec and backed by our warranty. You drive away knowing exactly what was done.

Warranty-safe

Independent service that keeps your warranty intact.

By law, having your car serviced here does not void your factory warranty. We use quality parts and keep detailed records, so your coverage stays exactly where it should: protecting you.

  • Factory-schedule service, fully documented
  • OE and OE-grade parts and fluids
  • Loaner cars so you're never stuck
Honest pricing

You'll never wonder what you paid for.

We quote before we start, we explain what each item is, and we tell you what can wait. The goal is a car that lasts and a shop you trust, not the biggest ticket this visit.
