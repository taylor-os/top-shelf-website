## Identity
- **Name:** Brightwater Dental · **Type:** modern family + cosmetic dentistry.
- **Tagline:** *Care you'll look forward to.*
- **One-liner:** Gentle, modern dentistry for the whole family, with none of the dread.
- **Locale (fictional):** Lakeside &amp; Cedar Hills.
- **Positioning:** Calm, judgment-free, transparent. Anxiety-friendly care, same-day crowns, modern digital tech, most insurance accepted plus an in-house membership plan for the uninsured. Kids welcome. Evening and Saturday hours.

## Voice
Warm, calm, plain-spoken, reassuring. Zero fear-mongering, zero jargon. Talks to a nervous patient like a friend who happens to be a great dentist.

## Theme / art direction
- **World:** bright, airy, clean, calming. The opposite of a cold clinical office. Feels like a spa that happens to fix teeth.
- **Palette:** white and soft mint grounds; **teal/aqua** `#17B0B8` + deep teal `#0E7C86` primary; sky `#EAF6F7` tints; warm ink `#123138` text; coral `#FF7A66` warm accent used sparingly.
- **Type:** friendly geometric sans (Manrope / DM Sans) headings, humanist sans (Inter) body. Rounded corners, soft shadows, lots of whitespace, gentle motion.
- **Motion:** Lenis smooth scroll; soft fade-up reveals; a calm split hero (no dark full-bleed); gentle hover lifts; count-up trust stats. Nothing aggressive. Reduced-motion respected.

## Services (content)
- **General &amp; Preventive:** exams, cleanings, digital x-rays, fillings, gum care, night guards.
- **Cosmetic:** professional whitening, porcelain veneers, bonding, smile makeovers.
- **Clear Aligners:** discreet, custom aligners to straighten teeth without metal braces.
- **Dental Implants:** natural-looking permanent tooth replacement, start to finish in one place.
- **Same-Day Crowns:** digitally designed and milled in a single visit.
- **Kids &amp; Family:** gentle first visits, sealants, and a team kids actually like.
- **Emergency Dentistry:** same-day relief for pain, breaks, and knocked-out teeth.

## Proof (fictional, believable)
- Rated 4.9 across 1,200+ reviews · gentle, anxiety-friendly · most PPO insurance accepted · Brightwater Membership from a low monthly price for the uninsured · modern tech (digital scans, same-day crowns) · evening &amp; Saturday appointments.
- Reassurances: transparent pricing before treatment, no judgment, no pressure, financing available.
