Good to know

New patient questions.

I have dental anxiety. Can you help?

Absolutely, it's one of the things we do best. We offer comfort options from blankets and headphones to sedation, we explain everything before we do it, and we never make you feel bad for being nervous or for time between visits.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Just your ID and your insurance card if you have one. Arrive a few minutes early to fill out a short health history, or complete it online ahead of time and skip the clipboard.

Do you see children?

Yes. We're a family practice and love treating kids. We keep first visits gentle and fun so the dentist becomes a good memory, not a scary one.

What if I haven't been to a dentist in years?

You're in good company, and there's no lecture waiting for you. We'll start where you are, focus on what matters most, and build a plan you're comfortable with.

Do you take dental emergencies?

Yes. Pain, chips, breaks, and knocked-out teeth get same-day priority. Call us and we'll get you seen and comfortable quickly.
