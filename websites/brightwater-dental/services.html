General & Preventive

The care that keeps everything else away.

Most dental problems are cheaper and easier to fix when they're caught early. Regular cleanings and exams are the whole game, and we make them quick, comfortable, and honest.

  • Exams & digital x-rays
  • Gentle cleanings
  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Gum health & deep cleaning
  • Night guards for grinding
  • Oral cancer screening

Cosmetic Dentistry

A brighter smile that still looks like you.

We're not in the business of blinding-white, all-the-same veneers. We shape cosmetic work to your face and your goals, so the result looks natural and the compliments are about you, not your teeth.

  • Professional whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Full smile makeovers

Clear Aligners

Straighter teeth, no one has to know.

Custom clear aligners gradually straighten your teeth without metal brackets or wires. We map the whole plan digitally on day one, so you can see the finish line before you start.

  • Discreet, removable trays
  • Digital preview of your result
  • Fewer visits than braces
  • Flexible monthly payment plans
