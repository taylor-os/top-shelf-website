Meet the team

The people who make the dentist easy.

A small, friendly team that takes the time to know you. No revolving door of strangers, just people who genuinely like what they do.

Our practice

Founded on a simple idea.

Dr. Maya Ellison opened Brightwater after years of watching patients dread a visit that didn't have to be scary. She built a practice around comfort, honesty, and taking the time to explain, and hired a team that believes the same thing.

Today we care for families all over Lakeside and Cedar Hills, from first baby teeth to full smile makeovers, with the same gentle hands and straight answers.

The office

A calm space, quietly high-tech.

Our office is designed to feel more like a calm waiting room than a clinic, warm light, quiet corners, and comfortable chairs. Behind the scenes, digital scanners and same-day crown milling mean less time in the chair and fewer trips back.

  • Digital scans instead of messy molds
  • Same-day crowns milled in-house
  • Comfort options at every visit
