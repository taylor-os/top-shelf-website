How we see a dent

A dent bends the light. We bend it back.

  1. 01
    Light board upA striped light reflects off the panel. Wherever the lines bend, the metal is out of place.
  2. 02
    Work it from behindSteel rods slide in behind the panel and push the low spots up, a little at a time.
  3. 03
    Lines run straightWhen the reflection is straight again, the dent is gone. It's still your original paint, with no filler underneath.
