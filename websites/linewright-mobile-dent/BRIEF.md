## Identity
- **Name:** Linewright Mobile Dent Co. · **Type:** mobile paintless dent repair + paint/finish repair. No shop, no storefront, no posted hours — booked by appointment, done where the car is parked.
- **Tagline:** *Every line, straight again.*
- **Concept:** a PDR tech reads a dent through the reflection of a striped light board — a dent bends the reflected lines; a finished repair makes them straight. The whole brand world is reflected light lines on paint.
- **Locale (fictional):** the Harlow Valley metro (coined towns below). Service area only, never an address.
- **Established:** 2014 (fictional).

## Voice
Calm, precise, a little proud of the craft. Talks like a tech who shows you the dent under the light before quoting it. Honest about what PDR can't fix (cracked paint → we say so and quote paint work instead). No hype.

## Theme / art direction
- **Palette:** ink `#0A0E14` / graphite `#121821` grounds; chrome text `#E9EDF2`, muted `#97A1AE`; one accent **line-lime** `#CCFF3D` (the "straight line" colour); light sections on paper `#F2F3EF` with ink text.
- **Type:** Archivo (expanded width, 800) for headlines; IBM Plex Sans body; IBM Plex Mono for labels/specs.
- **Signature motion:** a canvas **reflection-line field** — parallel stripes warped by a dent that straightens as you scroll (hero) and dents under the cursor then heals. Also: before/after drag sliders, an interactive car "where's the damage?" hotspot picker, falling-hail canvas on the hail page, count-ups, marquee, Lenis + GSAP. Reduced-motion respected; everything readable with JS off.

## Services (9)
1. **Paintless Dent Repair** — door dings, creases, larger dents; factory paint stays.
2. **Hail Damage Repair** — PDR for hail, insurance-claim help, direct estimates.
3. **Dent + Paint Repair** — when the paint is cracked: pull, fill, spot refinish the panel.
4. **Clear Coat Repair** — peeling/failing clear, deep scratches, spot refinish and blend.
5. **Paint Correction** — multi-stage machine polish, swirls, haze, water spots.
6. **Bumper Scuff & Scratch Repair** — small-area plastic repair and refinish.
7. **Wheel Curb Rash Repair** — scraped rims refinished on the car.
8. **Headlight Restoration** — yellow, hazy lenses restored and UV-sealed.
9. **Ceramic Coating & Paint Protection Film** — protect the finish after the repair.
Plus audiences: **Fleet & dealer** wholesale, **lease-return prep**.
