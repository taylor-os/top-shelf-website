How a hail repair goes.

Most hail cars take one to three days, and all of it happens at your home.

01

File the claim

Call your insurer and get a claim number. Snap a few photos of the roof and hood while you're at it.

02

Estimate under the light

We come out, put every panel under the light board and count the dents. You get a line-item estimate.

03

We talk to the adjuster

We send the estimate to your insurer and sort out any supplements directly with the adjuster.

04

Repair at home

We work through the car in your driveway or garage. You pay your deductible, and the insurer pays the rest.

Why PDR for hail

Your factory paint stays on.

  • Nothing is sanded or repainted, so a buyer's paint gauge won't find anything later.
  • Body shops get buried after a storm. We work at your place, on your schedule, and most cars are done in a few days.
  • We do every panel, including the rails and the small pillar dents that tents tend to skip.
Right after a storm

The 5-minute storm checklist.

  • Photograph the roof, hood and trunk in daylight, at an angle
  • Check the glass and lights. Cracked glass gets reported separately
  • Call your insurer and write down the claim number
  • Don't sign anything at a parking-lot tent until you've compared estimates
  • Send us the photos, and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a PDR job

About deductibles: you pay your deductible and the insurer pays the approved repair. If anyone offers to "cover" or waive your deductible, walk away. In many states that's insurance fraud, and you're the one who signs the claim.
