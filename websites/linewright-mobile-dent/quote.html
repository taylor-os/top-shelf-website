Photo tips from the techs

Shoot in shade or on a cloudy day. Direct sun hides dents.
Angle the phone so a straight line, like a garage door edge or a roofline, reflects across the dent. Where that line bends shows us the size.
Add one photo from a few steps back so we can see which panel it is.
