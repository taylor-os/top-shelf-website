What we need from you

A parking spot. That's about it.

  • A few feet around the car so we can set the light board up beside it.
  • Permission to work there. Most offices and HOAs are fine with it, but check if you're not sure.
  • Dry weather for paint jobs. PDR can happen under a carport in the rain. Paint and coatings can't.
  • No power, water or tools. The van brings everything.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Don't see yours? Text it to 123-456-7890 along with a photo of the dent.

Do you have a shop I can bring the car to?

No. We're fully mobile, which is the whole idea. Everything we need is in the van, including our own power, so all we need is your car and a little space around it.

What hours are you open?

We don't keep storefront hours because there's no storefront. We book by appointment and fit around your day: early mornings, lunch breaks, while you're at work. Send photos and we'll find a time.

How do I know if my dent can be fixed without paint?

If the paint isn't cracked, chipped or flaking, there's a good chance PDR will work. Sharp creases and stretched metal can be harder. Send three photos and we'll tell you honestly, before you book.

Do I need to be there?

Not usually. For PDR we often work with the car locked. For paint work, or when we need a door opened, we'll ask you to leave a key with someone or be around at the start.

What about bad weather?

Dent work isn't affected much by weather, and a garage or covered parking spot is ideal. Paint, clear coat and ceramic work need dry conditions and moderate temperatures, so we'll move those if rain or cold moves in.

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. For hail, and for any claim, we write the estimate, send it to your insurer and work directly with the adjuster. You pay your deductible, and nothing else gets waived or hidden.

What does the lifetime guarantee cover?

If a dent we repaired with PDR comes back or shows a flaw for as long as you own the car, we'll come back and fix it at no charge. Paint work carries its own warranty, which we spell out on your quote.
