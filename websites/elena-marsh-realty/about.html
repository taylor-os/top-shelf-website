My story

I grew up here. Now I help other people call it home.

I've lived in Cedar Ridge nearly my whole life. I know which streets flood, which ones get the morning sun, and which quiet blocks are about to get busy. After years of watching friends get pushed around by pushy agents, I decided to be the opposite kind.

That means I answer my phone, I tell you the truth about a house even when it costs me a sale, and I treat your money like it's my own. Most of my clients come from someone I've already helped — which is exactly how I like it.
