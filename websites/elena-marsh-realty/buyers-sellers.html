Good questions

The things people ask me first.

How much does it cost to work with you as a buyer?

In most cases the seller covers the buyer’s agent commission, so my help usually costs you nothing out of pocket. I’ll always explain exactly how it works for your situation up front.

Is now a good time to buy or sell?

Honestly, it depends on you more than the headlines. Let’s talk about your timeline and goals and I’ll give you my real read, even if that read is to wait a few months.

Do I need to fix up my house before selling?

Usually far less than people think. I’ll walk through with you and point out the handful of things that actually move the needle, and skip the ones that don’t.

How fast will you get back to me?

Minutes, not days, including evenings and weekends. In this market a slow reply can cost you a home, and that’s not going to happen on my watch.
