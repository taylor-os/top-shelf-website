The shop

A well-edited shop, top to bottom.

Everything we carry, grouped into six collections. Come by the shop to see it all in person, or call and we'll set something aside.

01 · Ceramics & Tableware

Made by hand, made to use.

Mugs with a good handle, bowls that feel right, and vases worth leaving out empty. From small studios we've met and trust.

  • Mugs
  • Bowls & plates
  • Vases
  • Serveware
  • Planters
02 · Textiles & Throws

The soft, warm layer.

Washed linen, chunky wool, and honest cotton for the sofa, the bed, and the table. The kind of thing that gets better with use.

  • Throws
  • Cushions
  • Table linens
  • Tea towels
03 · Plants & Pots

Green things that forgive you.

Easy-care plants that survive real life, plus the terracotta and stoneware to put them in. We'll tell you exactly how much to water.

  • Easy-care plants
  • Terracotta pots
  • Hanging planters
  • Care kits
04 · Candles & Scent

A shop you can smell from the door.

Small-batch candles and simple home fragrance, in scents that feel like a real place, not a laboratory. Come sniff before you buy.

  • Candles
  • Diffusers
  • Incense
  • Room sprays
05 · Gifts & Cards

The good-gift shortcut.

Tell us the person and the occasion, and we'll build a gift they'll actually love, wrapped for free. There's a card for everything, too.

  • Greeting cards
  • Gift sets
  • Stationery
  • Free wrapping
06 · Kitchen & Pantry

Beautiful, useful, everyday.

Boards, linens, and the small tools that make cooking nicer, plus a shelf of local pantry treats worth taking home.

  • Boards & tools
  • Aprons & linens
  • Ceramics
  • Local pantry
Gift services

We make gifting easy.

Always-free wrapping

Every gift leaves the shop beautifully wrapped, at no charge. Ask for a handwritten card while you're at it.

Gift registry

Weddings, babies, and new homes. Set up a registry in-store or over the phone and we'll handle the rest.

Local delivery

Order before noon and we'll deliver locally the same day. Perfect for the gift you forgot until this morning.

See it all in person.

The photos are nice, but the shop is nicer. Come browse, or call and we'll help you find exactly the right thing.
