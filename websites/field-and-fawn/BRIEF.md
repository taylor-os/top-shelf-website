# Field &amp; Fawn — Sample Site Brief

**Industry:** Retail &amp; Local · **Slug:** `field-and-fawn` · **Domain:** fieldandfawn.com
**Phone:** 123-456-7890 · **Email:** contact@fieldandfawn.com

## Identity
- **Name:** Field &amp; Fawn · **Type:** curated home &amp; lifestyle goods boutique.
- **Tagline:** *Beautifully useful things.*
- **One-liner:** A small, well-edited shop of home goods, gifts, and everyday beautiful things.
- **Locale (fictional):** Old Town, on Market Street.
- **Positioning:** A lovingly curated boutique for the home. Handmade ceramics, soft textiles, plants, candles, and thoughtful gifts, chosen by people who actually use them. Support-local, maker-forward, warm and unpretentious.
- **Since:** 2016 · woman-owned · a real brick-and-mortar shop with a small online edit.

## Voice
Warm, personal, a little playful. Talks like the shop owner who remembers your name. Sensory and specific about materials and makers, never salesy.

## Theme / art direction
- **World:** warm, tactile, editorial-boutique. Cream paper, terracotta, sage. Feels like a beautiful shop you don't want to leave.
- **Palette:** warm oat/cream `#F4EFE6` grounds; **terracotta** `#C0694A` + soft **sage** `#8B9A7C` accents; warm brown ink `#3A322B` text; clay `#E7D8C6` tints.
- **Type:** characterful grotesque (Bricolage Grotesque) for headlines, humanist sans (Inter) for body. Warm, rounded photography, generous white space, hand-picked feel.
- **Motion:** Lenis smooth scroll; soft fade-up reveals; gentle image hover-zoom; a warm marquee of shop values; nothing flashy. Reduced-motion respected.

## Collections / what we carry (content)
- **Ceramics &amp; Tableware** — handmade mugs, bowls, plates, and vases from small makers.
- **Textiles &amp; Throws** — linen, wool, and cotton for the sofa and the table.
- **Plants &amp; Pots** — easy-care greenery and the terracotta to put it in.
- **Candles &amp; Scent** — small-batch candles and simple home fragrance.
- **Gifts &amp; Cards** — the thing you actually want to give, wrapped and ready.
- **Kitchen &amp; Pantry** — beautiful, useful tools and a few local pantry treats.

## Proof (fictional, believable)
- A real shop in Old Town since 2016 · 200+ small makers stocked · gift wrapping always free · local delivery.
- Loved for: a tight, well-chosen edit; genuinely helpful staff; a shop that smells and feels wonderful.
- Extras: gift registry, workshops (candle-making, floral), and a loyalty punch card.

## Pages (5)
1. **index.html — Home:** warm hero (styled shop interior, "Visit the shop") -> values marquee -> collections grid (6) -> a styled "the shop" split -> maker/values pillars -> what people love (testimonials) -> workshops/events teaser -> visit CTA -> footer.
2. **shop.html — Shop:** intro -> the six collections as rich cards with imagery -> a note on our edit and makers -> gift services (wrapping, registry, delivery) -> CTA.
3. **story.html — Our Story:** the shop's story (woman-owned, since 2016) -> owner split -> what we believe (curated / local / kind) -> the space -> CTA.
4. **visit.html — Visit:** hours, location, parking -> what's on (workshops &amp; events) -> gift services -> a warm invitation -> map note -> CTA.
5. **contact.html — Contact:** message form (name, phone, email, reason: gift help / registry / workshop / stock question / hello, message) -> hours &amp; location -> gift &amp; registry note -> follow-us.

**Global:** cream sticky header (sprig mark + "Field &amp; Fawn"; Home·Shop·Our Story·Visit·Contact; phone + "Visit the Shop" button). Footer on warm brown: shop, visit, contact, Top Shelf sample credit.
