How a visit goes

You'll know what's happening.

You get a text when you book, another if we find something extra, and one when the car's ready.

  1. Book online

    Pick your size, package and a time. You see the total and the 25% deposit before you book, and a text confirms it.

  2. Drop it off

    Paloma checks you in. Check-in photos show the car as it came in.

  3. We ask first

    Find dog hair packed into the cargo carpet? We text you the price and wait for your OK before we start on it.

  4. Ready text

    When it's done you get a text. Pay by link or at the counter, and add a tip on the link if you like.

  5. Next time

    Want a reminder when the next detail is due? We'll text you when it's time. That's all it is, a text, so there's nothing to sign up for.
