Deposit and hourly work

How the deposit works

When you book online you pay 25% of the booking as a deposit. It holds your time and comes off your bill, and the rest is due at pickup. A full detail on an SUV is $220, so the deposit is $55.00 and $165.00 is left at pickup.

Pay the rest by text link or at the counter, and add a tip on the link if you like. If it's a no-show, the deposit is kept.

Paint correction, by the hour

Paint correction (1-step) isn't on the booking page. How long it takes depends on the paint, so it's priced at $75 an hour (sample price) and Dee quotes it herself. A four-hour job, for example, comes to $300.

Call 123-456-7890 to set it up.
