Deposit and hourly work

How the deposit works

When you book online you pay 25% of the booking as a deposit. It holds your time and comes off your bill, and the rest is due at pickup. For a full detail on an SUV:

Total$220.00
Deposit$55.00
At pickup$165.00

Pay the rest by text link or at the counter, and add a tip on the link if you like. If it's a no-show, the deposit is kept.
