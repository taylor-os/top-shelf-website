A two-bay shop in Rowlett.

How we run it

Three things we do every time.

None of it is complicated, and it saves everyone a phone call.

Prices on the site

Nobody wants to call for a quote on a detail. Our price list is on this site and on the booking page, by vehicle size, and you see the deposit before you pay anything.

Check-in photos

Every car gets photos when it comes in. If a rear bumper scuff was already there, the photos show it, and nobody has to remember.

We ask first

If the car needs something you didn't book, we text you the price and wait. Nothing extra goes on your bill until you say yes.

A visit, start to finish

From booking to keys.

Here's what a full detail looks like from our side of the counter.

  1. Book online

    Size, package, add-ons and a time. The 25% deposit holds it, and a text confirms it with a link back to your booking.

  2. Drop it off

    Paloma checks you in and the booked package carries straight onto the work order, so nobody retypes it.

  3. In the bay

    Trey or Minh takes it from there. If something extra turns up, you get a text before anyone starts on it.

  4. Ready text

    You get a text with what's left to pay. Pay by link or at the counter, and add a tip on the link if you like.

  5. Next time

    Ask for a rebook reminder and we'll text when the next detail is due. You aren't signing up for a plan, and nothing gets charged.
