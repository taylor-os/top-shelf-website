Our story

It started with a fire and a stubborn idea.

That good cooking doesn't need much, just great ingredients, live fire, and the patience to let both do their thing.

The fire

We took out the gas line on purpose.

When Chef Elena Marsh opened Ember & Oak, she made one rule that shaped everything else: no gas. Every plate is cooked over live oak and hardwood embers. It's harder, slower, and impossible to fake, which is exactly the point.

Fire is honest. It rewards a good cut of beef and a vegetable picked at the right time, and it exposes anything you try to hide. So we buy well, keep the menu short, and let the coals do the talking.

How we cook

Three rules, every night.

Live fire only

Oak and hardwood embers, never gas. If it can't be cooked over fire, it doesn't make the menu.

In season, close to home

We buy from growers and ranchers we can drive to, and cook what's actually ripe that week.

A short menu, done well

Fewer dishes, each one worth ordering. We'd rather do ten things right than thirty things fine.

"I want people to walk in, smell the fire, and feel like they've arrived somewhere. The food is the point, but the evening is the reason."

Chef Elena Marsh · Founder
