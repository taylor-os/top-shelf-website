Our kitchen

No gas, no shortcuts. Everything that leaves our kitchen has spent time over live oak, from the steaks to the vegetables to the flame-kissed bread. Fire does the work that fussiness pretends to.

The bar

Cocktails worth lingering over.

Our bar runs on the same idea as the kitchen: a few good things, made properly. Smoked spirits, house syrups, and big clear ice. Come early, sit at the bar, and let the bartender pour you something.

The room

Warm, low, and easy to lose an evening in.

Exposed brick, reclaimed timber, candlelight, and the glow of the fire from the open kitchen. It's the kind of room that's right for a first date, a long dinner with friends, or a seat at the bar on the way home.
