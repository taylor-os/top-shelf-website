Sourcing

Close to home, in season.

We buy from growers and ranchers we can drive to, cook what's ripe, and let the fire do the rest. If a dish isn't right yet, it isn't on the menu.

"The fire is honest. It rewards good ingredients and punishes shortcuts. That's the whole philosophy."

Chef Elena Marsh
