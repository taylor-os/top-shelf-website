# Ember & Oak — Sample Site Brief

**Industry:** Restaurants & Bars · **Slug:** `ember-and-oak` · **Domain:** emberandoak.com
**Phone:** 123-456-7890 · **Email:** contact@emberandoak.com

## Identity
- **Name:** Ember & Oak · **Type:** wood-fired kitchen + craft cocktail bar (upscale-casual).
- **Tagline:** *Cooked over fire.*
- **One-liner:** A neighborhood chophouse where everything touches live oak and open flame.
- **Locale (fictional):** the Warehouse District, Midtown.
- **Positioning:** Chef-driven, seasonal, wood-fire cooking (no gas — oak and embers only). Warm, moody, unpretentious. A place for a good steak, a great cocktail, and a long table of friends.

## Voice
Warm, confident, a little poetic but never fussy. Talks about fire, smoke, season, and craft. Sensory and inviting.

## Theme / art direction
- **World:** cinematic, moody, candlelit. Smoke and ember. Feels expensive but unpretentious.
- **Palette:** near-black charcoal `#141110` / `#1C1815` grounds; **ember** `#E8722A` + warm **gold** `#E6B24A` accents; cream `#F4EEE3` text; smoke `#B8AEA1` muted.
- **Type:** expressive serif display (Fraunces) for headlines, clean sans (Inter) for body. Large, confident, editorial. Generous negative space, warm grain, thin gold rules.
- **Motion:** Lenis smooth scroll; slow hero image parallax (Ken-Burns feel); serif headline mask-reveals; menu items fade up in sequence; warm ambient ember glow. Restrained and cinematic, not flashy. Reduced-motion respected.

## Pages (5)
1. **index.html — Home:** cinematic hero (full-bleed grill/interior, serif headline, "Reserve a table") → intro/ethos strip → signature dishes (3 featured) → the bar teaser → ambiance split → private events teaser → reservation CTA → footer.
2. **menu.html — Menu:** dinner menu by section (Wood-Fired Starters, From the Grill, From the Bar, Dessert) with dish names, short descriptions, prices → a note on sourcing/seasonality → CTA.
3. **story.html — Our Story:** the fire philosophy, the chef, local sourcing → split with chef image → values → CTA.
4. **events.html — Private Events:** private dining / chef's table / buyouts, capacities, what's included → gallery → inquiry CTA.
5. **reserve.html — Reserve:** reservation request form (name, phone, email, date, time, party size, occasion) → hours → location/parking → private-event link.

**Global:** dark sticky header (flame mark + "Ember & Oak" serif wordmark; Home·Menu·Story·Events·Reserve; phone + "Reserve" button). Footer: hours, location, contact, social, Top Shelf sample credit.
