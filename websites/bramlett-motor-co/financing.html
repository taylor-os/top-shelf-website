How buying here works

We write our own car loans here at the lot. This is how it goes from start to finish, so nothing surprises you.

  1. Pick the car first

    Find the car you want and take it for a drive. The price is on the car.

  2. Sit down with Yolanda

    Yolanda Pierce is our finance manager. She'll ask where you work, how you get paid and what you can put down. That's how we figure out what works for you.

  3. See every number on paper

    Before you sign, you'll see the price, tax, title and registration, your down payment, the amount financed, the rate, and what each payment is and when it's due. Ask about anything you want.

  4. Pick a schedule that fits your paydays

    Weekly, every two weeks or monthly. We set the due date around when you get paid.

  5. Drive home and pay us directly

    Pay at the counter with cash, a card or a check, or pay by card from a link we text you. Either way, your receipt comes by text.

What to bring

Bring these and we can usually get everything done in one visit.

Driver's license

A current license, for the test drive and the paperwork.

A recent pay stub

The most recent one you have. If you're paid in cash or self-employed, bring what shows your income and we'll talk about it.

Your down payment

Cash, a card or a cashier's check. We'll agree on the amount when we go over the numbers.

Trading in a car? Bring its title, or your payoff information if you still owe on it.

Other ways to buy

You don't have to finance with us. Some people pay cash, and some already have a loan lined up somewhere else. Both are fine.

  • Cash. Pay the price plus tax, title and registration, and the car is yours that day.
  • Your bank or credit union. Bring your approval and we'll handle the paperwork with your lender.
  • A loan from us. Written here at the lot, and you pay us directly.

Straight answers

Do you check credit?

We look at your whole situation: where you work, how long you've been there, what you make and what you can put down. Credit is part of it, but it isn't the only thing. Bring a recent pay stub and we'll go through it with you.

Can you promise I'll be approved?

No. Be careful with anyone who promises that before they know anything about you. What we can promise is a straight answer and every number in writing.

Can I use my own bank or credit union?

Yes. Bring your approval with you and we'll take care of the paperwork with them.

Can I trade in my car?

Yes. We'll look it over and give you a number. If you still owe on it, bring your payoff information and we'll show you how it fits into the deal.

What if a payment is going to be late?

Call or text Yolanda before the due date. We'd much rather sort it out ahead of time. Any late fee is written into your contract, so you'll know about it before you sign.

Can I pay my loan online?

Yes. We text you a pay link, you pay by card on your phone, and the receipt comes back by text. You can still pay at the counter if you'd rather.
