How buying here works

Four steps, and nobody rushes you through them.

  1. 1

    Pick a car

    Look online or walk the lot. Every car has the price on it.

  2. 2

    Drive it

    Take it out on roads you know. Bring your mechanic if you want.

  3. 3

    Go over the numbers

    Price, tax, fees, down payment and payment schedule, on paper, before you sign.

  4. 4

    Drive it home

    We handle the title work. If you financed with us, you pay us directly.

Read how financing works

How do you want to pay?

There are three ways to buy a car here. Pick one and we'll tell you how it goes.

Paying cash

Pay the price plus tax, title and registration, and the car is yours that day. You can pay with cash, a card or a cashier's check.

A loan from us

We write the loan ourselves, here at the lot. We look at your job, your pay and what you can put down, then work out a payment schedule that fits your paydays.

  • Weekly, every two weeks or monthly
  • Pay at the counter or by card from a link we text you
  • Your receipt comes by text

Your own bank or credit union

Already approved somewhere? Bring the details. We'll work with your lender on the paperwork so you can drive the car home.

Called after we closed? We'll text you back.

If you call after hours and we miss you, you'll get a text from our lot line. Tell us which car you're looking at and we'll set up a time for you to come see it.
