One lot in Garland, run by the people you'll meet there

Bramlett Motor Co. is a family-run used-car lot. Curtis Bramlett owns it, and he's usually on the lot.

Why Curtis runs it this way

Curtis wanted a lot where people could buy a decent car without feeling rushed or talked down to. That meant putting the price on every car, writing loans here so more people could buy, and keeping the same small team so you know who you're dealing with.

If you finance with us, you'll keep seeing us. You pay Yolanda, you call Yolanda, and if Yolanda's out, Curtis picks up.

A few things we always do

The price is on every car

On the lot and on this website. You shouldn't have to ask what a car costs, and the price doesn't change because of who's asking.

Numbers on paper

Price, fees, down payment and payments, written out before you sign.

Same people after

The people who sold you the car are the ones you call later.

We tell you what we know

If we know about a problem with a car, we'll tell you. Your mechanic is welcome to look it over.

We text

Appointments, pay links and receipts come by text, so you don't have to call.
