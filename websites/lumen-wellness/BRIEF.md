# Lumen Wellness — Sample Site Brief

**Industry:** Salon, Spa &amp; Fitness · **Slug:** `lumen-wellness` · **Domain:** lumenwellness.com
**Phone:** 123-456-7890 · **Email:** contact@lumenwellness.com

## Identity
- **Name:** Lumen Wellness · **Type:** combined hair &amp; beauty salon, treatment spa, and boutique fitness studio.
- **Tagline:** *Come back to yourself.*
- **One-liner:** A salon, a spa, and a studio under one calm roof.
- **Locale (fictional):** the Linden Quarter.
- **Positioning:** One membership, one calm space for the whole of you. Hair and beauty, restorative spa treatments, and a boutique movement studio, run by a small team who actually remember your name. Elevated and unhurried, without the intimidation of a big-box gym or the coldness of a clinical spa.
- **Established:** 2018.

## Voice
Warm, calm, quietly confident. Sensory but never flowery or gushing. It invites, it doesn't push. Speaks to a guest who wants to feel cared for and leave lighter.

## Theme / art direction
- **World:** serene, warm, elevated. A place that feels like a long exhale. Sand and oat grounds, dusty blush, a muted plum anchor, and fine soft-gold lines.
- **Palette:** sand `#F4EDE3` / cream `#FBF7F1` / oat `#EBE1D3` grounds; **plum** `#5B3B5E` anchor; **blush/rose** `#C77B72` accent; **soft gold** `#C1974E` hairlines and detail; ink `#33262F`, body `#5A4C54`.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (airy high-contrast serif display) + Jost (clean geometric sans body). Generous whitespace, soft large radii, thin gold rules, gentle fades.
- **Motion:** Lenis smooth scroll; soft slow reveals; hero image parallax; a rotating hero word (hair / skin / body / breath); count-up stats; gentle image zoom on hover. Calm, never busy. Reduced-motion respected.

## The three pillars (content)
- **Salon** — cut, color, styling, and bridal, by stylists who listen first.
- **Spa** — facials, massage, and body treatments that actually let you switch off.
- **Studio** — reformer pilates, sculpt, and slow-flow classes in small groups.

## Treatments (menu, fictional but believable)
- Salon: precision cut, balayage &amp; color, blowout &amp; styling, bridal &amp; events.
- Spa: signature facial, deep-tissue &amp; relaxation massage, body treatment &amp; wrap, waxing &amp; brows.
- Studio: reformer pilates, sculpt, slow flow, breathwork.

## Membership (recurring)
- **Glow** — monthly, for the salon-and-facial regular.
- **Restore** — monthly, spa plus studio, the balanced favorite.
- **Devotee** — unlimited studio plus monthly treatment credits and member pricing.
- Perks: member pricing, priority booking, guest passes, roll-over credits.

## Proof (fictional, believable)
- Since 2018 · a team of 20+ stylists, therapists and instructors · 4.9 from 1,200+ reviews · thousands of members · one calm space in the Linden Quarter.
