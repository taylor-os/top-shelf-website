Good to know

The questions we get most.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month to month. Pause or cancel any time with a little notice, no penalty and no hard feelings.

Do my credits really roll over?

They do. If you don't use a credit this month, it carries into next month so you never lose what you paid for.

Can I share my membership?

Your credits are yours, but every plan includes guest passes so you can bring a friend or partner along.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Move up or down as your routine changes; the new rate simply starts on your next cycle.
