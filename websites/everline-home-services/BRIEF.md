- **Motion:** Lenis smooth scroll; GSAP reveals (mask/curtain), a dynamic hero (parallax + kinetic headline), animated stat counters, hover lifts on cards, section color shift navy→ivory→navy. `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected.
