The firm

Boutique by design.

Halcourt & Vale was founded in 2004 by two attorneys who had grown tired of the big-firm model, where the person you meet is rarely the person who does your work. We kept the firm small on purpose, so senior judgment stays on every matter.

That means fewer clients, handled better. When you call, you reach a lawyer who knows your file, not a queue.
