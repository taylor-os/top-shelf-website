Practice areas

Where we do our best work.

Three areas we know deeply, handled by senior attorneys who have guided many clients through the same questions you're facing now.

01 · Family Law

When your family is changing.

Divorce and custody touch everything at once: your kids, your home, your finances. You get a straight read on your options, a plan built around what matters most to you, and an honest look at whether settling serves you better than a fight.

  • Divorce
  • Child custody & visitation
  • Custody modifications
  • Child support
  • Prenuptial agreements
  • Mediation
02 · Criminal Defense

When you need someone in your corner.

A criminal charge moves fast, and the first days matter. We explain where you stand in plain English, handle every court setting, and tell you honestly what to expect next.

  • Misdemeanor charges
  • Felony charges
  • Court settings & hearings
  • Plea negotiations
  • Trial defense
  • Calls about a family member's case
03 · Estate Planning

Protecting what you've built.

A good plan spares your family hard decisions at the worst possible time. We build clear, durable plans that pass on what you own the way you intend, and we guide families through probate when the time comes.

  • Wills & trusts
  • Powers of attorney
  • Healthcare directives
  • Asset protection
  • Probate administration
  • Trust & estate disputes
