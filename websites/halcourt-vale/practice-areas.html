Practice areas

Where we do our best work.

Six areas we know deeply, handled by senior attorneys who have guided many clients through the same questions you're facing now.

01 · Business & Corporate

The counsel a company runs on.

From the day you form the entity to the day you sell it, we're the outside general counsel a growing business leans on, close enough to know your operation, senior enough to handle what comes up.

  • Entity formation & governance
  • Financing & capital raises
  • Mergers & acquisitions
  • Ownership & partnership agreements
  • Outside general counsel
  • Succession & exit planning
02 · Civil Litigation

When a dispute has to be resolved.

We litigate to win, but we advise to protect your business. That means a straight assessment of your odds, a strategy built for your goal, and a hard look at whether settlement serves you better than a fight.

  • Breach of contract
  • Business torts & fraud
  • Partnership & shareholder disputes
  • Injunctions & emergency relief
  • Arbitration & mediation
  • Appeals
03 · Real Estate

Property, handled with care.

Commercial deals move fast and the details decide who's protected. We handle acquisitions, leasing, and development with the diligence a serious property requires.

  • Purchase & sale agreements
  • Commercial leasing
  • Development & land use
  • Title review & clearing
  • Financing & closings
  • Property disputes
04 · Estate Planning

Protecting what you've built.

A good plan spares your family hard decisions at the worst possible time. We build clear, durable plans that pass on your assets and your business the way you intend.

  • Wills & trusts
  • Powers of attorney
  • Business succession
  • Asset protection
  • Probate administration
  • Trust & estate disputes
05 · Employment Law

Counsel for employers.

Employment issues are cheaper to prevent than to litigate. We help employers put the right agreements and policies in place, and stand with them when a dispute does arise.

  • Handbooks & policies
  • Employment agreements
  • Non-competes & trade secrets
  • Wage & hour compliance
  • Terminations & separations
  • Workplace disputes
06 · Contracts & Transactions

Agreements that hold up.

Your business runs on its contracts. We draft and negotiate them to be clear, fair, and enforceable, so the agreement works when it matters most, not just when everyone gets along.

  • Drafting & review
  • Vendor & customer agreements
  • NDAs & licensing
  • Partnership & JV agreements
  • Negotiation support
  • Contract disputes
