How you pay

  • Your plan charges the card on file once a month.
  • The member app shows the date you're paid through.
  • If a charge is declined, we text you and try the card again a few days later. You can update the card in the app under You, then Membership.
  • After three declines we stop trying the card, and you can pay at the front desk next time you're in.

Before you join

Can I try a class before I join?

Yes. Ask for Intro / Foundations on the Visit page. It's a small class with Coach Malik for people who are new to the gym.

What's the difference between the two plans?

Monthly gets you into the open gym. Unlimited classes lets you book any class on the schedule.

How do I cancel Unlimited classes?

Let the front desk know 30 days before you want it to end.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is free and right out front.

Not sure which plan yet?

Come to an intro class first. You can decide after.
