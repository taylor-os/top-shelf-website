Your first class

How starting works

  1. Ask for an intro

    Fill in the short form on the Visit page or call 123-456-7890. Pick Tuesday or Thursday at 7:30 PM, or Saturday at 9:00 AM.

  2. Get our text

    We text you the time and your coach. If you need to change it, reply to that text.

  3. Show up ready

    Wear shoes you can move in and bring water. Parking is free and right out front.

  4. Pick a plan

    After the intro, choose Unlimited classes or the Monthly open gym plan. The front desk can set it up for you.

The member app

Book classes from your phone

Members book in the Larkspur app. It shows how many spots are left in each class and holds the check-in code you show at the front desk.

  • We text you when a booking is confirmed, then again two hours before class.
  • If a class is full, join the waitlist. When someone cancels, the next person moves up.
  • Your membership and the date you're paid through are in the app too.
