## Voice

## Theme / art direction
- **World:** warm, caring, modern-neighborhood. Soft cream grounds, a grounded forest/emerald green, and a friendly coral accent, with a sage tint. Rounded, approachable, calm.
- **Palette:** cream `#FBF8F3` / paper `#F3EEE4` / sage mist `#EAF1EA` grounds; **forest green** `#1E6B54` primary; **coral** `#EE7256` warm accent; ink `#24312B`, body `#4C5A52`.
- **Type:** Quicksand (rounded, friendly display) + Nunito Sans (warm, readable body). Soft large radii, gentle shadows, rounded everything. Distinct from the clinical teal dental sample.
- **Motion:** Lenis smooth scroll; soft reveals; hero image parallax; a rotating hero word (dogs / cats / puppies / seniors); count-up stats; gentle hover lifts. Calm and friendly. Reduced-motion respected.

- **Sick &amp; Urgent Visits** — same-day appointments when something's wrong, with clear next steps.
- **Dentistry** — cleanings, x-rays, and extractions for the health problem owners miss most.
- **Surgery** — spay/neuter and soft-tissue surgery in a modern, monitored suite.
- **Diagnostics &amp; Imaging** — in-house labs, digital x-ray and ultrasound for answers the same day.
- **Senior Care** — gentle, tailored care for older pets who need a little more attention.
