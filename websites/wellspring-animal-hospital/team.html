Dr. Marcus Reed

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on.

Compassion first

Every pet is someone's family. We treat them the way we'd want our own cared for, gently and without judgment.

The pet's comfort

Fear-free isn't a buzzword here. We go at your pet's pace and do everything we can to make visits calm.

Honesty, always

Clear estimates, real options, and straight answers. We'd rather earn your trust than pad a bill.

Come meet us in person.
