Automation for Electricians

Automation for electricians keeps every quote, appointment, and job on track without you remembering, following up on panel-upgrade estimates, sending reminders before each visit, and keeping dispatch moving, so nothing slips during a week when your techs are booked days out.

A booked week runs on reminders nobody sees you send

When your techs are booked days out, the work is not the hard part, the coordination is. A customer needs a reminder the day before so they are home, a quote from last week needs a nudge before it goes cold, a tech needs the address and scope before they roll, and every one of those is a small task that slips during a busy stretch. A customer who is not reminded and misses the window, or a quote nobody followed up on, is money lost to a full schedule, not an empty one.

Every quote, followed up automatically

The jobs electricians quote and wait on, panel upgrades, rewires, generator and EV charger installs, are exactly the ones a steady follow-up wins. When you send a quote, the automation starts a sequence: a check-in the next day, a helpful note a few days later, a last nudge before it goes cold. You still make every real call. The routine follow-up is simply handled, on time, on every quote, whether you sent one this week or a dozen.

Reminders that cut the no-shows and the callbacks

Before each scheduled visit, the customer gets an automatic confirmation and a reminder, so they are home when your tech arrives and you are not eating a wasted trip across town. After the work is done, a follow-up can go out to check everything is working and to ask for a review while the job is fresh. None of it depends on you remembering during a week when you are already stretched thin.

Dispatch and follow-through, kept on track

As a job moves, the right people get the right update: the tech gets the address and scope, the customer gets an on-the-way or running-late note, and a job that needs a permit or an inspection gets a reminder so it does not stall. A lead that went quiet can resurface on a schedule instead of falling out of your memory entirely. It all runs against the same database as your CRM, so nothing here duplicates a customer or a note.

How it plays out

A booked week where nothing slips through

Your schedule is full three days out. Without you touching your phone, each customer gets a reminder the day before their visit, the panel-upgrade quote you sent Monday gets a friendly check-in Wednesday, and the homeowner waiting on an inspection gets a note the day it is scheduled. You never sat down to send any of it, and nobody fell through the cracks just because the week was busy.

Questions Electricians Ask

Common Questions

01

What does the automation actually handle?

The routine follow-through around your jobs: quote follow-up sequences, appointment confirmations and reminders, after-job check-ins and review requests, and nudges on jobs waiting for a permit or inspection. You still make every real decision and call.

02

Does this replace me talking to customers?

No. It handles the routine reminders and follow-ups so you are not the one remembering to send them during a busy week. You step in personally for anything that actually needs a conversation.

03

Will reminders really cut down no-shows?

They help a lot. An automatic confirmation and a reminder before each visit are the single biggest lever on missed appointments, so your techs are not driving to homes where nobody is there.

04

What happens to quotes and leads that go quiet?

They get a scheduled follow-up instead of being forgotten. You set how long a quote or lead should sit before it gets another nudge, so old opportunities keep getting a reason to come back instead of sitting in a list.

05

How long does setup take?

Setup is included with no separate fee. We build the quote, reminder, and follow-up sequences around how you already work, so it is running within days. Start with a free audit and we will show you what is currently falling through during your busy weeks.
